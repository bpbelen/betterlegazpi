Jeepney routes

These are the public utility jeepney (PUJ) routes serving Legazpi City under the city's updated routing plan. Most of them begin in Daraga or in another Albay town rather than inside Legazpi itself — that is how the routes are written in the plan, and how people actually travel into the city. Routes within Legazpi proper are not yet published here.

Select a route below to zoom the map to it. The written street sequence on each route card is the authoritative version.
