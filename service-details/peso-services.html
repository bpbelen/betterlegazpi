Public Employment Service Office (PESO)

Citizen's Charter, 2026 First Edition. Read the source document

What do you need to do?

  • You are looking for a job 2 services
    1. Looking for work
    2. Other DOLE grant-based programmes (TUPAD, GIP, DILEEP)
  • You are a student or out of school 2 services
    1. Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)
    2. JobStart Philippines Program
  • You came home from working abroad 1 service
    1. OFW help desk
  • You are hiring 1 service
    1. Hiring through PESO as an employer

Finding work 2 services

1 Looking for work 6 minutes at the counter 24 minutes in total 2 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Register as a jobseeker, see what vacancies the office holds, and get referred to an employer. The office keeps a catalogue of vacancies from partner employers and can also point you at technical and vocational training.

What to bring 2 items

How it works

  1. 1Submit your resume, ask to see the available job vacancies, and request a job referral.

    The office then

    1. Registers you and issues the National Skills Registration Program form (NSRP Form 1).

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    2. Accepts the resume and collects the accomplished NSRP Form 1.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    3. Assesses and evaluates the documents for completeness of the data required in the form.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    4. If you are only asking for the list of available vacancies, presents the PESO job vacancies catalogue.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    5. For job matching or referral, interviews the qualified applicant and suggests vacancies from partner employers or technical and vocational institutions.

      5 minutesSupervising Labor and Employment Officer / Labor and Employment Officer III

  2. 2Receive the referral slip.

    The office then

    1. Issues the referral slip.

      3 minutesSupervising Labor and Employment Officer / Labor and Employment Officer III

Total time 24 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • Every PESO service is free.

Programmes for students and youth 2 services

4 Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES) 3 minutes at the counter 21 minutes in total 5 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The DOLE programme that puts students and out-of-school youth into paid work. PESO checks your papers and forwards qualified applicants to DOLE for final approval.

Before you go

Your time
3 minutes at the counter, 21 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
One visit

Public Employment Service Office

Office or division
Public Employment Service Office
Who may avail

Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C)
Classification
Simple Transaction

What to bring 5 items

How it works

  1. 1Apply for the SPES programme.

    The office then

    1. Registers you.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant

    2. Assesses and evaluates the documents for completeness of requirements.

      10 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Labor and Employment Officer III or I

    3. If you qualify and your documents are complete, processes the application and includes you in the list of qualified beneficiaries submitted to DOLE for final approval.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III or I / Admin Aide I or IV

    4. If you do not qualify or your documents are incomplete, notifies you so you can act on it.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III or I / Admin Aide IV

Total time 21 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • PESO's part is the screening. The final approval is DOLE's, and the charter puts no time on it.

Livelihood and grant programmes 1 service

6 Other DOLE grant-based programmes (TUPAD, GIP, DILEEP) 3 minutes at the counter 28 minutes in total 2 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Grant-based DOLE programmes such as TUPAD emergency employment, the Government Internship Program and DILEEP livelihood assistance. Slots are limited: if you qualify you are given a form and a list of requirements to bring back the next day, or the slot passes to the next qualified applicant.

Before you go

Your time
3 minutes at the counter, 28 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
One visit
Where to go
Public Employment Service Office
What you pay
Nothing
Office or division
Public Employment Service Office
Who may avail
Qualified beneficiaries, depending on the programme (TUPAD, GIP, DILEEP and others)
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C)
Classification
Simple Transaction

What to bring 2 items

How it works

  1. 1Apply for a DOLE grant-based programme.

    The office then

    1. Registers you.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    2. Conducts an initial interview and assessment of your qualifications.

      15 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III / Admin Aide I or IV

    3. If you qualify, gives you the form and the list of requirements to submit the following day. If they are not submitted, the slot goes to the next qualified applicant.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III / Admin Aide I or IV

    4. If you do not qualify, notifies you so you can act on it.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III / Admin Aide I or IV

Total time 28 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • Bring your requirements back the next day. The charter is explicit that an unclaimed slot passes to the next qualified applicant.

For employers 1 service

2 Hiring through PESO as an employer 9 minutes at the counter 43 minutes in total 3 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Accreditation for a local or overseas employer or agency wanting to use PESO Legazpi, and the follow-on services once accredited: browsing resumes, posting jobs, and scheduling a local or overseas recruitment activity.

Before you go

Your time
9 minutes at the counter, 43 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
3 separate visits
Where to go
Public Employment Service Office
What you pay
Nothing
Office or division
Public Employment Service Office
Who may avail
Employers
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C)
Classification
Simple Transaction

What to bring 3 items

How it works

  1. 1Request accreditation for a new agency or employer to access PESO Legazpi services.

    The office then

    1. Registers you and issues the National Skills Registration Program form (NSRP Form 2).

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    2. Collects the accomplished NSRP Form 2.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    3. Issues the list of requirements.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

  2. 2Submit the requirements for accreditation.

    The office then

    1. Registers the submission.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    2. Assesses and evaluates the documents for completeness of requirements.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

    3. Assists you based on your request.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Assistant / Admin Aide I or IV

  3. 3Request resume browsing, job posting, or the scheduling of a local or overseas recruitment activity.

    The office then

    1. Registers the request.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III / Admin Aide I or IV

    2. Collects the letter of intent and evaluates the request.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III / Admin Aide I or IV

    3. Verifies whether you have already submitted your complete requirements.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III / Admin Aide I or IV

    4. If you have no requirements yet, or the documents you submitted have expired, gives you the list of documents needed.

      3 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III / Admin Aide I or IV

    5. Assists you based on your request.

      5 minutesLabor and Employment Officer III / Admin Aide I or IV

Total time 43 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • The charter runs accreditation and the services that follow it as one block, so the total covers a first-time employer going all the way through.
