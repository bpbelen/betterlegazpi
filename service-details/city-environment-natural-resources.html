Use of Sanitary Landfill Facility (SLF)

Disposal access and clearance for residual and institutional solid waste at the Banquerohan Landfill

Standard Disposal Requirements

  • Duly Accomplished Landfill Entry Request Form (2 copies) Available at the OCENR Information Counter
  • Waste Characterization / Manifest Sheet (1 original copy) Certifying that waste contains strictly residual/non-hazardous materials
  • Barangay Endorsement / Waste Certification (if applicable)
  • Valid ID of Authorized Hauler / Representative

Commercial & Bulk Hauling Requirements

  • Valid Mayor's / Business Permit & Environmental Clearance
  • Truck Vehicle Registration (LTO OR/CR) & Enclosed Hauler Inspection
  • Certificate of Non-Hazardous Waste Generation (DENR-EMB compliance)
  • Trip Ticket / Waste Weighbridge Slip (issued at SLF gate)
Transaction / Waste Volume Tipping Fee Processing Time
Residual Waste Disposal (1 to 3 Metric Tons per Trip) ₱675.00 / trip 30 minutes
Residual Waste Disposal (Over 3 Metric Tons per Trip) ₱875.00 / trip 30 minutes
Special / Commercial Residual Waste (Excess weight) ₱0.50 / kg 30 minutes
Government & Non-Profit Environmental Cleanup Waste FREE 30 minutes
3-Step Sanitary Landfill Access Workflow
1 SUBMIT & WEIGH

Present disposal manifest at SLF weighbridge; staff checks segregation compliance and records gross weight.

SLF Weighbridge Staff
2 PAY TIPPING FEE

Pay applicable tipping fee at Treasury Cashier counter or Landfill collection desk.

Cashier / Landfill Collector
3 UNLOAD & CLEAR

Unload residual waste at designated active cell under supervision and receive stamped clearance.

Landfill Operations Supervisor

Sanitary Landfill Facility — Banquerohan, Legazpi City

Operating daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM for accredited haulers and local government collection units

Inquire via Email

Ecological Solid Waste Management (ESWM) Seminar

Mandatory environmental orientation for business permit applicants, barangay officials, and schools

Business Permit Applicants

  • Duly Accomplished ESWM Registration Slip (1 copy)
  • Copy of Business Permit Application / Renewal Form Showing assessment or payment from BPLO / City Treasury
  • Official Receipt of Business Tax / Regulatory Fees
  • 1 Valid Government-issued ID of Attendee / Proprietor

Barangays, Schools & Organizations

  • Official Letter-Request addressed to OCENR Head (1 original copy)
  • List of Participants & Proposed Training Schedule
  • Designation of Barangay / Institutional Eco-Coordinator
  • Confirmation of Venue (if on-site community seminar)
Training Service / Certificate Fee Processing / Duration
ESWM Modular Seminar for Business Permit Applicants FREE 2 hours
Certificate of ESWM Seminar Attendance (Original) FREE Issued immediately after session
Barangay & School Ecological Waste Workshop (On-site Outreach) FREE Half-day / 1 day
Replacement / Re-issuance of Lost ESWM Certificate ₱100.00 1 hour
3-Step ESWM Seminar & Certification Process
1 REGISTER & ATTEND

Register at the training desk; attend the 2-hour lecture on RA 9003, segregation, and city eco-ordinances.

ESWM Training Officer
2 EVALUATE

Accomplish short post-training comprehension check and course evaluation questionnaire.

Training Coordinator
3 CLAIM CERTIFICATE

Claim official Certificate of ESWM Attendance required for BPLO environmental clearance.

EMS II Releasing Officer

Tree Cutting, Trimming & Pruning Clearance

Assessment, ocular inspection, and clearance for cutting or pruning trees on private and public land

Standard Application Requirements

  • Duly Accomplished Tree Cutting / Pruning Application Form
  • Barangay Certification / No Objection Resolution (1 original copy) Issued by the Barangay Captain where tree is situated
  • Proof of Land Ownership: Land Title / Tax Declaration / Deed of Sale
  • Clear Photos of the Tree(s) & Vicinity Sketch Map (4R printouts)

Special & Hazardous Situations

  • Hazardous / Calamity-Damaged Trees: Rapid CDRRMO / Barangay hazard verification
  • Infrastructure / Road Right-of-Way: DPWH / City Engineering concurrence
  • Powerline Obstruction: APEC / Electric Cooperative endorsement
  • Replacement Seedling Commitment: Turn in 50 native seedlings per tree cut
Service / Tree Classification Clearance Fee Processing Time
Inspection & Clearance: Common / Exotic Tree (per tree) ₱50.00 / tree 3–5 working days
Inspection & Clearance: Premium / Hardwood Species (per tree) ₱150.00 / tree 3–5 working days
Hazardous / Emergency Tree Pruning (Public Safety) FREE 1–2 working days
Replacement Seedling Turnover (per permitted cut) Required (In-kind) Upon clearance issuance
3-Step Tree Cutting Inspection & Permitting Process
1 FILE & SCHEDULE

Submit application, photos, and barangay endorsement; forestry officer reviews documents and schedules inspection.

Forestry Aide / Inspector
2 OCULAR INSPECTION

OCENR foresters inspect tree health, hazard level, and utility risks; prepare Tree Assessment Report.

City Forester
3 PAY & CLAIM

Pay clearance fee at Cashier, turn in committed replacement seedlings, and claim approved Permit.

OCENR Releasing Officer

DENR-CENRO Coordination Required for Premium Species

Cutting of naturally-grown Narra, Molave, Tindalo, and other premium species requires concurrent DENR permit

Chainsaw Registration & Permitting

Registration, renewal, and ownership transfer of chainsaw units under Republic Act No. 9175

New Chainsaw Registration

  • Duly Accomplished Chainsaw Registration Application (2 copies)
  • Official Receipt & Sales Invoice / Deed of Sale (proof of legal purchase)
  • Technical Specifications & Serial Number verification
  • Valid Government ID & 2x2 Photo of Registered Owner (2 copies)

Renewal & Transfer of Ownership

  • Original Certificate of Chainsaw Registration (Previous)
  • Notarized Deed of Sale / Transfer of Ownership (if transferred)
  • Physical Presentation of Chainsaw Unit for Serial Number Stamping
  • Barangay Certification of Legitimate Utilization (woodcraft/farming)
Transaction / Permit Type Fee Processing Time
New Chainsaw Registration & Metal Validation Tag (Valid for 2 Years) ₱150.00 1–3 working days
Renewal of Chainsaw Registration Certificate ₱150.00 1–2 working days
Transfer of Chainsaw Ownership / Re-tagging ₱150.00 1–2 working days
Certified True Copy of Chainsaw Registry Certificate ₱100.00 1 hour
3-Step Chainsaw Registration Workflow
1 SUBMIT & INSPECT

Submit application forms, sales invoice, and present actual chainsaw unit for physical serial number verification.

Chainsaw Registry Officer
2 PAY FEE

Pay ₱150 registration fee at City Treasury Cashier and secure Official Receipt (OR).

CTO Cashier
3 CLAIM CERTIFICATE

Receive official Certificate of Chainsaw Registration and stamped metal validation security tag.

Forest Protection Officer

Distribution of Free Tree Seedlings & Planting Stock

Free native, fruit-bearing, and forest tree seedlings for reforestation, school greening, and community planting

Individual & Household Requesters

  • Letter of Request addressed to City ENR Officer (1 copy) Indicating planting location, species preference, and purpose
  • Valid Government-issued ID of Applicant (photocopy)
  • Barangay Residency Certification (if requested in bulk)
  • Commitment to Nurture and Maintain Seedlings

Barangay, School & Institutional Greening

  • Official Letter-Request on Institutional Letterhead
  • Tree Planting Activity Plan (target site, date, & number of volunteers)
  • Endorsement from Barangay Captain or School Head
  • Post-Activity Geotagged Photo Documentation Report
Seedling Category / Support Service Cost Processing Time
Native Forest Seedlings (Narra, Pili, Mahogany, Molave, etc.) FREE Same day (subject to stock)
Fruit-Bearing & Agroforestry Seedlings FREE Same day (subject to stock)
Mangrove Propagules / Coastal Species (for coastal barangays) FREE 1–2 working days
Technical Assistance & Tree Planting Supervision FREE By appointment

Nursery Caretaker / Aide
2 ASSESS & ALLOCATE

City Nursery Supervisor

Claim potted seedlings at the City Nursery in Banquerohan; sign property acknowledgement receipt.

Septage Desludging & Environmental Complaints (PACD)

Residential septic tank desludging under City Septage Code, and Public Assistance & Complaints Desk

Septic Tank Desludging Request

  • Duly Accomplished Desludging Service Request Form
  • Water Bill or Proof of Residence in Legazpi City
  • Property Location & Septic Access Verification Sketch Confirming truck accessibility to septic tank inlet
  • Signed Authorization & Consent of Homeowner

Feedback & Environmental Violations (PACD)

  • Environmental Incident Report: Illegal dumping, open burning (anti-siga), smoke belching, or noise/odor pollution
  • Supporting Evidence: Photos, video, or location coordinates
  • How to File: Fill up PACD Complaint Form at Ground Floor, City Hall, or email legazpi.ocenr@gmail.com
  • Response Turnaround: Immediate action & inspection within 24–48 hours
Service / Environmental Transaction Fee Processing Time
Residential Septic Tank Desludging (Standard Tank ≤ 5 cu.m.) ₱1,500.00 / tank 1–2 working days
Commercial / Institutional Septage Desludging & Treatment ₱500.00 / cu.m. 1–2 working days
Certificate of Septic Tank Desludging Compliance FREE Issued after service
Environmental Public Assistance, Verification & Inquiries (PACD) FREE Immediate / 2 hours
3-Step Septic Tank Desludging Workflow
1 BOOK SCHEDULE

Submit request form with proof of address; staff verifies truck access route and schedules service date.

Septage Coordinator
2 DESLUDGE

City vacuum tanker team arrives at residence, pumps out septage, and transports it to the Septage Treatment Plant.

Sanitation Crew
3 PAY & CERTIFY

Pay desludging fee, receive Official Receipt, and claim Certificate of Desludging Compliance.

Collector / Desludging In-Charge
