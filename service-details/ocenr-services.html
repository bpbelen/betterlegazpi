7 Getting a Certificate of No Objection to cut a tree 6 days & 40 minutes at the counter Done in one visit 15 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The city's certificate saying it does not object to trees being cut. It is not the permit itself: the Permit to Cut Tree is issued by the DENR, and the DENR requires this certification from the LGU as one of its requirements. Go to DENR-PENRO Albay first for their checklist, then come here.

Feedback and complaints

Answer the Feedback Form at the OCENR frontline desk and drop it in the drop box, or hand it to the Officer of the Day. You can also call 0946-599-0597.

The Administrative Division checks what the feedback is about within one working day and passes it to the division concerned. Once that division replies, the office contacts you by email, text or phone. Complaints go on the Complaint Form (CSC Form No. 3) or by email, and need your full name, address and contact details, an account of what happened, any evidence, and the name of the person being complained about. The Administrative Division reads complaints daily, coordinates with the division concerned, investigates where needed, then writes an Incident Report for the Legal Office. The Legal Office sends its findings to the City Mayor, copy to OCENR, for final action.
