Securing of Permit to Dump

Face-to-Face

Permits for individuals and commercial firms requesting direct disposal of segregated solid waste at the city's designated sanitary landfill or composting facilities.

Tap to view requirements, fees, & 4-step process

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter of Request: 1 original and 1 photocopy of Letter of Request. Client
  • Permit to Dump Form: Duly filled-out Permit to Dump Form. OCENR

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
1 Hour & 35 Minutes
Standard Fees
None (₱0.00)
Mode of Transaction
Face-to-Face (OCENR Front Desk & Site)
Policy Overview: The OCENR implements timed and scheduled segregated collection daily from established Materials Recovery Facilities or Drop-Off-Centers (MRDOC) of each barangay. The "No Segregation, No Collection" policy is strictly enforced. Direct disposal by individuals and firms is accommodated on a case-to-case basis upon request.
Step Client & Agency Action Fees Officer
1 Filing of Request (Location: OCENR – Front Desk)
• Submit letter of request to the Desk Officer one week before the scheduled date.
• Desk Officer reviews the request and issues Permit to Dump Form (5 mins). 		—
Administrative Personnel
2 Accomplishment of Form (Location: OCENR – Front Desk)
• Fill out the Permit to Dump Form.
• Desk Officer reviews the accomplished form and checks if all fields are properly filled out (10 mins). 		—
Administrative Personnel
3 Ocular Inspection (Location: Disposal Site / Client's Location)
• Allow inspection of garbage for disposal.
• Technical personnel conduct an ocular inspection of the garbage subject for disposal (1 hr, depends on location). 		—
Technical Personnel
4 Evaluation & Recommendation (Location: OCENR – Technical Office)
• Wait for inspection report and recommendation.
• Administrative personnel prepare the inspection report and recommendation for approval (10 mins). 		—
Administrative Personnel
5 Issuance of Permit (Location: OCENR – Front Desk)
• Claim the Permit to Dump (or receive explanation if declined).
• City ENRO issues the Permit to Dump if approved (5 mins) or explains reason for disapproval if declined (5 mins). 		—
City ENRO

Securing of Visitors Entry Pass to the City Sanitary Landfill Facility

Face-to-Face

Entry clearance and orientation passes for visiting LGUs, academic institutions, students, and entrepreneurs visiting the Legazpi City Sanitary Landfill Facility (SLF) in Banquerohan.

Tap to view visitor zoning, requirements, & steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter of Request: 1 original and 1 photocopy of Letter of Request stating date and purpose. Client
  • SLF Visitors Entry Pass Form: Duly filled-out SLF Visitors Entry Pass Form. OCENR

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
1 Hour & 18 Minutes
Standard Fees
None (₱0.00)
Mode of Transaction
Face-to-Face (OCENR Front Desk & Banquerohan SLF)
Facility Information: Established under RA 9003 ("Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000"), the Banquerohan SLF protects public health and the environment. Visitors are classified and guided into Yellow Zone (Safe Area) and Red Zone (Critical Area) with technical assistance and project orientation provided by OCENR staff.
Step Client & Agency Action Fees Officer
1 Filing of Request (Location: OCENR – Front Desk)
• Submit letter of request to the Desk Officer.
• Information Desk Officer refers the client to the OCENR personnel project-in-charge (3 mins).
• OCENR Personnel requests the client to fill out the SLF Visitors Entry Pass Form (5 mins). 		—
Administrative Personnel
2 Accomplishment of Form & Orientation (Location: OCENR – Front Desk / SLF Site)
• Fill out the SLF Visitors Entry Pass Form.
• OCENR Personnel checks accomplished form and asks if project orientation is needed (5 mins).
• If orientation is required, City ENRO assigns Technical Personnel to conduct it (1 hr).
• OCENR issues the SLF Visitors Entry Pass (5 mins). 		—
Special Operations Officer III Administrative Personnel

Securing of a Certificate of No Objection to Cut Tree

Face-to-Face

LGU clearance certifying that Legazpi City has no objection to cutting down trees, required as a prerequisite before securing a Permit to Cut Tree from the DENR.

Tap to view 15 checklist requirements, inspection, & steps

Checklist of Requirements (2 Original Copies)

  • Letter of Request: Addressed to City Mayor, Attn: City ENRO (with focal person contact number). Client
  • Barangay Certificate: Brgy. Certificate of No Objection to Cut Trees. Barangay
  • Land Title / Tax Declaration: Proof of ownership for private lots. Client
  • Tree Map & Geotagged Photos: Diagram and individual photos with GPS coordinates and inventory list. Client
  • Site Plan & Replacement Undertaking: Site development plan and signed Tree Replacement Undertaking. Client / OCENR
  • Clearances (if applicable): ECC/CNC from EMB V, Zoning Certificate, & Locational Clearance (City Ord. No. 14-0011-2019). CPDO / EMB V

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
6 Days & 40 Minutes
Standard Fees
None (₱0.00)
Mode of Transaction
Face-to-Face (Mayor's Office, OCENR & Site)
Advisory: Clients should proceed first to DENR-PENRO Albay to seek guidance on DENR requirements and checklists. Processing time at OCENR may be extended depending on the complexity of ocular validation and simultaneous applications.
Step Client & Agency Action Fees Officer
1 Filing of Request (Location: City Mayor's Office / OCENR Front Desk)
• Submit letter of request with complete requirements to City Mayor's Office and provide duplicate copy to OCENR Desk Officer.
• Information Desk Officer receives and records the letter, then forwards it to City ENRO (5 mins). 		—
Administrative Personnel
2 Initial Evaluation (Location: OCENR – ENRO Office)
• Wait for instruction on evaluation.
• City ENRO conducts initial evaluation of request and gives instructions to assigned personnel (15 mins). 		—
City ENRO
3 Document Evaluation & Scheduling (Location: OCENR – Technical Office)
• Wait for inspection schedule.
• Personnel in-charge conducts document evaluation and schedules actual inspection date (1 day). 		—
Project Development Officer III / Technical Personnel
4 Ocular Inspection (Location: Project Site)
• Allow inspection of trees to be cut.
• Technical personnel conduct ocular inspection to validate request (5 days, depending on complexity).
• Prepares necessary certification for City ENRO's signature. 		—
Project Development Officer III / Technical Personnel
5 Review & Signing of Certificate (Location: OCENR – ENRO Office)
• Wait for review and approval.
• City ENRO checks and reviews Certificate of No Objection and signs certification if valid (10 mins). 		—
City ENRO
6 Release of Certification (Location: OCENR – Front Desk)
• Claim the Certificate of No Objection to Cut Tree.
• Desk Officer records and releases signed certification to client to support DENR permit application (10 mins). 		—
Administrative Personnel

Face-to-Face

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter of Request: Written letter requesting septic tank desludging. Client
  • Survey Form: Completely accomplished Septage Survey Form. OCENR

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
2 Hours & 12 Minutes
Standard Fees
Septage Fee (As Assessed by CTO/CEO)
Mode of Transaction
Face-to-Face (OCENR, CTO & Client Site)
Legal Mandate: Enacted under City Ordinance No. 0025-2015 ("Septage Management Code"), this program protects public health and water bodies across the territorial jurisdiction of Legazpi City. Actual vacuum truck operation depends on vehicle availability.

Surveyor / Coordinator
