1. Securing of Permit to Dump (Solid Waste Management)

OCENR implements timed and scheduled segregated collection daily from Barangay MRDOCs. The strictly enforced "No Segregation, No Collection" policy mandates proper waste diversion to composting facilities or the City Sanitary Landfill Facility. Direct garbage disposal requests by individuals and firms are issued on a case-to-case basis.

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter of Request Client
    1 original and 1 photocopy submitted to Desk Officer at least one (1) week prior to scheduled date
  • Permit to Dump Form OCENR Front Desk
    Duly filled-out form detailing disposal source, volume, and waste type

2. Securing of Visitors Entry Pass to City Sanitary Landfill Facility (Banquerohan SLF)

Under RA 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000), Legazpi City established its Category 4 Sanitary Landfill Facility (SLF). The facility frequently welcomes visiting LGUs, academic institutions, and entrepreneurs. Areas are designated into Yellow Zone (Safe Area) and Red Zone (Critical Area) with guided technical assistance.

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter of Request Client
    1 original and 1 photocopy stating date, purpose, and total number of visitors
  • SLF Visitors Entry Pass Form OCENR Front Desk
    Duly filled-out visitor pass form & orientation commitment

3. Environmental Certificate for Business Permit (Face-to-Face & Virtual Seminars)

Per City Ordinance No. 0010-2008, all commercial establishments operating in Legazpi City must undergo an Ecological Solid Waste Management (ESWM) Seminar prior to issuance or renewal of Mayor's Business Permit. Certificates are valid for three (3) years. Seminars run 5x/week during peak renewal (Jan-Mar) and 2x/week from Apr-Dec.

Checklist of Requirements

General Requirements (All Applicants)
  • Environmental Baseline Data Sheet (EBDS) OCENR Facebook / Google Forms
    Accomplished EBDS form filed online via Google Forms link
  • Seminar Attendance OCENR (Zoom / 3rd Floor OCENR Office)
    Attendance to Virtual Environmental Webinar via Zoom or Face-to-Face Seminar
SEMINAR MODALITIES & CATEGORIES (CITY ORDINANCE NO. 0010-2008)
Category I: Virtual (Non-Compliant / Expired)

For operational businesses with expired certificates. Online EBDS via Google Forms + Zoom webinar (2 hrs 55 mins total).

Category II: Virtual (New / Renewal)

For new applications or renewals with active EC validity from BPLO database. Zoom webinar attendance (2 hrs 50 mins total).

Category III: Face-to-Face (Non-Compliant / Expired)

In-person seminar at 3rd Floor OCENR Office for non-compliant or expired certificates (2 hrs 23 mins total).

Category IV: Face-to-Face (New / Renewal)

In-person seminar at 3rd Floor OCENR Office for new/renewal permits upon EBDS submission (2 hrs 13 mins total).

4. Certificate of No Objection to Cut Tree (DENR Requirement)

Entities wishing to cut trees inside/outside private property or project sites must secure a Permit to Cut Tree from DENR-PENRO Albay. As a mandatory prerequisite, a Certification of No Objection must be obtained from LGU Legazpi City through OCENR.

Checklist of Requirements (2 Original Copies)

  • Letter of Request to City Mayor (Attn: City ENRO) Client
    Include contact mobile number for inspection scheduling
  • Barangay Certificate of No Objection Barangay Hall
  • Land Title / Tax Declaration & Map Diagram Client
    Includes geotagged photos of trees & tree inventory list
  • Development Plan, ECC/CNC & Tree Replacement Undertaking CPDO / DENR-EMB / OCENR
    For commercial/government projects (Zoning & Locational Clearance per City Ordinance No. 14-0011-2019)

Summary & Information

5. Certification for Registration of Chainsaw (DENR Requirement)

Prior to registering a chainsaw unit with the DENR under the Chainsaw Act, owners must secure a supporting LGU Certification of No Objection / Registration Support from OCENR Legazpi City.

6. Request for Free Tree Seedlings (Community Tree Growing)

Schools, civil society organizations, businesses, and community groups conducting tree growing and greening activities may request free seedlings from OCENR City Nursery to support urban reforestation and biodiversity conservation.

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter of Request to City Mayor (Attn: City ENRO) Client
    Specifying planting site, target quantity, tree species, and focal person contact details
  • Promissory of Undertaking OCENR Office
    Signed agreement ensuring care, maintenance, and monitoring of planted seedlings

8. Desludging & Syphoning of Septic Tanks (Septage Management Code)

Under Legazpi City Ordinance No. 0025-2015 ("Septage Management Code"), all residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional structures in the city must undergo periodic septic tank desludging to protect groundwater quality and public health.

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter of Request Client
  • OCENR Septage Survey Interview Form OCENR Office
  • Official Receipt of Septage Fee Payment CTO / CEO
  • Signed Manifest Form OCENR Desludging Team
