Recruitment, Selection and Placement

Walk-in & Online Submission

Facilitates merit-based hiring and placement for plantilla vacant positions based on Civil Service Commission Qualification Standards (QS) and the Equal Opportunity Principle (EOP).

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Personal Data Sheet (PDS): 1 fully accomplished CS Form No. 212 (Revised 2025) with Work Experience Sheet and recent passport-sized or unfiltered digital picture; digitally or electronically signed. (Downloadable via csc.gov.ph)
  • Performance Rating: 1 hard copy or electronic copy of performance rating in the last rating period (if applicable; from office where applicant is assigned).
  • Proof of Eligibility / Rating / License: 1 hard copy or electronic copy of CSC Eligibility, PRC Board Rating/ID, or relevant bar/board license (from CSC / PRC).
  • Transcript of Records (TOR): 1 hard copy or electronic copy of official Transcript of Records / Diploma (from school graduated).

Processing Application for Leave

Internal LGU Processing

Processes, computes, certifies, and audits all statutory leave applications (Vacation, Sick, Maternity/Paternity, Monetization, Forced, SLP, Terminal, and Rehabilitation).

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Application Form: 3 copies of CSC Form No. 6 / Application for Leave Form.
  • Vacation & Forced Annual Schedule: Must be included in the annual forced leave roster.
  • Special Leave SLP Application: Up to 3 days non-cumulative per calendar year.
  • Sick Leave > 5 Days Medical Certificate: Detailing diagnosis and resting days.
  • Maternity / Paternity Delivery Notice: Proof of delivery or legal child custody.
  • Monetization > 10 Days Mayor's Approval: Letter of Intent + Employee Waiver Form.
  • Terminal Leave Separation Clearance: Official LGU clearance and certified leave credits.
  • Rehabilitation Leave Incident Report: Letter request, medical cert, and police blotter.

Work Immersion / On-the-Job Trainees (WI/OJT) Deployment

School Endorsement

Facilitates the institutional Memorandum of Agreement (MOA), orientation, placement, and Certificate of Completion for high school and college student interns.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter Request: 1 official letter request with list of students, approved by the City Mayor before the school year starts (from School Concerned).
  • Memorandum of Agreement (MOA): Executed partnership MOA between the educational institution and LGU Legazpi City.
  • Personal Data Sheet (PDS): 1 original copy per student applicant.
  • Medical Certificate: 1 original copy indicating that the student is physically fit to perform assigned duties/tasks (from Healthcare Professional).
  • Senior High School Parent Consent: Required for all work immersion students.
