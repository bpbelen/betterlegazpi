Recruitment, Selection and Placement (RSP)

Qualification Standards (QS) hiring for city government plantilla positions

Checklist of Requirements (Plantilla Vacancies)

  • Personal Data Sheet (PDS): CS Form No. 212 (Revised 2025) with Work Experience Sheet & recent passport-sized picture; digitally or electronically signed From the CSC Official Website (csc.gov.ph)
  • Performance Rating: Hard copy or electronic copy of rating in the last rating period (if applicable) From Office where applicant is assigned
  • Proof of Eligibility / License: Hard copy or electronic copy of CSC eligibility rating or PRC professional license From Civil Service Commission (CSC) / PRC
  • Transcript of Records (TOR): Hard copy or electronic copy of academic transcript From School / University graduated

Important Notes on Filing

  • Complete Supporting Documents: Applications with incomplete documents shall not be processed or evaluated. From CHRMO Qualification Standards Policy
  • CSC Publication Deadlines: Applications submitted before publication or after the deadline posted in the CSC website shall no longer be entertained. From Civil Service Commission (CSC) Portal
  • Specific Position Specification: The specific position applied for including item number and place of assignment must be clearly stated in the application letter. Without such specification, the application shall be disregarded. From Applicant Application Letter
  • Equal Opportunities (EOP): Open to all qualified applicants including PWDs and indigenous communities, regardless of sexual orientation, civil status, religion, or political affiliation. From City Government of Legazpi EOP Policy

Work Immersion / On-the-Job Trainees (WI/OJT) Deployment

Facilitation of academic internship and work immersion deployment for partner schools

Checklist of Requirements (Schools & Students)

  • School Letter Request: Complete list of students, submitted & approved by the City Mayor before the school year starts From School Concerned
  • Memorandum of Agreement (MOA): Duly executed institutional partnership agreement From School Concerned & Sangguniang Panlungsod
  • Personal Data Sheet (PDS): One (1) original copy of student info sheet From Student Applicant
  • Medical Certificate: One (1) original copy indicating student is physically fit to perform duties From Licensed Healthcare Professional
  • Parental Consent: Mandatory consent form for senior high school immersion students From Parent / Legal Guardian

Training Deliverables & Completion

  • Orientation & Briefing: Mandatory 1.5-hour orientation on office decorum and local government protocols From Learning and Development (L&D) Division
  • Departmental Deployment: Trainees deployed to corresponding city departments according to specialization (1 hr) From Assigned City Hall Department / Office
  • Issuance of Certificate of Completion: Prepared, signed, and released upon completion of required internship hours (20 mins) From Learning and Development (L&D) Division

Preparation of Contract of Services / Job Order Appointments

Facilitation and execution of contracts and appointments for city government project personnel

Checklist of Requirements

  • Endorsement Letter: Formal endorsement from the concerned Chief of Office From Office Concerned
  • Personal Data Sheet (PDS): Fully accomplished CS Form No. 212 (Rev. 2025) with recent passport-sized picture From Client / Appointee
  • Drug Test Result: Recent negative drug screening certificate From Accredited Drug Test Clinic

Important: Notarization

  • For Contract of Service (COS): After signing, the client must cause the contract to be notarized before it takes effect. From Licensed Notary Public (Client's responsibility)
  • For Job Order Appointments: No notarization required. CHRMO records and releases a certified true copy of the appointment directly. From CHRMO Officer of the Day

Checklist of Requirements (Travel Orders)

  • Annex A – Travel/Training Request Form: Duly accomplished form for the requested training, seminar, or convention From Office Concerned

  • Certificate of Availability of Funds (CAF): Certification that funds are available to cover the training-related expenses From City Budget Office

Processing Applications for Leave & Leave Monetization

Leave credit computation, certification, and monetization processing for city employees

Checklist of Requirements per Leave Type

  • Vacation Leave / Forced Leave / SLP: CSC Form No. 6 / Application for Leave (3 copies); for Forced Leave, dates must be included in the schedule submitted to CHRMO From AO / PICAR of assigned office/division
  • Sick Leave: Medical Certificate required if sick leave exceeds five (5) consecutive days From Attending Physician / Health Clinic
  • Maternity / Paternity Leave: CSC Form No. 6 / Application for Leave (3 copies) From AO / PICAR of assigned office/division
  • Rehabilitation Leave: Request Letter to Mayor via HR; Medical Certificate stating nature of injury, treatment process, and rehabilitation period; plus other pertinent documents (e.g., police blotter) From Attending Doctor / Health Facility / PNP
  • Terminal Leave: Filed upon separation/retirement; processed together with retirement documents From CHRMO Records Management Division
  • Monetization of Leave Credits (>10 days): Letter of Intent approved by the City Mayor (citing reason for availment) + Signed Waiver Form from fellow plantilla employee From City Mayor's Office & Co-employee

Processing Standards & Financial Release

  • Standard Leave Processing: Verification and certification of available leave credits (1 hr 30 mins) From Records Management Division (RMD)
  • Monetization Financial Documents: Preparation of financial vouchers and transmission to Budget, Accounting, and Treasury (5 days) From City Budget, Accounting, and Treasury
  • Document Distribution: Two (2) copies released to applicant / office, one (1) copy retained for CHRMO filing From Records Management Division (RMD)

Processing of Retirement Documents

Facilitation of retirement records, leave credit computation, and terminal leave benefits

Retirement Categories & Requirements

  • Optional Retirement: Letter of Intent to Retire with the formal approval of the City Mayor From Retiree or AO/PICAR of assigned department
  • Mandatory Retirement (Age 65): Automatic processing initiated by CHRMO Records Management Division From CHRMO Records Management Division

Terminal Leave & Financial Clearances

  • Lifetime Leave Re-computation: Updating and re-computing earned leave credits starting from initial date of city employment (5 days) From Records Management Division (RMD)
  • Financial Package Preparation: Drafting retirement resolutions, terminal leave vouchers, and clearance packages (1 day) From CHRMO Administrative & Financial Team
  • Inter-Agency Transmittal: Transmitting retirement documents to Budget, Accounting, and Treasury for fund release (5 days) From City Budget, Accounting, and Treasury

Public Assistance & Complaints Mechanism (PACD)

Citizen feedback, inquiries, ARTA compliance, and official redress procedures

How to Send Feedback & Inquiries

  • Feedback Forms: Answer the Feedback Form at the CHRMO Frontline Desk and place it in the drop box or hand over to Officer of the Day (OD) From CHRMO Frontline Desk
  • Feedback Processing: L&D Division verifies queries within one (1) working day and informs client via email, text, or call From Learning and Development (L&D) Division
  • Direct Inquiries: 0929 494-6681 | chrmo.leg@legazpi.gov.ph / chrmo.legazpicity@gmail.com From CHRMO Public Assistance Desk

How to File a Formal Complaint (CSC Form #3)

  • Complaint Form (CSC Form #3): Provide complainant full name, contact info, narrative of complaint, evidences, and respondent name From Complainant / CSC Form #3
  • Investigation & Incident Report: L&D Division evaluates complaints daily, investigates with the concerned division, and drafts Incident Report From Learning and Development (L&D) Division
  • Executive Review: Incident Report referred to City Legal Office and City Mayor for appropriate executive action From City Legal Office & City Mayor
