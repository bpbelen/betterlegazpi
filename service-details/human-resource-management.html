How it works

  1. 1Applicants submit application thru email or walk-in

    The office then

    1. Receives/accepts the application and evaluates completeness of supporting documents

      10 minutesOfficer-of-the Day/RSP Division

    2. Acknowledge receipt of applications thru email

      10 minutesAdmin. Asst. VI (CO III), RSP Division

    3. Conducts assessment and evaluates application based on the submitted documents and minimum Qualification Standards

      1 hr/applicantSAO/Admin. Asst. VI (CO III)/Admin. Aide I, RSP Division

    4. Profiling of qualified applicants and preparation of documents for HRMPSB selection/deliberation

      1 hr/applicant; 30 min/applicant; 7 daysSAO/Admin. Asst. VI (CO III), RSP Division

    5. For applicants who did not qualify for the positions based on pre-assessment or not selected by the Appointing Authority: Inform the applicant thru email or a letter/notice

    6. Conducts Background Investigation

    7. Conducts Behavioral Event Interview

      30 min.HRMPSB members

    8. Assessment, deliberation and rating of qualified applicants by the HRMPSB

      2 days; 15 daysCGDH I, CHRMO; HRMPSB members

  2. 2If selected, applicant complies with the requirements

    The office then

    1. Prepare appointment papers and other CSC required documents

      SAO/Admin. Asst. VI (CO III)/Admin. Aide I, RSP Division; CGDH I, CHRMO; Appointing Authority

    2. Send letters to qualified applicants who were not selected

      5 daysAdmin. Asst. VI (CO III), RSP Div.; CGDH I, CHRMO

Total time 29 days, 3 hrs. & 20 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • Applications with incomplete documents shall not be processed or evaluated.
  • Applications submitted before the publication and after the deadline indicated in the publication of vacant positions posted in the CSC website shall no longer be entertained.
  • The specific position applied for including the item and place of assignment must be indicated in the application letter. Without such specifications, the application shall be disregarded.
  • All interested and qualified applicants are encouraged to apply. This includes Persons with Disability (PWD) and members of the indigenous communities, irrespective of sexual orientation, civil status, religion and political affiliation. The City Government of Legazpi does not discriminate in the selection of employees based on the aforementioned pursuant to Equal Opportunities for Employment Principle (EOP).

Feedback and complaints

Answer the Feedback Form located in the Frontline Desk of the CHRMO, then place it inside the drop box or personally hand it over to the Officer of the Day (OD).

The L&D Division verifies the nature of the queries and feedback within one (1) working day. The same shall be referred to the concerned Division. Upon receipt of reply from the concerned Division, the Client will be informed via email, text or phone call.
