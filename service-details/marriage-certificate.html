Mandatory Registration Deadlines

The Certificate of Marriage (COM) of a Civil or Church wedding with Marriage License must be registered within fifteen (15) days after solemnization. For weddings under Article 34 (cohabitation) or P.D. 1083 (Muslim Law), registration must be within thirty (30) days. Registration of on-time marriage certificates is 100% FREE OF CHARGE pursuant to Section 6 of P.D. No. 651. Delayed registration fee is ₱500.00 (subject to a mandatory 10-day notice of posting).

Step-by-Step Registration Process

Follow these steps to register your Certificate of Marriage in Legazpi City

1

Submit Certificate of Marriage

Submit 4 original copies of accomplished Certificate of Marriage (COM / Form 97). Must be duly signed by contracting parties, solemnizing officer, and at least two witnesses.

2

Verification & Assessment

Receiving officer (M. Loria / D. Abejuro / M. Azcune / J.B. Morcoso) examines completeness. Timely registration is FREE; delayed registration receives Order of Payment (₱500).

3

Claim Registered Certificate

Receive your registered copy of COM duly signed and sealed by the City Civil Registrar (Priscilla L. Galicia). Timely registration takes ~25 minutes.

Requirements Checklist

Documents needed for marriage registration and certified copies

For Timely Registration (Within 15 / 30 Days) — FREE

  • 4 original copies of Certificate of Marriage duly signed by bride, groom, and solemnizing officer
  • Signatures of at least two (2) principal witnesses of legal age
  • Original Marriage License or notarized Affidavit under Article 34 (cohabiting 5+ years) / PD 1083

For Delayed Registration (Beyond Deadline) — ₱500.00

  • Accomplished Affidavit for Delayed Registration of Marriage on the reverse of the COM
  • PSA Negative Certification of Marriage (CRS Form No. 3)
  • Affidavit of the Solemnizing Officer or presenting person explaining circumstances and reason for delay
  • 10-day mandatory public notice of posting before release

For Certified True Copy (CTC / Local Copy) — ₱70.00

  • Accomplished Verification Form at CCRO counter
  • Valid Government-Issued ID of either spouse (or Authorization Letter if representative)
  • Official Receipt of ₱70.00 paid at Cashier (released in ~10 minutes)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee for registering a marriage certificate on time?
No. On-time registration of marriage certificates is 100% free of charge pursuant to Section 6 of Presidential Decree No. 651 and the Legazpi City Citizen's Charter.
What is the deadline for registering a marriage?
For civil or church weddings solemnized with a Marriage License, the deadline is fifteen (15) calendar days after the wedding ceremony. For marriages exempted from license requirements (under Article 34 cohabitation or Muslim Code PD 1083), the deadline is thirty (30) days.
Who is responsible for submitting the marriage certificate for registration?
Under Philippine law, the solemnizing officer (judge, priest, pastor, or mayor) is primarily responsible for transmitting copies of the COM to the Local Civil Registrar. However, contracting parties or their authorized representatives may also submit it directly.
How long is an issued Marriage License valid?
A Marriage License is valid anywhere in the Philippines for one hundred twenty (120) days from the date of issuance.

Basic Requirements for Marriage License

  • PSA Certified Birth Certificate (1 original + 1 photocopy each)
  • PSA Certificate of No Marriage Record / CENOMAR (valid within 6 months)
  • Barangay Certificate of Residency in Legazpi City (at least one applicant)
  • Valid Government ID & Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
  • 1 pc. 2x2 ID picture of both bride and groom

Special Cases & Age Requirements

  • Ages 18–21: Notarized Parental Consent signed by father/mother/guardian
  • Ages 22–24: Notarized Parental Advice from parents
  • Foreign Citizens: Certificate of Legal Capacity to Marry issued by Embassy in the PH
  • Widowed: PSA Certified Death Certificate of deceased spouse
  • Annulled / Divorced: Court Decree of Annulment or Foreign Divorce with Certificate of Finality
