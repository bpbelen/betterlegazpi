New Business Permit Application

Face-to-Face (Lane 2) or Online

Issuance of Mayor's Permit to operate a new commercial establishment in Legazpi City. Walk-in applications are submitted via Lane 2, or processed online via the city's official Filipizen portal.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Application Form: 1 original duly filled-out Unified Application Form (UAF).
  • Location Sketch: 1 original copy of Sketch of Business Location.
  • Certificate of Occupancy: 1 original & 1 photocopy of Certificate of Occupancy of the new space/building.
  • Business Registration: 1 original & 1 photocopy of DTI (Sole Prop), SEC (Corp/Partnership), or CDA (Cooperative) Certificate.
  • If Leased Tenancy Proof: 1 original Notarized Lease Contract or Letter of Authorization/Consent from owner.
  • Market Stalls Only Market Clearance: 1 original & 1 photocopy of Market Clearance.

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
3 Days (Maximum)
Standard Fees
Computed in TOP (Ord. No. 13-2007)
Mode of Transaction
Lane 2 (Intake) • Lane 9 (Releasing) / Online (Filipizen)
Step Client Step Agency Action Fees Officer
1 Submit Application & Requirements
Submit documents to Lane 2 (or upload PDF/image files online at filipizen.com). 		Action Officer encodes data, electronically submits for BPLO approval; CTO Assessor assesses taxes/fees; BPLO Staff prints application form, Tax Order of Payment (TOP), and feedback form. N/A Lane 2 Action Officer
2 Pay Taxes & Fees
Proceed to City Treasurer's Office or pay online via Land Bank, DBP, GCash, Maya, Visa, or Mastercard using the TOP assessment record. 		CTO Collector receives payment and issues Official Receipt (or payment gateway processes e-payment). Computed in TOP
City Ord. No. 13-2007 		CTO Revenue Collector
3 Claim Business Permit & Clearances
Proceed to BPLO Lane 9 (Releasing Area) and submit completed Feedback Form (or receive e-copy via email). 		Releasing Officer issues Locational Clearance, Mayor's Permit to operate, Business Plate, and Official Sticker. N/A Lane 9 Releasing Officer

Renewal of Business Permit

Face-to-Face (Lanes 1, 3-7) or Online

Annual renewal of existing business permits in Legazpi City. Walk-in transactions are handled through dedicated lane routing (Priority, Express, and Bulk lanes), and online renewal is supported through BIN lookup.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Proof of Income: 1 original & 1 photocopy of latest BIR income tax return or Audited Financial Statement (AFS).
  • Market Stalls Only Market Clearance: 1 original copy for Market Stall Holders (returned upon permit release).
  • Pawnshops & MSBs Authority to Operate: 1 original & 1 photocopy of Certificate of Authority (JMC No. 1 s. 2019) or BSP Letter Code.
  • Online Renewal BIN Account: Business Identification Number (BIN) from previous permit barcode.

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
2 Hours (F2F) • 1.5 Hours (Online)
Standard Fees
Computed in TOP (Ord. No. 13-2007)
Mode of Transaction
Lanes 1, 3-7 (Intake) • Lane 9 (Releasing) / Online (Filipizen)
Step Client Step Agency Action Fees Officer
1 Submit Renewal Documents
Present documents to designated renewal lane (Lane 1, 3, 4, 6, or 7) or input BIN online at Filipizen. 		Action Officer encodes data, submits electronically for BPLO approval, CTO Assessor calculates taxes/fees, prints final application & TOP. N/A Designated Renewal Lane Officer
2 Pay Computed Taxes & Fees
Pay at CTO Cashier or via partner banks (Land Bank, DBP) / e-wallets (GCash, Maya). 		CTO Collector prepares and issues Official Receipt; directs applicant to Lane 9. Computed in TOP
City Ord. No. 13-2007 		CTO Revenue Collector
3 Claim Business Permit
Proceed to Lane 9 (Releasing) with completed Feedback Form. 		Releasing Officer issues Renewed Business Permit, sticker, plate, and regulatory clearances. N/A Lane 9 Releasing Officer

Transfer of Business Location

Face-to-Face (Lane 5)

Updating permit records and clearances when relocating an existing business establishment to a new physical site within Legazpi City.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Application Form: 1 copy of duly filled-out Application Form.
  • New Site Sketch: 1 copy of Sketch of Business Location (New Site).
  • Certificate of Occupancy: 1 original Certificate of Occupancy of the new space/building.
  • Business Registration: 1 photocopy of DTI/SEC/CDA Certificate.
  • Fire Safety Certificate (FSIC): 1 original FSIC for occupancy (with fire insurance policy if available).
  • If Leased Lease Contract: Valid Lease Contract from owner (lessor must hold valid business permit).

Service Summary

3 Days (Maximum)

Lane 5 (Intake) • Lane 9 (Releasing)
Step Client Step Agency Action Fees Officer

Submit relocation requirements to Lane 5. 		Action Officer verifies documents, encodes data, prints TOP and feedback form. N/A Lane 5 Action Officer
2 Pay Transfer Fees & Taxes
Pay computed transfer fees & taxes at City Treasurer's Office. 		CTO Collector receives payment and issues Official Receipt. Per TOP Assessment CTO Revenue Collector
3 Claim Relocated Permit
Proceed to Lane 9 with Official Receipt and Feedback Form. 		Releases updated Mayor's Permit, business plate/sticker for new location. N/A Lane 9 Releasing Officer

Mayor's Tricycle Operators' Permit (MTOP)

Lane 11 (Inspection) & Lane 10 (Releasing)

Franchise licensing, annual renewal, motor substitution, and transfer for motorized tricycle-for-hire units operating within Legazpi City.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Application Form: 1 duly filled-out MTOP Application Form.
  • Cedula: 1 original copy of Community Tax Certificate.
  • If Changing Motor Dropping & Substitution: 1 original copy of Dropping and Substitution documents.
  • Renewal / Transfer Franchise Certificate: 1 original copy of Franchise Certificate.
  • Family / Estate Proof of Relation: Marriage/birth certificate (if filed by immediate family) or death certificate (if holder is deceased).

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
1 Hour (Maximum)
Standard Fees
Per TOP Assessment (Ord. No. 13-2007)
Mode of Transaction
Lane 11 (PSO Inspection) • Lane 10 (Releasing)

Present requirements to Lane 11, proceed to defined inspection area for PSO roadworthiness check, and return vehicle inspection report. 		Action Officer verifies documents, issues vehicle inspection report form; PSO inspects unit; verifies report, issues Order of Payment & feedback form. N/A Lane 11 / PSO Inspector
2 Pay MTOP Fees
Proceed to City Treasurer's Office to pay assessed fees. 		CTO Collector receives payment and issues Official Receipt. Per TOP List
City Ord. No. 13-2007 		CTO Revenue Collector
3 Claim MTOP & Sticker
Proceed to Lane 10 (Releasing MTOP), submit Official Receipt and Feedback Form, and sign logbook. 		Encodes and prints Mayor's Permit in 2 copies, routes for signature (BPLO & Local Chief Executive), releases permit and official sticker. N/A Lane 10 Releasing Officer

Mayor's Clearance

Face-to-Face (Lane 8)

Official municipal clearance issued by the City Mayor for local employment, overseas travel, firearm licensing, and other legal transactions.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Police Clearance: 1 original copy of Police Clearance.
  • Barangay Clearance: 1 original copy of Barangay Clearance.
  • City Court Clearance: 1 original copy of City Court Clearance.
  • City Prosecutor's Clearance: 1 original copy of City Prosecutor's Clearance.
  • Note Clearance Retention: All original clearances are returned to the applicant together with the issued Mayor's Clearance.

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
20 Minutes (Maximum)
Standard Fees
Per City Ordinance No. 13-2007
Mode of Transaction
Lane 8 (Intake & Releasing) • City Mayor's Office
Step Client Step Agency Action Fees Officer
1 Submit Clearance Requirements
Submit 4 original clearances to Lane 8 and sign logbook. 		Action Officer checks completeness, encodes/prints clearance in 2 copies, issues feedback form, directs to CTO. N/A Lane 8 Action Officer
2 Pay Clearance Fee
Proceed to City Treasurer's Office and pay clearance fee. 		CTO Collector receives payment and issues Official Receipt. Per City Ordinance CTO Revenue Collector
3 Claim Signed Clearance
Return to Lane 8, submit OR & Feedback Form, affix signature and thumbmark. 		Action Officer secures BPLO initial, releases document & original requirements, directs client to City Mayor's Office for final signature. N/A Lane 8 Action Officer
