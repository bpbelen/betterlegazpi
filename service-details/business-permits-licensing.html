How it works

  1. 1Submit the required documents to Lane 2.

    The office then

    1.   3. 3Go to the BPLO releasing area at Lane 9 and hand in the accomplished feedback form. You receive the locational clearance for the business and the Mayor's Permit to operate it.

    The office then

    1. The BPLO Releasing Officer releases the business permit with the business plate or sticker and the other clearances.

      Lane 9 Releasing

What to bring 1 item

How it works

  1. 1Log in to legazpi.gov.ph, go to Online Services then Services, click "new business application", specify the required information, fill out the Unified Application Form, attach the required documents as PDF or image files, and review the information before submitting. When the application is approved a notification about the Tax Order of Payment reaches you by email or text.

    The office then

    1. On receiving the complete requirements the Action Officer processes the application and sends the Tax Order of Payment or Assessment Record by email or text. If the application is not eligible you are given a notice of deficiency for compliance. The Action Officer emails a Feedback Form to be submitted when you claim the permit, and advises you to pay through Land Bank, DBP, GCash or Paymaya following the instructions on the city website, or at the City Treasurer's Office payment area.

      3 daysLicensing Inspector I / Admin. Officer I

  2. 2To pay online, type the application number found on the Tax Order of Payment into the required space, fill in the payment details and click "Pay Now" to choose a payment type. To pay in person, bring the Tax Order of Payment to the City Treasurer's Office.

    The office then

    1. If you paid online, Land Bank, DBP staff, GCash and Paymaya process the payment. If you paid at the City Treasurer's Office, the Revenue Collection Officer processes it and issues the official receipt.

      Based on the Tax Order of Payment issued by the CTO under City Ordinance No. 13-2007Land Bank or DBP staff, GCash or Paymaya, Revenue Collection Officers of the CTO

  3. 3Send the e-copy of the feedback form to legpermit2@gmail.com, or hand the accomplished form to the Lane 9 Releasing Officer if you would rather claim the permit at the BPLO. If a representative will claim it, they must bring an authorization letter.

    The office then

    1. Once the City Mayor's Office approves, the Action Officer emails an electronic copy of the business permit after receiving the accomplished feedback form, or tells you to claim it at BPLO Lane 9. The Lane 9 Action Officer releases the permit on submission of the accomplished customer's feedback form and, where a representative is claiming, an authorization letter.

      Licensing Inspector I / Admin. Officer I / Lane 9 Releasing

Total time 3 days Total fee Based on the Tax Order of Payment issued by the CTO under City Ordinance No. 13-2007

Good to know

  • The charter publishes no fee amounts. What you pay is set by the Tax Order of Payment the City Treasurer's Office issues under City Ordinance No. 13-2007.

    1. The Action Officer asks you to sign the logbook and gives you a feedback form to submit when you claim the clearance, then checks that the documents are complete. If they are accurate and complete the clearance is encoded and printed in two copies, and you are advised to pay at the CTO.

    1. The Action Officer asks you to sign and thumbmark the clearance, forwards it to the BPLO for initialling, then releases it with your submitted requirements and tells you to go to the City Mayor's Office for the local chief executive's signature.

      Community Affairs Assistant II

Total time 20 minutes Total fee Based on the Tax Order of Payment issued by the CTO under City Ordinance No. 13-2007

Good to know

  • The twenty minutes covers the BPLO's part only. The clearance is not finished until the City Mayor's Office signs it, and the charter puts no time on that.

Feedback and complaints

Call or text 0915-872-7722, email legpermit2@gmail.com, or fill up the Feedback Form. Clients are given a form for each service they use.

The assigned officer collects the forms at the end of each transaction day and submits them to the Administrative Officer for recording. They are collated quarterly and averaged into the rating clients gave for each service, presented as the Client Satisfaction Measurement Report, then submitted to the City Human Resource Management Office for the Harmonized CSM Report that goes to the Anti-Red Tape Authority. Complaints may be filed in writing or in person: write to the head of office, email legbplo@gmail.com, or call or text 0915-872-7722. The head of office asks the officer concerned to verify the complaint, then acts on it as a light or grave offence depending on what was done, with the City Human Resource Management Office applying sanctions where it is called on to assist. A client who asks for the action in writing is given it.
