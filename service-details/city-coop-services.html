City Cooperative Development Office (CCDO)

Citizen's Charter, 2026 First Edition. Read the source document

What do you need to do?

  • Your group wants to become a cooperative 3 services
    1. Registering a new cooperative
    2. Requesting an orientation
    3. Requesting a Pre-Membership Education Seminar
  • Your cooperative has reports or permits due 2 services
    1. Endorsing a mandatory cooperative report
    2. Endorsement for a cooperative's business permit
  • Your cooperative needs training 2 services
    1. Arranging a mandatory training
    2. Requesting a Pre-Membership Education Seminar

Training 1 service

5 Arranging a mandatory training 1 day & 25 minutes at the counter 8 days & 25 minutes in total 2 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The mandatory training a registered cooperative needs, run with speakers and trainers accredited by the Cooperative Development Authority. The office has to request the schedule from the CDA, which is what makes this the longest service it publishes.

Before you go

Your time
1 day & 25 minutes at the counter, 8 days & 25 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
3 separate visits
Where to go
City Cooperative Development Office
What you pay
Nothing
Office or division
City Cooperative Development Office
Who may avail
Registered cooperatives
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C), Government to Business (G2B), Government to Government (G2G)
Classification
Highly Technical

What to bring 2 items

How it works

  1. 1Submit the request letter.

    The office then

    1. Receives the letter of request.

      5 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  2. 2Consult the CDO Officer.

    The office then

    1. Discusses the details of the training.

      20 minutesCity Cooperative Development Officer

    2. Requests the training schedule from the Cooperative Development Authority.

      7 daysCity Cooperative Development Officer

  3. 3Attend the training.

    The office then

    1. Facilitates the training with CDA-accredited speakers and trainers.

      1 dayCity Cooperative Development Officer / Cooperatives Development Specialist I

Total time 8 days & 25 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • The charter notes that the CDA requires 16 hours of training. That is the requirement the training has to meet, not an extra wait on top of the schedule.
  • Wait for the official notice from the office about your training schedule.

Feedback and complaints

Fill up the CCDO Feedback and Complaint Form and drop it in the box at the receiving desk, or send a direct message to the office's Facebook page. You can also call 742-6136 or 0910 333 9650 or 0917 158 2837, or email.

Completed forms are gathered and encoded every other day and summarised into a client feedback report for the CCDO Officer, who reviews and analyses it and acts on any complaints. The CCDO Officer then updates the client on the action taken.
