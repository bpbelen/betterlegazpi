Tricycle franchises 4 services

3 Renewing a tricycle or taxicle franchise 1 hr. & 10 minutes at the counter 6 days, 1 hr. & 30 minutes in total 12 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Renewing the permit to operate a tricycle-for-hire or taxicle-for-hire, which a vehicle needs to keep carrying passengers in the city. The application goes to a regular session of the Sangguniang Panlungsod, and the certificate is signed by the Vice Mayor.

Before you go

Your time
1 hr. & 10 minutes at the counter, 6 days, 1 hr. & 30 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
4 separate visits
Where to go
Transportation Development and Regulation Division
What you pay
Nothing
Office or division
Transportation Development and Regulation Division
Who may avail
All operators of tricycles-for-hire and taxicles-for-hire renewing their franchise
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C)
Classification
Complex

What to bring 12 items

How it works

  1. 1Submit the required documents at the window.

    The office then

    1. Receives the required documents for evaluation.

      20 minutesSenior Transportation Officer / Admin. Asst. / Admin. Aide

    2. Inspects the tricycle or taxicle.

      20 minutesAuthorized inspector / Admin. Asst.

  2. 2Receive the letter of undertaking.

    The office then

    1. Issues the letter of undertaking and the franchising brochure, in lieu of the two-hour seminar conducted every Tuesday.

      20 minutesSenior Transportation Officer / Admin. Asst. / Admin. Aide

    2. Transmits the applications to the Office of the Vice Mayor for inclusion in the regular session of the Sangguniang Panlungsod.

      3 daysSenior Transportation Officer / Admin. Asst.

    3. Prepares the certificate of franchise once the approved resolution is received, for the Vice Mayor's signature.

      3 daysSenior Transportation Officer / Admin. Asst.

  3. 3Claim the order of payment on presenting the letter of undertaking.

    The office then

    1. Assesses the fees and issues the order of payment.

      15 minutesSenior Transportation Officer / Admin. Asst.

  4. 4Pay the fees. Make sure you get the official receipt issued on payment at the City Treasurer's Office.

    The office then

    1. Releases the certificate of franchise.

      15 minutesSenior Transportation Officer / Admin. Asst.

Total time 6 days, 1 hr. & 30 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • The two-hour Tuesday seminar is replaced by a letter of undertaking and a brochure, so you do not have to attend it.
  • The charter notes that the resolution is received within 20 working days from the date of the session, which is longer than the three days shown against the step.

Office personnel 1

Named in the Citizen's Charter. Any of the staff for a division may serve you, which is why the steps above name the division rather than an individual.

ML Ma. Leticia B. Bello City Secretary
See all 0 staff across 0 divisions

The charter names the City Secretary and the Vice Mayor, who presides over the Sangguniang Panlungsod and signs the franchises. Everyone else is identified by position: Senior Transportation Officer, Administrative Assistant, Administrative Aide, LLSO IV and Legal Aide. This charter covers the Secretariat. The Sangguniang Panlungsod itself, the city council, is a separate body.

Feedback and complaints

Answer the client feedback form and drop it in the box provided. For inquiries and follow-ups, call 836-9033, 0917-770-3307 or 0999-988-9950.

Feedback needing an answer is forwarded to the Vice Mayor. The Administrative Officer opens the complaints drop box daily and evaluates each complaint, then forwards it to the Vice Mayor for investigation. The Vice Mayor takes the necessary action and gives feedback to the client.
