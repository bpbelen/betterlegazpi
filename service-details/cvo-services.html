6 Rabies mass vaccination 32 minutes at the counter 59 minutes in total 3 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The free house-to-house rabies vaccination run in each barangay once its pre-registration is in. Your pet is interviewed for, vaccinated, and the vaccination card filled up on the spot.

Before you go

Your time
32 minutes at the counter, 59 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
3 separate visits
Where to go
City Veterinary Office
What you pay
Nothing
Office or division
City Veterinary Office
Who may avail
Pet owners
Type of transaction
Frontline Service
Classification
Simple

What to bring 3 items

How it works

  1. 1Prepare your animals for vaccination.

    The office then

    1. Orients the barangay officials, barangay nutrition scholars and barangay tanod on the assistance needed for the mass vaccination.

      10-15 minutesAdministrative Aide I

    2. Conducts the house-to-house vaccination.

      Depends upon the distance between pet ownersAdministrative Aide IV

  2. 2Present the vaccination card.

    The office then

    1. Interviews the owner or handler about the animal's current medical condition before vaccinating.

      5-7 minutes per petAdministrative Aide II / Administrative Aide I

    2. Vaccinates the animals.

      2-4 minutes per petJob order workers

    3. Fills up the vaccination cards.

      2-4 minutes per petJob order workers

    4. Prepares and records the vaccination report.

      3-4 minutes per dogAdministrative Aide I

    5. Reviews and signs the pet registration of the vaccinated dogs and cats.

      10-15 minutesCity Veterinarian

  3. 3The barangay receives its file copy.

    The office then

    1. Issues the vaccination summary and the certificate of registration, signed by the veterinarian and the vaccinator, as the barangay's file copy.

      5-10 minutesAdministrative Aide I

Total time 59 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • The vaccination is free and comes to you: the team goes house to house once your barangay is scheduled.

Permits and certificates 1 service

3 Getting a veterinary health certificate to travel with a pet 25 minutes at the counter 38 minutes in total 2 documents P100.00 per head

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The health certificate and shipping permit needed to transport an animal, as required by the Bureau of Animal Industry. If the animal's vaccination is not up to date the office vaccinates it first.

Before you go

Your time
25 minutes at the counter, 38 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
5 separate visits
Where to go
City Veterinary Office
What you pay
P100.00 per head
Office or division
City Veterinary Office
Who may avail
Pet owners
Type of transaction
Frontline Service
Classification
Simple

What to bring 2 items

How it works

  1. 1Fill up the veterinary certificate request form.

    The office then

    1. Interviews you about the date and destination of the animal to be transported.

      3-5 minutesSenior Administrative Assistant II

    2. Verifies the vaccination records against the animal health card or vaccination certificate.

      3-5 minutesClinic Attendant

  2. 2Present the pet or animal for examination.

    The office then

    1. Conducts a physical examination of the animal.

      3-5 minutesCity Veterinarian

  3. 3Go to the City Treasurer's Office to pay.

    The office then

    1. If approved, issues the referral slip for payment at the City Treasurer's Office. If not approved, carries out the applicable vaccination first.

      3-5 minutesP100.00 per headAdministrative Aide I / City Veterinarian

  4. 4Present proof of payment to the clinic attendant.

    The office then

    1. Records the proof of payment issued by the City Treasurer's Office and gathers the client and animal information.

      3-5 minutesAdministrative Aide I / Clinic Attendant

    2. Prepares the shipping permit.

      3-5 minutesSenior Administrative Assistant II

    3. Reviews and signs the shipping permit.

      1-3 minutesCity Veterinarian

  5. 5Sign the receiving logbook or file copy, and receive the veterinary health certificate.

    The office then

    1. Releases the shipping permit, to be received by the requesting party on signing the logbook or file copy.

      3-5 minutesAdministrative Aide I

Total time 38 minutes Total fee P100.00 per head

Good to know

  • If the animal's vaccinations are not current, the office vaccinates it before approving the certificate.

7 Reporting stray dogs and cats 6 days & 1 hr. at the counter 6 days, 1 hr. & 10 minutes in total 2 documents Fee applies

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

How a barangay asks for help catching stray dogs and cats, what happens to the animals afterwards, and how an owner claims one back. Impounded animals are held at the barangay for three days, then moved to the city facility at Banquerohan.

Before you go

Your time
6 days & 1 hr. at the counter, 6 days, 1 hr. & 10 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
8 separate visits
Where to go
City Veterinary Office
What you pay
Settled at the City Treasurer's Office; the charter publishes no amount
Office or division
City Veterinary Office
Who may avail
Barangay Rabies and Control Committee
Type of transaction
Frontline Service
Classification
Simple

What to bring 2 items

How it works

  1. 1Report stray dogs to the barangay officials or the City Veterinary Office.

    The office then

    1. Receives the letter submitted by the requesting barangay.

      3-5 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  2. 2The barangay submits a written request for a dog catching activity.

    The office then

    1. Records the request.

      3-5 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  3. 3The barangay officials fill up the request form at the information desk.

    The office then

    1. Issues the request form or slip.

      3-5 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  4. 4The stray animal activity is carried out.

    The office then

    1. Coordinates with the barangay concerned to provide assistance on their dog catching activity.

      10-15 minutesAdministrative Aide IV and job order workers

  5. 5Impounded animals are temporarily housed at the barangay impounding facility for three days.

  6. 6Citation tickets are issued to the identified owners.

  7. 7To claim an impounded animal, settle the payment at the City Treasurer's Office.

    The office then

    1. Picks up the unclaimed impounded animals to be transferred to the temporary impounding facility in Banquerohan, Legazpi City, and shelters them.

      3 daysSettled at the City Treasurer's Office; the charter publishes no amountAdministrative Aide IV and job order personnel

    2. Unclaimed dogs are rehabilitated with the necessary medication, deworming and vaccination, and put up for adoption.

      5-10 minutesCity Veterinarian / Administrative Aide IV

  8. 8Prospective owners attend an orientation and sign the adoption contract.

    The office then

    1. Runs the orientation and takes the signed adoption contract.

      15-30 minutesCity Veterinarian / Administrative Aide I

    2. Dogs with infectious diseases, or in a condition deemed not rehabilitatable, are humanely euthanized.

      City Veterinarian

Total time 6 days, 1 hr. & 10 minutes Total fee Settled at the City Treasurer's Office; the charter publishes no amount

Good to know

  • If your pet has been impounded, you have three days at the barangay facility before it is moved to Banquerohan. Claiming it means paying at the City Treasurer's Office.
  • Unclaimed animals are rehabilitated and offered for adoption where they can be; those too sick to recover are humanely euthanized.
