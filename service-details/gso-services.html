Checklist of Requirements

Primary Document Requirements
  • Requisition and Issue Slip (RIS) signed by Requesting Department / Office Head (3 copies)
  • Approved Purchase Request (PR) and Purchase Order (PO) for items for issuance
  • Property Acknowledgement Receipt (PAR) for capital equipment & asset transfers
  • Inventory Clearance Certificate for unserviceable property return or transfer

Summary & Information

Total Processing Time
1 Working Day
Fees
FREE (₱0.00)
Internal LGU service provided free of charge
Classification & Type
Simple / G2G
Step Applicant Step Fees Processing Time Person Responsible
1 Submit approved Requisition Slip (RIS) or Property Transfer form at GSO Supply Division. Free 10 minutes Supply Receiving Officer
2 Stock inventory verification and item allocation by GSO Property Custodian. Free 3 hours Property Custodian
3 Inspection and turnover of requested supplies or asset items to requesting office representative. Free 1 hour Warehouse Keeper
4 Signing of Property Acknowledgement Receipt (PAR) and recording in COA asset registry. Free 15 minutes Asset Inventory Officer

Checklist of Requirements

Primary Document Requirements
  • Vehicle Repair Job Order Request signed by Department Head (3 copies)
  • Pre-Repair Inspection Report prepared by GSO Motor Pool Mechanic
  • Trip Ticket logbook copy and driver official assignment memo
  • Requisition for replacement spare parts or lube oil requirement

Summary & Information

Total Processing Time
2 Working Days
Fees
FREE (₱0.00)
Labor provided free for official LGU Legazpi vehicles
Classification & Type
Complex / G2G
Step Applicant Step Fees Processing Time Person Responsible
1 File Job Order Request for vehicle preventive maintenance or repair at GSO Motor Pool Section. Free 15 minutes Motor Pool Receiving Clerk
2 Conduct technical diagnostic inspection and prepare repair cost assessment. Free 3 hours Master Mechanic
3 Execute mechanical, electrical, or body repair work on the city vehicle / heavy equipment. Free 1.5 days Motor Pool Technician Team
4 Post-repair test drive, quality inspection, and release of repaired vehicle to assigned driver. Free 30 minutes Motor Pool Supervisor

Checklist of Requirements

Primary Document Requirements
  • Maintenance Work Order Request form signed by Office Head
  • Details of facility defect (Electrical, Plumbing, Carpentry, Aircon, or Janitorial request)
  • Safety Inspection permit for major electrical or plumbing alterations

Feedback is processed daily by the GSO Admin Section and addressed within 3 working days.
