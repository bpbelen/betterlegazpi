What do you need to do?

  • You want to supply the city 3 services
    1. Filing a letter of intent as a prospective bidder
    2. Asking the Bids and Awards Committee a question
    3. Request for quotation and abstract of canvass
  • You are retiring, resigning or transferring 1 service
    1. Getting a clearance when you leave city employment
  • The Mayor approved your solicitation 1 service
    1. Following up a solicitation approved by the City Mayor
  • Your office needs supplies or equipment 3 services
    1. Filing a purchase request and getting the mode of procurement certified
    2. Preparing a purchase order
    3. Accepting a delivery and issuing the supplies

Starting a procurement 3 services

1 Filing a purchase request and getting the mode of procurement certified 5 minutes at the counter 1 day & 5 minutes in total 3 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The purchase request is prepared by the end-user office for supplies, property or equipment. It is the basis for canvassing before a purchase order is drawn up. Once the City Mayor approves it, the Bids and Awards Committee certifies which mode of procurement applies.

3 Request for quotation and abstract of canvass 1 day at the counter 2 days in total 2 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The request for quotation sets out the specification, quantity, approved budget for the contract and terms for the items being procured, and goes to at least three qualified suppliers, contractors or consultants. The abstract of canvass and award summarises the quotations that come back.

5 Accepting a delivery and issuing the supplies 4 hrs. at the counter 2 days & 4 hrs. in total 4 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Inspection and acceptance of a delivery against the approved purchase order, then issuing the items. A property acknowledgement receipt covers property worth Php 50,000.00 and above; an inventory custodian slip covers supplies or equipment worth Php 14,999.00 or below. Both are signed by the accountable employee.

For city employees 1 service

11 Getting a clearance when you leave city employment 1 hr. & 40 minutes at the counter 3 hrs. & 40 minutes in total 1 document Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The clearance an employee needs when retiring, resigning, transferring to another office, going on official leave, or travelling abroad while still in government service. It clears you of the property and equipment you are accountable for, which has to be transferred, returned or condemned first.

How it works

  1. 1Present the clearance form for retirement, resignation, transfer or leave, and request the transfer, return or condemnation of property, plant and equipment if that applies.

    The office then

      40 minutesAdministrative Officer I / Administrative Aide IV / RMO II, Administrative and Records Divisions

    2. Recommends the next steps to complete the clearance form, prepares the property acknowledgement receipt or inventory custodian slip for a transfer, prepares the form for returning items, prepares the inspection and inventory or report of waste materials for property, plant and equipment, and submits it initialled and dated to the department head for final signature.

Feedback and complaints

Answer the Client Satisfaction Measurement Survey at the front desk of the office, or email gso.legazpi@gmail.com. The office is on the ground floor of the City Hall compound, Rizal Street, Barangay 13 Ilawod West.

Every Friday the Officer-in-Charge compiles and records the feedback submitted. Feedback needing an answer goes to the division concerned, which must reply within two days of receipt, and the office's answer is relayed to the client. Complaints are evaluated by the Officer-in-Charge, forwarded to the division concerned for explanation, written up in a report for the Department Head to act on, and the outcome is given back to the client.
