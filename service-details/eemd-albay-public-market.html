Albay Public Market

Your guide to getting a stall at the Albay Public Market and to the lease contract that goes with it.

Office

EEMD Albay

Location

Albay Public Market

Hours

8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Mon-Fri)

Contact / Hotline

Albay Public Market Office

Economic Enterprise Management Division, Albay Public Market (EEMD Albay)

Citizen's Charter, 2026 First Edition. Read the source document

What do you need to do?

  • You want a stall at the Albay Public Market 1 service
    1. Being awarded a vacant market stall
  • You already hold a stall 1 service
    1. Signing a market stall lease contract

Getting and keeping a stall 2 services

1 Being awarded a vacant market stall 39 minutes at the counter 50 minutes in total 1 document Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

How a vacant stall at the Albay Public Market is awarded, after the vacancy has been posted for thirty days. If only one person applies the stall goes to them; if there is more than one, it is drawn by lot.

How it works

  1. 1Submit a copy of the application letter.

    The office then

    1. Receives the application letter.

      3 minutesAdministrative Aide I / Administrative Aide II

  2. 2Attend the awarding of the stall.

    The office then

    1. Awards the stall to the sole applicant. Where there is more than one applicant, a drawing of lots is conducted.

      5 minutesMarket Supervisor IV

    2. Gives the qualified applicant or awardee the essential forms.

      3 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  3. 3Fill out the stallholder information sheet, the application to lease a market stall and the licence to conduct business, and sign the lease contract.

  4. 4Return the duly filled-out forms.

    The office then

    1. Checks and reviews the forms submitted.

      3 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  5. 5Wait for the release of the market clearance.

    The office then

    1. Signs the market clearance.

      3 minutesMarket Supervisor IV

    2. Releases the market clearance.

      5 minutesAdministrative Aide I

    3. Records and files the duplicate copy of the market clearance.

      3 minutesAdministrative Aide I

Total time 50 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • A stall is only awarded after its vacancy has been posted for thirty days.
2 Signing a market stall lease contract 3 days & 18 minutes at the counter 3 days & 28 minutes in total 2 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The lease contract between a stallholder and the City Government of Legazpi. The contract goes to the City Mayor's Office for signature, which is what makes this take days rather than minutes.

How it works

  1. 1Approach the Officer of the Day.

    The office then

    1. Acknowledges the client.

      3 minutesAdministrative Aide II

  2. 2Give a photocopy of the business permit.

    The office then

    1. Receives and checks the documents.

      5 minutesAdministrative Aide I

    2. Prepares the lease contract.

      10 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  3. 3Sign the lease contract.

    The office then

    1. Forwards the lease contract to the City Mayor's Office for signature.

      10 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  4. 4Wait for the release of the lease contract.

    The office then

    1. Releases the signed lease contract.

      1-3 daysAdministrative Aide I

Total time 3 days & 28 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • Everything but the last step happens at the market office in under half an hour. The wait is the City Mayor's Office signing.

Office personnel 4

The Economic Enterprise Management Division is published under the City Mayor's Office and issues a separate charter for each market it runs. This one covers the Albay Public Market.

Feedback and complaints

Answer the client feedback form and drop it in the box at the Albay Public Market Office.

Issues are discussed so a solution can be reached. A complaint may be filed in writing or verbally at the market office, and the complainant and the respondent appear together at a preliminary conference.

About this information

This page reproduces the Economic Enterprise Management Division, Albay Public Market Citizen's Charter, 2026 First Edition. The published charter is always the authority. If anything here differs from it, follow the charter and please tell us so we can correct the page.

Total processing times are added up from the individual steps. Where a charter states a total that does not match its own steps, the figure shown here is the sum of the steps.
