3 Frontline Agricultural Services

1. Farm Registration

Walk-in • CAO Admin Section

Official farmer registration for government programs, certifications, RSBSA enrollment, crop insurance (PCIC), and motorized boat/banca registration.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Request Form (from the AT Assigned)
  • RSBSA Registration

Types of Registration

  • Certification
  • Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA)
  • Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC) Insurance
  • Boat Registration

3. Production Inputs (Distribution)

Walk-in • CAO Office & Bodega/Nursery

Distribution of certified seeds, fertilizers, fingerlings, and planting stock.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Palay

Inbred & hybrid palay seed distribution for farmers

Seeds / Planting Materials

Quality vegetable seeds & crop planting stock

Fertilizers

Organic & inorganic fertilizers for crop production

Fingerlings

Tilapia & bangus fingerlings for local aquaculture

Mangrove Seedlings

Mangrove planting stock for coastal protection

Checklist of Requirements

  • Request Form (from the AT Assigned)
  • RSBSA Registration
  • Request letter from President of Association/Organization
  • Approved Letter from the Mayor
  • RSBSA ID
