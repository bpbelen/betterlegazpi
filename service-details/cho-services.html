What do you need to do?

  • You need a medical certificate 3 services
    1. Getting a medical certificate
    2. Getting your medical certificate signed
    3. Getting a medical certificate for travel
  • Someone has died 3 services
    1. Getting a death certificate
    2. Getting a burial or transfer permit signed
    3. Getting a transfer permit for a deceased person
  • You are starting work that needs a health card 3 services
    1. Getting a health card issued
    2. Sputum and fecalysis tests for a health card
    3. Registering your health card or yellow card
  • You are pregnant, or have a new baby 4 services
    1. Prenatal and postnatal care
    2. Prenatal and postnatal care at your barangay
    3. Under Five Clinic and childhood immunisation
    4. Nutrition assessment and counselling
  • You are unwell 4 services
    1. Seeing a doctor at the City Health Office
    2. General consultation and nursing services
    3. Care for a cough or difficulty breathing
    4. TB testing and treatment
  • You want a confidential test 3 services
    1. Free HIV, RPR and Hepatitis B screening
    2. Check-up for a sexually transmitted infection
    3. Getting a PAP smear
  • You run a food or water business 4 services
    1. Registering a sanitary permit, health clearance or health card for a business
    2. Permits for a water refilling station
    3. Getting a sanitary clearance for a delivery vehicle
    4. Requesting water sampling

Feedback and complaints

Fill in a feedback form at the office, or call the division contact directly. Each division publishes its own number alongside the City Health Officer's.

The office asks short questions, considers the client's experience, acts on what it hears and shares the feedback with the whole office. Complaints are filed on Form 3 from the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk, dropped in the PACD box, and answered by getting the facts, offering a solution and, where warranted, disciplining the employee concerned.
