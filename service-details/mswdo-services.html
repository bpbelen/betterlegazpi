1. Limited Financial Assistance / Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)

Limited cash extended to clients in crisis due to death, illness, calamity, and other disasters. Includes assistance for transportation, education, burial, medical, and livelihood.

Checklist of Requirements

General Requirements (All Applicants)
  • Proof of Identity PhilHealth / GSIS / SSS / LTO / PSA / Barangay
    Valid ID with photo & birthdate (Voter's, UMID, Driver's, 4Ps, Barangay, National ID) — Original for validation
  • Barangay Certification Barangay Hall
    Certificate of Indigency / Residency — Original document
  • CSWDO Form CSWDO
    Certificate of Eligibility (CE) Form
  • Authorization Letter Client Representative
    Signed letter & ID — Required if representative will claim cash on behalf of client
Assistance-Specific Documents (As Applicable)
  • Medical Assistance Hospital / Attending Physician
    Medical Certificate, Abstract, Prescription, Quotation, Lab Order, or Hospital Bill — Photocopy & original
  • Educational Assistance School Registrar
    Certificate of Enrolment — Photocopy & original
  • Burial Assistance Local Civil Registrar / PSA
    Death Certificate — Original document

4. Issuance of Solo Parent ID (RA 11861 Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act)

Application, orientation, background verification, and issuance of Solo Parent Identification Card and benefits under Republic Act 11861 across 13 recognized categories of solo parents.

Checklist of Requirements

General Requirements (All Categories)
  • Proof of Identity PhilHealth / GSIS / SSS / LTO / PSA / Barangay
    Valid ID with photo & birthdate — Original document
  • Child's Birth Certificate PSA / LCR
    Birth certificate of child or children
  • Barangay Certification Barangay Hall
    Certificate of Residency
  • Photos Photo Center
    2 pcs 1x1 identical pictures
  • Sworn Affidavit PAO / Private Attorney
    Affidavit declaring non-cohabitation & sole custody
  • Tax Document BIR
    Income Tax Return (working) or Tax Exemption (non-working)
Category-Specific Requirements (13 Categories under RA 11861)
Category 1: Rape Victim

Complaint Affidavit (PNP/NBI) & Medical record on rape incident.

Category 2: Deceased Spouse

Death Certificate of spouse (PSA/LCR) & Marriage Certificate.

Category 3: Detained Spouse

Certificate of detention serving 3+ months sentence (BJMP) & Affidavit of Cohabitation.

Category 4: Incapacitated Spouse

Medical abstract of physical/mental incapacity within 3 months (Doctor) & Affidavit of Cohabitation.

Category 5: Legal Separation / De Facto

Judicial decree of legal separation or Affidavit of 2 disinterested persons attesting 6+ months separation.

Category 6: Annulment / Nullity

Judicial decree of nullity or annulment of marriage / foreign divorce recognition with sole custody.

Category 7: Abandoned Spouse

Marriage cert, Affidavit of 2 disinterested persons attesting abandonment & Police/Barangay record.

Categories 8 & 9: OFW Spouse

Overseas Employment Certificate (OEC), Passport stamps showing 12+ months continuous overseas work, Employment contract (OWWA).

Category 10: Unmarried Parent

Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR from PSA) & Barangay Affidavit attesting residency & sole custody.

Category 11: Foster Parent / Guardian

Proof of legal guardianship, foster care, or adoption (DSWD / Court).

Category 12: Parent Death / Absence

Death cert of parents/guardians, or Police/Barangay record of disappearance for 6+ months (PSA/LCR/Barangay).

Category 13: Pregnant Woman

Medical record of pregnancy (OB / Doctor/Specialist).

5. Assistance to Persons with Disability (PWD) & PDAO Services

Services, PWD ID issuance, 20% discount & VAT exemption privileges, and welfare assistance for persons with disabilities in Legazpi City.

Requirements for PWD ID

  • Barangay Certification Barangay Hall
    Certificate of Residency
  • Medical Certificate Attending Physician / Clinic
    Indicating specific type of disability
  • Photos Photo Center
    1x1 ID photo (2 copies)
  • Proof of Identity Government Agency
    Valid ID of applicant or guardian
  • Birth Certificate PSA / LCR
    Birth Certificate photocopy

7. Women's & Children's Protection Services

Programs and immediate protective services for women and children's welfare, VAWC rescue, child abuse intervention, Day Care services, and supplemental feeding.

Services & Interventions Available

  • VAWC Assistance CSWDO / PNP WCPD
    Immediate rescue, counseling, & temporary shelter
  • Child Abuse & Neglect CSWDO / DSWD
    Protective intervention & custody
  • CICL Diversion CSWDO / PAO
    Children in Conflict with the Law diversion & legal aid
  • Day Care Services Barangay Day Care
    Barangay Child Development Centers
  • Supplemental Feeding CSWDO / City Health
    Nutritional feeding program for undernourished children
  • Legal Referral PAO / IBP
    Legal aid referral
