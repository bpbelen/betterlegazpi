Transfer of Real Property Ownership

Face-to-Face • Assessor Frontline

Processing of transfer of Tax Declaration for real property upon change of ownership through sale, donation, extrajudicial settlement, or inheritance.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Letter Request / Form: 1 original letter request or duly accomplished request form (from City Assessor's Office).
  • Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT): 2 certified true copies of TCT (from Registry of Deeds).
  • Deed of Conveyance: 2 original duplicate copies of Deed of Sale, Deed of Extra Judicial Settlement of Estate with Publication, or Deed of Donation.
  • Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR): 2 photocopies of CAR (from Bureau of Internal Revenue).
  • Previous Tax Declaration: Original Owner's Copy or Certified True Copy of Tax Declaration.
  • Tax Clearance: 1 photocopy of current year Tax Clearance (from City Treasurer's Office).
  • Official Receipts: 1 photocopy of Official Receipt of Transfer Tax & Processing Fee, and 2 photocopies of Receipt of Registration with original copy for authentication.
  • IF REPRESENTATIVE Authorization & ID: 1 photocopy of valid ID of representative and Special Power of Attorney (SPA) or Authorization Letter.

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
2.8 Hours
Standard Fees
₱100.00
Mode of Transaction
Frontline Desk (Intake & Releasing) • Walk-in
Step Client Step Agency Action Fees Officer
1 Submit Request & Complete Requirements
Submit accomplished request form together with TCT, Deed of Conveyance, CAR, previous TD, and Tax Clearance to Frontline Desk. 		Frontline Officer checks completeness of documents and forwards docket to the City Assessor. — Frontline Officer
2 Pay Processing Fee & Await Assessment Processing
Pay ₱100 fee at City Treasurer's Office and submit Official Receipt to City Assessor's Office. 		City Assessor approves request; Appraisal Division evaluates requirements and prepares FAAS; Asst. City Assessor reviews/signs FAAS; Tax Mapping assigns PIN; ETRACS encodes and prints new TD; Records Division cancels previous TD; City Assessor signs new TD. ₱100.00 Appraisal & Records Division
3 Claim New Tax Declaration
Present transaction receipt or claim stub at Frontline Releasing Desk. 		Frontline Officer segregates docket files, records release in the logbook, and releases official new Tax Declaration to client. — Frontline Officer
