What do you need to do?

  • You bought or inherited property 3 services
    1. Transferring real property to a new owner
    2. Getting a certified true copy of a tax declaration
    3. Tracing the history of a property record
  • You finished building or renovating 2 services
    1. Declaring a newly built house, building or machine
    2. Having a building re-assessed
  • You are splitting or merging a lot 2 services
    1. Splitting, merging or subdividing a lot
    2. Seeing where a lot sits on the tax map
  • You are taking out or paying off a loan 2 services
    1. Annotating a mortgage or claim on a tax declaration
    2. Removing a mortgage annotation once a loan is paid
  • Something on your tax declaration is wrong 2 services
    1. Correcting an error on a tax declaration
    2. Re-classifying a property
  • Someone asked you to prove what property you own 2 services
    1. Getting a landholding, no improvement or no property certification
    2. Getting a certified true copy of a tax declaration

Feedback and complaints

Answer the Feedback Form at the City Assessor's Office frontline desk and drop it in the box, or hand it to the Officer of the Day. You can also call 742-6882 or 0966-812-0533.

The Administrative Division checks what the feedback is about within one working day and passes it to the division concerned. Once that division replies, the office contacts you by email, text or phone. Complaints go on the Complaint Form (CSC Form No. 3) or by email, and need your full name, address and contact details, an account of what happened, any evidence, and the name of the person being complained about. The Administrative Division reads complaints daily, coordinates with the division concerned, investigates where needed, then writes an Incident Report for the Legal Office. The Legal Office sends its findings to the City Mayor, copy to the City Assessor's Office, for final action.
