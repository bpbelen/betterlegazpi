Important Registration & Fee Schedule

Registration of Certificate of Death within thirty (30) days is mandatory and 100% FREE OF CHARGE pursuant to Section 6 of P.D. No. 651. For burial permits and logistics, the following statutory city fees apply: Burial Permit: ₱30.00 • Issuance/Certification: ₱30.00 (Total local burial: ₱60.00) • Transfer of Cadaver Fee: ₱60.00 (if buried outside Legazpi City, Total: ₱120.00). Delayed registration fee beyond 30 days is ₱500.00.

Requirements Checklist

Required documents for death registration and permits

For Death in Hospital / Clinic (Within 30 Days)

  • 4 original copies of Certificate of Death duly signed by the attending physician
  • Certification of Embalmer signed by the licensed embalmer
  • Valid Government-Issued ID of the Informant / nearest kin
  • Burial Permit (₱30) + Issuance (₱30) = ₱60.00 (plus ₱60 if transfer outside Legazpi)

For Death at Home (Unattended by Physician)

  • Barangay Certificate of Death signed by the Punong Barangay
  • Certification of Cause of Death from the City Health Office (CHO)
  • Certification of Embalmer
  • Valid ID of Informant & accurate personal data of the deceased

For Delayed Registration (Beyond 30 Days) — ₱560.00 / ₱620.00

  • Accomplished Affidavit for Delayed Registration of Death at the back of the COD
  • PSA Negative Certification of Death (CRS Form No. 2)
  • Hospital records, Cemetery Burial Certificate, or Proof of Death
  • Delayed registration fee of ₱500.00 + Burial/Issuance fees

For Certified True Copy (CTC / Local Copy) — ₱70.00

  • Accomplished Verification Form at CCRO counter
  • Valid Government ID of requesting kin (or Authorization Letter if representative)
  • Official Receipt of ₱70.00 paid at Cashier (released in ~10 minutes)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is registration of death free in Legazpi City?
Yes, registering the death certificate itself within 30 days is 100% free under Presidential Decree No. 651. The only fees paid are statutory city fees for the Burial Permit (₱30.00), Issuance (₱30.00), and Transfer Fee (₱60.00 if remains are buried outside Legazpi City).
What is the difference between a Burial Permit and a Transfer Permit?
A Burial Permit (₱30.00) is required for interments within any public or private cemetery inside Legazpi City. A Transfer of Cadaver Permit (₱60.00) is required by sanitation and health laws whenever the body is transported outside Legazpi City for burial in another municipality, city, or province.
What is the deadline for registering a death certificate?
The Certificate of Death must be registered within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of death. After 30 days, it is classified as Delayed Registration, requiring a PSA Negative Certification and a ₱500.00 delayed registration fee.
