2 Asking for GAD information or a referral 25 days & 3 minutes at the counter 25 days & 16 minutes in total 3 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Information on gender and development concerns, and referral to the agency or department that can help where the matter is beyond the division's mandate. How long the information takes depends on whether it is already to hand.

What to bring 3 items

How it works

  1. 1Submit your written request.

    The office then

    1. Receives and records the written request.

      3 minutesGAD Staff

    2. Refers the concern to the GAD Focal Person for clarification and proper action.

      3 minutesGAD Staff / GAD Focal Person

    3. Advises you on the appropriate action to take and refers you to the agency or institution with the mandate or competence to give the assistance you need.

      10 minutesGAD Focal Person

  2. 2Wait for the information.

    The office then

    1. Releases the information. A simple transaction is GAD-related information or data already readily available; a complex one is information not readily available, needing review and approval by the appropriate authorities before release; a highly technical one needs further research, consultation with other stakeholders, processing of raw tables from available databases, or review and approval by the GAD Focal Point System.

      3 days for a simple transaction, 7 days for a complex one, 15 days for a highly technical oneGAD Staff / GAD Focal Point System

Total time 25 days & 16 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • How long you wait depends on the information, not on you: three days if the division already holds it, up to fifteen if it has to be researched or cleared first.
  • The charter gives the service's availability as eight hours and its email as gadlegazpihb1@gmail.com.

Training 5 services

5 Requesting a GAD training in writing 4 days & 2 minutes at the counter 8 days & 32 minutes in total 1 document Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The full route for a barangay, department, national agency, private institution or LGU asking for a training to be run. It goes to the GAD Focal Person, then the Local Chief Executive, then a resource speaker, and ends in the training itself with an evaluation and documentation report.

Before you go

Your time
4 days & 2 minutes at the counter, 8 days & 32 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
3 separate visits
Where to go
GAD Division
What you pay
Nothing
Office or division
GAD Division
Who may avail
Barangays, departments, national agencies, private institutions, LGUs and other clients
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C), Government to Non-Government (G2N)
Classification
Simple Transaction

What to bring 1 item

How it works

  1. 1Submit the letter of training request.

    The office then

    1. Receives and records the letter request and forwards it to the GAD Focal Person for approval.

      2 minutesGAD Staff

    2. The GAD Focal Person reviews the request and recommends approval to the Local Chief Executive.

      2 daysGAD Focal Person / Local Chief Executive

    3. Coordinates with the City Human Resource Management Office for the office order covering the personnel attending, approved by the Local Chief Executive.

      2 daysGAD Staff / CHRMO / Local Chief Executive

  2. 2Discuss the training needs, profile and preliminary requirements, and confirm the dates and venue.

    The office then

    1. Coordinates with the GAD resource speaker and discusses with you the training needs, profile and preliminary requirements, the programme, the materials needed, and the confirmation of dates, venues and other arrangements.

      2 daysGAD Staff / GAD Focal Person / Secretariat / Resource Speaker

    2. Prepares the letter to the GAD resource person, reviewed by the GAD Focal Person and approved by the Local Chief Executive.

      30 minutesGAD Staff / GAD Focal Person / Local Chief Executive

  3. 3Attend the training.

    The office then

    1. The resource speaker and training team conduct the training, with an evaluation and documentation report.

      2 daysGAD Resource Speaker / GAD Training Team

Total time 8 days & 32 minutes Total fee Free

The charter names only the Officer-in-Charge. Everyone else appears by role: GAD Staff, the GAD Focal Person, the GAD Focal Point System, and the GAD Technical Working Group of the office concerned. The division is published under the City Mayor's Office.

Feedback and complaints

Write to the office or email gadlegazpihb1@gmail.com. You can also call 0912 158 3909.

To complain, write on the Complaint Form (CSC Form No. 3) or email, giving your full name, address and contact information, an account of the complaint, any evidence, and the name of the person being complained about. Complaints are processed and acted on, and the outcome is communicated to the complainant by email or by phone.
