Checklist of Requirements

Select Permit Sub-Category
Building Permit Application
  • Four (4) copies of duly accomplished and notarized Application Form for Building Permit
  • Proof of lot/property ownership (certified true copy of OCT/TCT; Deed of Sale, Lease Contract, etc.)
  • Geodetic Engineer Certification & Four (4) sets of Survey/Design Plans signed and sealed
  • Structural Analysis (3 sets), Electrical Analysis (3 sets), and Bill of Materials (4 sets)
Certificate of Occupancy Application
  • Three (3) copies of Application Form & Certificate of Completion signed by Engineer/Architect
  • Three (3) sets of As-Built Plans & Geodetic Engineer Certification
  • Construction Logbook with photographs & Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC)
Electrical/Temporary Wiring Permit & CFEI/CEI
  • Letter of intent & Four (4) copies of Electrical Permit Application Form
  • Electrical Design Plan signed and sealed by PEE & Proof of lot ownership
  • Location Sketch, Structure Photographs, and Barangay Clearance
Mechanical Permit Application
  • Letter of intent & Four (4) copies of Mechanical Permit Application Form
  • Five (5) sets of Mechanical Design Plans, Specs & Bill of Materials signed by PME
  • Design Computation (elevators), Heat Load Calculation (HVAC), Hydraulic Calculation (sprinklers)
Mechanical Permit to Operate Application
  • Approved Mechanical Permit & Approved Mechanical Plans
  • Two (2) copies of PME Safety Certification under RA 8495
  • Two (2) copies of Test Results (Weight/Trip, Hydro, Emission, Pneumatic, Nitrogen)
Demolition Permit Application
  • Letter of intent & Four (4) copies of notarized Demolition Permit Application
  • Five (5) sets of Demolition Plans signed by Architect/Civil Engineer
  • Geodetic Engineer Certification, Proof of ownership, Building pictures & GSO Clearance
Excavation and Ground Preparation Permit Application
  • Letter of intent & Four (4) copies of Excavation Permit Application Form
  • Five (5) sets of Building Plans & Structural Analysis signed by Civil/Structural Engineer
  • Geodetic Certification, Construction Logbook & CPDO Zoning Clearance
Temporary Sidewalk Enclosure and Scaffolding Permit Application
  • Letter of intent & Four (4) copies of Sidewalk Enclosure / Scaffolding Application
  • Five (5) sets of Building Plans signed by Architect/Civil Engineer
  • DPWH Clearance (if along national road)
Excavation Permit Application (Maintenance Division)
  • Letter of Intent/Request & Three (3) copies of Excavation Permit Application
  • Three (3) sets of Estimated Cost, Construction Schedule (PERT-CPM), & Street Plans
  • Traffic Re-routing Maps & As-Built Plans
