23 Getting a building permit 45 minutes at the counter 20 days & 13 hrs. in total 16 documents Fee applies

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The permit the Building Official issues before you may build, install, add to, alter, renovate, convert, repair, move or demolish a structure. It is applied for at the One-Stop Shop for Construction Permits, which handles the zoning and fire safety clearances alongside it.

How it works

  1. 1Submit the application form and the documentary requirements at Window B, the receiving window.

    The office then

    1. Receives the application form and the documentary requirements.

      15 minutesReceiving Clerk

    2. Reviews the application form and the documentary requirements.

      1 hourDocument Verifier

    3. If compliant, issues the claim stub. If not, issues the checklist of deficiencies.

      15 minutesReceiving Clerk

    4. Zoning review and evaluation.

      4 hoursZoning Evaluator / Zoning Officer / Zoning Administrator

    5. If non-compliant, issues the notice of deficiencies.

      30 minutesZoning Evaluator / Zoning Officer / Zoning Administrator

    6. Technical review and evaluation.

      5 days (for complex applications), 15 days (for highly-technical applications)Bureau of Fire / Trade Evaluators / Building Permit Division Chief / Fire Marshall / Building Official

    7. If non-compliant, issues the notice of deficiencies.

      30 minutesBuilding Permit Division Chief / Building Official

    8. Prepares the consolidated order of payment.

      1 hourAssessor

    9. Reviews the consolidated order of payment.

      1 hourBuilding Permit Division Chief

    10. Approves the consolidated order of payment.

      1 hourBuilding Official

  2. 2Receive the order of payment by SMS and email, then pay the fees and charges at Window A, the cashier, or online.

    The office then

    1. Receives the payment.

      15 minutesBased on the order of paymentCashier

    2. Prepares the permits and clearances for issuance.

      1 hourClerk

    3. Recommends issuance of the building permit.

      1 hourBuilding Permit Division Chief

    4. Approves issuance of the building permit.

      1 hourBuilding Official

  3. 3Receive the building permit, locational clearance and fire safety evaluation clearance at Window C, the releasing window.

    The office then

    1. Releases the building permit, locational clearance and fire safety evaluation clearance.

      15 minutesReleasing Clerk

Total time 20 days & 13 hrs. Total fee Based on the order of payment

Good to know

  • Only complete and compliant applications are accepted.
  • Under JMC 2018-01 a simple structure is one with a maximum floor area of 1,500 sq. m., covering a single dwelling of not more than three storeys, a commercial building of not more than two storeys, a renovation within a mall that already has a building permit, and a warehouse storing non-hazardous substances.
  • Plans must be A3 (297 mm x 420 mm) or standard size for residential buildings, and standard size (20 x 30 inches or 30 x 40 inches) for commercial and other building types, drawn to a scale of not less than 1:100 m with readable, complete labels, dimensions and specifications.

Feedback and complaints

Text, email or call the City Engineer's Office on 0917-134-4453 or 0968-243-4819. You can also fill up and sign the Client's Feedback Form near the suggestion box at the Public Assistance and Complaints Desk and drop it in, or write to the City Engineer directly.

The office reviews feedback for areas needing corrective measures and looks at it from other perspectives for possible improvement. Complaints are retrieved, classified and summarised, analysed and acted on, and the client is notified of the action taken by letter. A complaint outside the office's authority is endorsed to the head of office.
