Locational clearances 3 services

1 Locational clearance for a new business permit 1 day at the counter Done in one visit 7 documents Fee applies

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The zoning clearance a new business needs before its permit is issued, confirming the business is allowed at that location under the City Zoning Ordinance. You do not collect it separately: it comes with the Mayor's Permit at the Business Center.

Before you go

Your time
1 day, and you leave with it
Trips to the office
3 separate visits
Where to go
City Planning and Development Office, Zoning Division
What you pay
Zoning fee, included in the one-time assessment of fees issued by BPLO, under City Ordinance No. 13-2007 and City Zoning Ordinance No. 14-0011-2019
Office or division
City Planning and Development Office, Zoning Division
Who may avail
Those applying for a locational clearance for a new business permit
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C)
Classification
Simple

What to bring 7 items

How it works

  1. 1Send an e-file copy of the documentary requirements to legazpicpdo@gmail.com, or bring them to the CPDO.

    The office then

    1. Zoning Division staff acknowledge the application forwarded by the Business Center. If it is incomplete or has deficiencies, they email you a notice of deficiency and you resubmit for re-evaluation. If it is complete, they issue the zoning fee or order of payment for BPLO's reference and process the application for the Zoning Officer's and Zoning Administrator's signature. If the application does not conform with the City Zoning Ordinance, they prepare a notice of denial for the Zoning Administrator's signature.

      1 dayZoning Inspector I / Zoning Officers I, II and III / Zoning Administrator

  2. 2Pay the zoning fee, which is included in the one-time assessment of fees BPLO issues, at the City Treasurer's Office or through Legazpi City's online services.

    The office then

    1. Zoning Division staff prepare the transmittal and send the approved locational clearances to the Business Center.

      Zoning fee, included in the one-time assessment of fees issued by BPLO, under City Ordinance No. 13-2007 and City Zoning Ordinance No. 14-0011-2019Revenue Collection Officer, City Treasurer's Office

  3. 3Receive the locational clearance when the Mayor's or business permit is released at the Business Center.

    The office then

    1. Releasing of documents.

      Releasing Officer, Business Permits and Licensing Office

Total time 1 day Total fee Zoning fee, included in the one-time assessment of fees issued by BPLO, under City Ordinance No. 13-2007 and City Zoning Ordinance No. 14-0011-2019

Good to know

  • This clearance is bundled with the business permit. The zoning fee is part of BPLO's one-time assessment, not a separate payment.

Land development and housing 3 services

8 Preliminary approval and locational clearance for a subdivision 33 days at the counter Done in one visit 10 documents From Php 360.00

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

The approval a lot owner needs before developing or subdividing land in the city. It runs through the City Legal Office, the City Engineer's Office, an ocular inspection, the City Mayor and a Sangguniang Panlungsod hearing, and ends with an approved subdivision plan.

Before you go

Your time
33 days, and you leave with it
Trips to the office
3 separate visits
Where to go
City Planning and Development Office, Housing and Homesite Regulation Division
What you pay
Processing fee Php 360.00 per hectare (PD 957), Php 90.00 per hectare (BP 220 socialized), Php 216.00 per hectare (BP 220 economic); inspection fee Php 1,500.00 per hectare
Office or division
City Planning and Development Office, Housing and Homesite Regulation Division
Who may avail
Any lot owner within the City of Legazpi with the intention to develop the lot or land
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C)
Classification
Highly Technical Transaction

What to bring 10 items

How it works

  1. 1Submit the required documents to CPDO-HHRD.

    The office then

    1. If the application meets all the requirements it is officially received. If it is incomplete or has deficiencies, HHRD staff issue a notice of deficiency and re-evaluate what you resubmit. HHRD then endorses the application to the City Legal Office and the City Engineer's Office for comments and recommendations; if it comes back with findings, the deficiencies are sent to you for compliance. HHRD conducts an ocular inspection, prepares an inspection report, endorses findings to you if anything needs clarifying, then prepares an endorsement letter to the City Mayor's Office for endorsement to the Sangguniang Panlungsod. HHRD staff attend the SP committee hearing and recommend approval. On receiving the SP resolution approving the application, HHRD issues the order of payment and prepares the marking of the plan for the signature of the CPDC and the City Mayor. The City Mayor's Office endorses the approved PALC to CPDO and HHRD tells you it is ready to claim.

      33 daysCity Mayor / CPDC / HHRO V / HHRO II / Draftswoman I

  2. 2Pay the processing fee and the inspection fee at the City Treasurer's Office, then return to HHRD so the receipts can be validated and recorded.

    The office then

    1. The CTO Collector receives the payment and issues an official receipt, then tells you to go to CPDO-HHRD for validation.

      Processing fee Php 360.00 per hectare (PD 957), Php 90.00 per hectare (BP 220 socialized), Php 216.00 per hectare (BP 220 economic); inspection fee Php 1,500.00 per hectareCTO Revenue Collection Officer

  3. 3Go to the CPDO and hand in the accomplished feedback form. You receive a copy of the certification approving the simple subdivision signed by the Mayor, and one set of the approved subdivision plan.

    The office then

    1. CPDO-HHRD releases the approved PALC plan with the City Mayor's certification approving it.

      Draftswoman I / HHRO II

Total time 33 days Total fee Processing fee Php 360.00 per hectare (PD 957), Php 90.00 per hectare (BP 220 socialized), Php 216.00 per hectare (BP 220 economic); inspection fee Php 1,500.00 per hectare

Good to know

  • The processing fee depends on which subdivision standard the project falls under, and both fees are charged per hectare, so the total depends on the size of the land.

Feedback and complaints

Call 742-0821, email legazpicpdo@gmail.com, or fill up the Feedback Form. Clients are given a form for each service they use. The office is on the 2nd floor of the City Hall building.

The assigned officer collects the forms at the end of the transaction day and submits them to the Administrative Officer for recording. They are collated quarterly and averaged into the rating clients gave for each service, presented as the Client Satisfaction Measurement Report, then submitted to the City Human Resource Management Office for the Harmonized CSM Report that goes to the Anti-Red Tape Authority. Complaints may be filed in writing or in person: write to the City Planning and Development Coordinator, email jocelyncodorniz29@gmail.com, or call or text 0919-841-3056. The head of office asks the officer concerned to verify the complaint, then acts on it as a light or grave offence depending on what was done, with the City Human Resource Management Office applying sanctions where it assists. A client who asks for the action in writing is given it.
