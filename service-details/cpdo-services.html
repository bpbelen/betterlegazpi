Checklist of Requirements

Primary Document Requirements
  • Duly accomplished Application Form for Locational Clearance (3 copies)
  • Architectural Site Development Plan showing site boundaries and setbacks (3 sets, signed & sealed)
  • Certified True Copy of Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) or Proof of Ownership / Authorization
  • Barangay Clearance for Locational / Zoning Clearance purposes
  • Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) or CNC from DENR-EMB (if commercial / industrial project)
  • Bill of Materials and Project Description Summary

Summary & Information

Total Processing Time
2 Working Days
Fees
Assessed Fee
Computed based on project classification and floor area per Zoning Ordinance
Classification & Type
Complex / G2C, G2B
Step Applicant Step Fees Processing Time Person Responsible
1 Submit application for Locational Clearance together with site plans and title documents at CPDO Receiving Desk. Free 15 minutes Receiving Staff
2 Zoning evaluation and site inspection to verify land use compliance against the Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP). Free 1 working day Zoning Evaluator
3 Pay assessed locational clearance fee at the City Treasurer's Office and present official receipt. Assessed Fee 20 minutes City Treasurer Teller
4 Approval and issuance of signed Locational / Zoning Clearance by the City Planning & Development Coordinator. Free 30 minutes CPDO Coordinator

Checklist of Requirements

Primary Document Requirements
  • Application Form for Development Permit under BP 220 / PD 957 (5 copies)
  • Approved Subdivision Plan and Topographic Map signed by Geodetic Engineer & Civil Engineer
  • Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) from DENR-EMB
  • DAR Clearance / Land Conversion Order (if agricultural land conversion)
  • Water Supply Compatibility Clearance and Electric Power Connection Commitment
  • Traffic Impact Assessment (TIA) for major subdivision or commercial estate developments

  • Govt-issued ID of Requestor / Owner Authorization Letter if representative
