On-Time Registration of Certificate of Live Birth

Walk-in (Frontline Counter)

Declaration of facts and circumstances surrounding the birth of a child born in Legazpi City, to be registered within thirty (30) days from the date of birth.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Application Form: 4 copies of duly accomplished Certificate of Live Birth (prepared by hospital, lying-in clinic, or birth attendant).
  • MARITAL CHILD Marriage Certificate of Parents: 1 original or certified true copy from PSA or LCRO.
  • NON-MARITAL CHILD Affidavit of Paternity / AUSF: Duly notarized Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father & Admission of Paternity.
  • Hospital Information Sheet: 1 original copy from the hospital or lying-in clinic.
  • Valid Government-Issued ID: 1 photocopy of valid ID of parents / informant with signature.
Turnaround Time
2 Hours (Maximum)
Standard Fees
Free (Marital) / ₱300.00 (Non-Marital AAP/AUSF)
Mode of Transaction
Frontline Receiving Counter (Window 1)

Proceed to the City Treasurer's Office (CTO) Cashier for payment (if non-marital child requiring AAP/AUSF). 		Accepts payment and issues Official Receipt (OR). ₱300.00

Delayed Registration of Certificate of Live Birth

Walk-in (Frontline Counter)

Registration of birth reported after the 30-day reglementary period or for individuals with no existing record in LCRO and PSA. Includes Out-of-Town Delayed Registration and BRAP assistance.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Certificate of Live Birth (COLB): 4 original copies with Affidavit of Delayed Registration on reverse side.
  • PSA Negative Certification: 1 original copy & 1 photocopy certifying no prior birth record exists in PSA.
  • Two (2) Documentary Evidences: Certified copies (e.g. Baptismal Certificate, School Form 137, Voter's Certification, SSS/PhilHealth record).
  • Affidavit of 2 Disinterested Persons: 1 original Joint Affidavit with valid IDs of affiants.
  • Barangay Residency & 2x2 Photo: 1 original Barangay Certification and 2x2 picture of applicant on white background.
  • BRAP APPLICANTS Certificate of Indigency: 1 original copy from Punong Barangay (services 100% Free under BRAP).
Turnaround Time
10 Days Posting + 3 Hours
Standard Fees
₱500.00 (Marital) / ₱800.00 (Non-Marital) / Free (BRAP)
Mode of Transaction
Frontline Receiving Counter • Posting Board
STEP CLIENT STEP AGENCY ACTION FEES OFFICER
1 Submit Application & Interview
Submit accomplished COLB with supporting documentary evidence for legal evaluation. 		Interviews applicant, assesses authenticity of supporting evidence, and prepares 10-day notice of posting. — Registration Officer II
2 Pay Delayed Registration Fee
Pay late filing fee at the City Treasurer's Office (waived for BRAP beneficiaries). 		Receives payment and issues Official Receipt. ₱500.00 / ₱800.00
Free for BRAP 		CTO Cashier
3 Mandatory 10-Day Posting Period
Wait for the statutory 10-calendar-day public posting period. 		Posts Notice of Delayed Registration on the official public bulletin board as required by Act No. 3753. — Registration Staff
4 Claim Registered Birth Certificate
Return after completion of posting period and claim the registered Certificate of Live Birth. 		Assigns local registry number, enters record into register, and releases official registered copy. — City Civil Registrar

Legitimation of Child by Subsequent Marriage of Parents

Walk-in (Legal Instruments Division)

Registration of legitimation for children conceived and born outside of wedlock to parents without legal impediment at the time of conception, who subsequently contracted a valid marriage.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Affidavit of Legitimation: 4 original notarized copies executed by both biological parents.
  • Certificate of Live Birth (COLB): 1 original PSA copy / Local Civil Registry copy of child.
  • Certificate of Marriage of Parents: 1 original PSA copy / certified local copy.
  • PSA Advisory on Marriages (CENOMAR): 1 original copy for each parent issued by PSA.
  • Valid IDs of Both Parents: 1 photocopy of government-issued IDs with signature.
Turnaround Time
1 Day (24 Hours)
Standard Fees
₱500.00 (Legitimation & Annotation Fee)
Mode of Transaction
Legal Instruments Division (Ground Floor)
STEP CLIENT STEP AGENCY ACTION FEES OFFICER
1 Submit Legitimation Documents
Submit notarized Affidavit of Legitimation and civil registry certificates. 		Examines legal capacity, verifies authenticity of marriage, and prepares order of payment. — Supervising Admin Officer (SAO)
2 Pay Legitimation Fees
Pay registration and annotation fees at the City Treasurer's Office. 		Receives payment and issues Official Receipt. ₱500.00 CTO Cashier
3 Claim Annotated Birth Certificate
Present Official Receipt and claim the Certificate of Live Birth with approved legitimation annotation. 		Enters legitimation into registry books, stamps official annotation on COLB, and releases registered documents. — City Civil Registrar

Application for Marriage License

Walk-in (Marriage Division)

Filing and issuance of Marriage License required for couples intending to marry in Legazpi City, with mandatory 10-day public publication period.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • PSA Birth Certificate: 1 original PSA copy for each applicant.
  • PSA Certificate of No Marriage (CENOMAR): 1 original copy issued within the last 6 months.
  • PMOC Certificate: 1 original Pre-Marriage Orientation and Counseling Certificate from City Health/Population Office.
  • Barangay Certificate of Residency: 1 original certification from the barangay of applicant residence.
  • AGE 18-21 Parental Consent: Duly notarized consent of parents.
  • AGE 22-25 Parental Advice: Duly notarized written parental advice.
  • FOREIGN CITIZEN Certificate of Legal Capacity: Issued by applicant's Embassy in the Philippines.
Turnaround Time
10 Days Posting + 3 Hours
Standard Fees
₱350.00 (License & Application Fee)
Mode of Transaction
Marriage Division (Ground Floor)
STEP CLIENT STEP AGENCY ACTION FEES OFFICER
1 Submit Application & Pre-Marriage Interview
Both applicants appear in person, fill out application form, and submit requirements. 		Verifies age, legal capacity, and PMOC certificate; administers oath and prepares notice of posting. — Administrative Aide IV
2 Pay Marriage License Fee
Pay application and license fees at the City Treasurer's Office. 		Issues Official Receipt. ₱350.00 CTO Cashier
3 Mandatory 10-Day Publication / Posting
Wait for the 10 consecutive calendar days public posting period. 		Posts notice on public bulletin board as required by Article 17 of the Family Code. — Marriage Registration Officer
4 Claim Marriage License (Form 90)
Claim official Marriage License upon completion of the 10-day posting. 		Issues signed Marriage License (valid for 120 days nationwide). — City Civil Registrar

Petition for Correction of Clerical Error (RA 9048)

Walk-in (Legal Instruments Division)

Administrative petition to correct typographical, spelling, or clerical errors in civil registry entries (Birth, Marriage, Death) without court order.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Petition Form (RA 9048 Form): 3 original notarized copies.
  • PSA Document with Error: 1 original PSA copy containing the clerical error.
  • Two (2) Supporting Documents: Form 137, Baptismal Certificate, Voter's Record, SSS/GSIS Static Information, or Medical Records.
  • Valid ID & Community Tax Certificate: 1 photocopy of valid ID and Cedula of petitioner.
Turnaround Time
10 Days Posting + 5 Days Review
Standard Fees
₱1,000.00 (Filing Fee per RA 9048)
Mode of Transaction
Legal Instruments Division (Ground Floor)
STEP CLIENT STEP AGENCY ACTION FEES OFFICER
1 File Petition & Legal Evaluation
Submit notarized petition and supporting documents for legal assessment. 		Evaluates petition, verifies jurisdictional facts, and prepares order of payment. — Supervising Admin Officer (SAO)
2 Pay RA 9048 Filing Fee
Pay administrative filing fee at the City Treasurer's Office. 		Issues Official Receipt. ₱1,000.00 CTO Cashier
3 10-Day Posting & Decision Rendering
Wait for the mandatory 10-day posting period and subsequent review. 		Posts notice for 10 days; renders official Decision and Certificate of Finality. — City Civil Registrar
4 Claim Decision & PSA Endorsement
Claim approved Decision and Certificate of Finality for PSA affirmation. 		Releases approved decision and endorses record to PSA Civil Registrar General. — Registration Officer

Change of First Name or Correction of Sex / Date of Birth (RA 10172)

Walk-in (Legal Instruments Division)

Administrative petition for change of first name (RA 9048) or correction of day/month in birth date or sex entry (RA 10172) without court litigation.

Tap to view checklist, turnaround, & process steps

Checklist of Requirements

  • Petition Form (RA 10172 Form): 3 original notarized copies.
  • PSA Birth Certificate: 1 original copy containing the incorrect entry.
  • Earliest School Records & Medical Records: Form 137/138 (Elementary & High School), Medical Certificate.
  • Clearances: NBI Clearance, Police Clearance, and Certificate of No Pending Case / Employment Record.
  • FOR SEX CORRECTION Government Physician Certification: Affidavit certifying petitioner has not undergone sex change.
  • Affidavit of Publication: Proof of 2 consecutive weeks publication in newspaper of general circulation.
Turnaround Time
2 Weeks Publication + 10 Days Posting
Standard Fees
₱3,000.00 (Filing Fee per RA 10172)
Mode of Transaction
Legal Instruments Division
STEP CLIENT STEP AGENCY ACTION FEES OFFICER
1 Filing & Legal Examination
Submit notarized petition form, medical clearances, and early educational records. 		Reviews clearances, interviews petitioner, and issues notice for newspaper publication. — Supervising Admin Officer (SAO)
2 Pay Filing Fee
Pay administrative filing fee at the City Treasurer's Office. 		Issues Official Receipt. ₱3,000.00 CTO Cashier
3 Newspaper Publication & Bulletin Posting
Publish notice once a week for 2 consecutive weeks in a local newspaper and wait for 10-day posting. 		Posts notice on bulletin board and verifies publisher's affidavit of publication. — Registration Officer
