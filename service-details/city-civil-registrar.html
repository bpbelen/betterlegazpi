Registration of Live Birth

Registration of birth for children born in Legazpi City (On-Time, Delayed, & Special Programs)

Timely Registration (Within 30 days)

  • Duly Accomplished Certificate of Live Birth (4 copies) From Hospital, Lying-in, or birthing facilities
  • For Marital Child: Marriage Contract (1 certified copy) From PSA or Local Civil Registrar Office
  • For Non-Marital Child: AUSF (Affidavit to Use Surname of Father) & Admission of Paternity (original) + Parents' Birth Certs
  • Information Sheet & Valid ID (1 photocopy with signature) From Hospital/Lying-in and Government/Private sector

Delayed Registration (After 30 days) & BRAP

  • Certificate of Live Birth (4 accomplished copies) + PSA Negative Certification
  • Two (2) Documentary Evidences (Baptismal, School records, Voter's, MDR, SSS, Police/NBI)
  • Affidavit of 2 Disinterested Persons / Joint Affidavit (w/ ID copies)
  • Barangay Residency, 2x2 Photo, & Valid IDs (Registrant & Parents)
  • Under BRAP (Free): Negative Omnibus Certification + Certificate of Indigency For Indigenous Peoples, Muslim Filipinos, & poorest sectors
Service / Transaction Fee Processing Time
On-Time Registration (Marital Child) FREE 2 hours
On-Time Registration (Non-Marital Child — AUSF ₱200 + Paternity ₱100) ₱300.00 2 hours
Delayed Registration under BRAP (Birth Registration Assistance Project) FREE 3 hours (10 days posting)
Delayed Registration (Marital Child — 0 to 79 & 80+ yrs old) ₱500.00 3 hours (10 days posting)
Delayed Registration (Non-Marital Child — Service Fee ₱500 + AUSF ₱200 + Paternity ₱100) ₱800.00 3 hours (10 days posting)
Legitimation by Subsequent Marriage of Parents ₱410.00 base 3 hours

Registration of Death

Registration of death for persons who died in Legazpi City, Albay

Timely Registration (Within 30 days)

  • Death Certificate reviewed by City Health Office (4 original copies) From Hospital, City Health Office, or other medical facility
  • Burial Permit Fee / Transfer Permit Fee Receipt (1 original copy) From City Treasurer's Office
  • Certification of Not Embalmed (if not embalmed) / Certificate of Cremation (if cremated) From Funeral Parlor / Informant
  • Valid ID of Informant

Delayed Registration (After 30 days)

  • Notarized Death Certificate reviewed by CHO (4 original copies)
  • PSA Negative Result (1 original copy) From Philippine Statistics Authority
  • Burial Certificate (1 original copy) From Cemetery / Church where deceased was buried
  • Burial Permit / Transfer Permit & Cremation Cert (if applicable)
Service / Fee Component Fee Processing Time
Timely Registration (Burial Fee ₱30 + Issuance ₱30 + Transfer Fee ₱60 if applicable) ₱60.00 – ₱120.00 1 hour
Delayed Registration (Burial ₱30 + Transfer ₱60 + Issuance ₱30 + Service Fee ₱500) ₱620.00 1 hour (10 days posting)
Burial Permit Fee (standalone) ₱30.00 Same day
Transfer Permit Fee (for cadaver transfer outside Legazpi) ₱60.00 Same day

Marriage Registration & Licensing

Marriage License application, on-time registration, and delayed registration / reconstruction

Marriage License Application

  • Birth Certificate (1 certified photocopy) & CENOMAR (1 original from PSA)
  • Valid IDs & Mandatory Personal Appearance of Couple
  • Age 18–20: Parental Consent | Age 21–24: Parental Advice
  • If Widowed: Death Cert of Spouse | If Annulled: Judicial Decree of Nullity
  • Foreign National: Certificate of Legal Capacity to Marry from Embassy

Marriage Certificate Registration

  • On-Time Registration (Within 15 days w/ License, 30 days w/ Art. 34): Duly accomplished Marriage Certificate (4 copies) + Affidavit of Cohabitation
  • Delayed Registration: Accomplished Marriage Cert + PSA Negative Result of Marriage
  • Reconstruction of Marriage: CENOMAR, PSA Negative Result, Matrimony record, Children's birth certs, Affidavit of 2 Disinterested Persons
Service / Transaction Fee Processing Time
On-Time Registration of Certificate of Marriage FREE 1 hour
Application & Issuance of Marriage License (Family Planning ₱100 + Application ₱300 + License ₱200 + Solemnization) ₱1,100.00 3 hours (10 days posting)
Delayed Registration / Reconstruction of Marriage Certificate ₱500.00 1 hour (10 days posting)

Certified True Copies & PSA Endorsements

Certified true copies of civil registry documents, electronic transmittals, and certifications

Certified True Copies / Other Certifications

  • Verification Form (properly filled-up and signed by requesting party) Available at the LCRO Information Desk
  • Valid ID (1 photocopy with picture and signature)
  • Authorization Letter & Owner's ID / Affidavit of Kinship (if requester is not the document owner)
  • For Other Certifications: Official Letter-Request (1 original copy)

Electronic Endorsement to PSA

  • Advance Endorsement: Transmittal Letter (3 copies), OCRG Form 102/103/97, Proof of Urgency, Letter Request
  • Negative to PSA CRS: PSA Negative Certification, Form 1A/2A/3A, Certified Copy of Registry Book, Municipal Form copy
  • Blurred / Illegible Entries: Blurred PSA-SECPA copy, Transmittal, Form 1A/2A/3A, Certified Copy of Registry Book

Corrections, Petitions & Court Decrees

Administrative corrections under RA 9048/10172, RA 9255, court decrees, and supplemental reports

R.A. 9048 & R.A. 10172 Petitions

  • Clerical Error (R.A. 9048): Document subject for correction (2 copies), Parents/siblings' COLB, at least 2 personal records bearing correct entry
  • Change of First Name (R.A. 9048): COLB, NBI & Police Clearances, Employer Cert of No Pending Admin Case, 2-week newspaper publication, 3 public records
  • Gender / Day / Month of Birth (R.A. 10172): Certified transcription of registry book, Earliest school record (Elem/HS), Medical/Baptismal cert, NBI/PNP/Employer clearances, City Health Officer cert of no sex change

Court Decrees, R.A. 9255 & Supplementals

  • Court Decrees (Adoption, Nullity, Annulment): Certified Xerox of Court Decision (4 copies), Certificate of Finality/Entry of Judgment (4 copies), Authenticity Cert if outside RTC Legazpi
  • Change of Surname (R.A. 9255): Child's LCRO/PSA birth cert, Subscribed AUSF (3 copies), Notarized Father's Admission of Paternity
  • Supplemental Report: Certified copy of document, Subscribed Affidavit of Supplemental Report (2 orig), Supporting records

Email Inquiries & Anti-Red Tape Complaints (PACD)

Online record assistance, ARTA compliance, and Public Assistance & Complaints Desk

Email-Assisted Inquiry / Verification

  • Free Online Assistance: Send verification requests for birth, death, or marriage certificates without visiting the office in person.
  • Required Details: Full name of document owner, date of birth/marriage/death, place of occurrence, and attached valid ID.
  • Official Inquiry Email: legazpicivilregistrar@gmail.com
  • Response Turnaround: Within 2 hours during working days.

Feedback & Complaints (PACD / ARTA / CCB)

  • How to Send Feedback: Fill up the Customer Feedback form at the Information Desk. Feedback is immediately forwarded to the concerned section head.
  • How to File a Complaint: Accomplish the PACD Complaint/Feedback form. The PACD interviews the client, endorses the matter to the City Civil Registrar, and provides prompt resolution.
