Your consolidated guide for birth, marriage, death registrations, legal instruments, and vital records in Legazpi City.

Office of the City Civil Registrar (CCR)

Citizen's Charter, 2026 First Edition. Read the source document

What do you need to do?

  • A baby was born 3 services
    1. Registering a birth within 30 days
    2. Registering a birth after 30 days, for people under 80
    3. Getting a certified copy of a birth, marriage or death record
  • You have no birth certificate 3 services
    1. Registering a birth after 30 days, for people under 80
    2. Registering a birth after 30 days, for people aged 80 and above
    3. Checking a record by email without going to the office
  • You are getting married 3 services
    1. Applying for a marriage licence
    2. Registering a marriage on time
    3. Getting a certified copy of a birth, marriage or death record
  • Someone in the family died 2 services
    1. Registering a death within 30 days
    2. Registering a death after 30 days
  • A name or detail on your record is wrong 3 services
    1. Correcting a clerical error or changing a first name
    2. Adding information left blank on a record
    3. Letting a child use the father's surname
  • The PSA says it has no copy, or the copy is unreadable 2 services
    1. Endorsing a record to the PSA so a copy comes out sooner
    2. Getting a certified copy of a birth, marriage or death record
  • A court decided your case 2 services
    1. Registering a court decree on adoption, annulment or legal separation
    2. Legitimating a child after the parents marry

  1. 1Submit the court decision or order, together with the certificate of finality, entry of judgment or decree.

    The office then

    1. Examines and evaluates the court decree for completeness and accuracy, registers it in the Registry Book of Court Decrees, and prepares and signs the certificate of registration and the annotated civil registry documents.

      2 hoursLegal Instrument and Judicial Decrees Division staff

  2. 2Pay the fee.

    The office then

    1. Issues the official receipt.

      P500.00 for registration of an application of a foreign decree or adoption; P700.00 for registration of an annulment or declaration of nullity of marriage; P5.00 per page for a photocopy certified from the originalCashier

  3. 3Claim the annotated civil registry documents.

    The office then

    1. Endorses the document to the PSA in Quezon City so the PSA database is updated.

      Legal Instrument and Judicial Decrees Division staff

Total time 2 hrs. Total fee P500.00 for registration of an application of a foreign decree or adoption; P700.00 for registration of an annulment or declaration of nullity of marriage; P5.00 per page for a photocopy certified from the original

Feedback and complaints

Fill up the Customer Feedback Form at the information desk of the office. You can also reach the office on 0960-688-4345, or the contact officers on 0995-469-7747, 0935-818-5300 or 0936-970-0665.

Feedback is forwarded to the section or person concerned for action. To complain, fill up the Client's Complaint and Feedback Form at the Public Assistance and Complaints Desk. The office evaluates the form, interviews the complainant, and endorses the complaint to the staff concerned or to the Local Civil Registrar, who takes it up with the person complained about and gives feedback.
