City Agriculture Office

Official Citizen's Charter for farm registration, certifications, agri-fishery support, and agricultural production inputs for Legazpeño farmers.

Office

CAO

Location

Brgy. 38-Gogon

Hours

8 AM - 5 PM (Mon-Fri)

3 Key Services

City Agriculture Office (CAO)

Citizen's Charter, 2026 First Edition. Read the source document

What do you need to do?

  • Planting a crop or stocking a pond 2 services

    2. Getting agri-fishery technical advice or a farm visit
  • Registering as a farmer or fisherfolk 1 service

Farm and fishery support 2 services

1 Requesting seeds, seedlings, fertilizer or fingerlings 11 minutes at the counter 14 minutes in total 5 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Release of agricultural and fishery production inputs to farmers, fisherfolk and organisations: palay and vegetable seeds, planting materials, fertilizers, tilapia and bangus fingerlings, and mangrove seedlings.

How it works

  1. 1Request production inputs (vegetable seeds or seedlings, fertilizers, palay seeds, planting materials or fishery inputs).

    The office then

    1. Assist the client in filling up the visitor's logbook.

      2 minutesOfficer of the Day

    2. Ask for the request form for agri-inputs for record purposes.

      1 minuteAgricultural Technologist assigned / Aquaculturist

    3. Check availability of seeds and planting materials, then issue a requisition slip.

      1 minuteAgricultural Technologist assigned / Aquaculturist

    4. Forward the requisition slip to the City Agriculture Office for approval and signature.

      1 minuteCity Agriculturist

  2. 2Sign the forms and fill up the release logbook.

    The office then

    1. Assist the client to sign the forms and fill up the logbook for release.

      2 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  3. 3Proceed to the bodega or nursery, wait for release and receive the agri inputs.

    The office then

    1. Release the agricultural inputs.

      5 minutesAdministrative Aide I

  4. 4Fill up the Client Satisfaction Form.

    The office then

    1. Receive the Client Satisfaction Form.

      2 minutesOfficer of the Day

Total time 14 minutes Total fee Free

How it works

  1. 1Request a certification, or enrolment in RSBSA, PCIC insurance or boat registration.
