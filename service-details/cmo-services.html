Assistance 1 service

1 Asking the Mayor's Office for medical, burial or financial assistance 15 minutes at the counter Done in one visit 5 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Help from the City Mayor's Office with hospital bills and medicines, a funeral, or other financial need. What you bring depends on which kind of help you are asking for, and the office is explicit that incomplete documents are not accommodated.

How it works

  1. 1Submit the requirements for the kind of assistance you need at the receiving area.

    The office then

    1. Receives the required documents for pre-assessment, then photocopies the pre-assessed documents.

      10 minutes per clientAdministrative Aide IV and job order staff

  2. 2Receive the assistance from the City Mayor's Office.

    The office then

    1. Releases the medical, burial or financial assistance to the client.

      5 minutes per clientExecutive Assistant IV / Administrative Aide IV

Total time 15 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • Incomplete documents are not accommodated, so bring everything on the list for the kind of assistance you need.
  • The charter states these times as per client, being served one at a time.

2 Getting a document signed by the Mayor 40 minutes at the counter Done in one visit 5 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

How financial documents and communication letters reach the City Mayor for signature and come back out. This covers obligation requests, vouchers, purchase requests and orders, and letters addressed to the Mayor.

What to bring 5 items

How it works

  1. 1Submit the documents at the receiving area.

    The office then

    1. Receives and records the documents in the logbook, forwards them to the Executive Secretary of the Mayor for instructions and signature, then forwards them to the Mayor. Signed and noted financial documents and letters go back for release and endorsement, and noted documents are endorsed to barangays, private agencies and other city offices for action.

      30 minutesAdministrative Aides I and IV, Executive Assistant III, and job order and contract of service staff

  2. 2Claim the signed documents.

    The office then

    1. Releases the financial documents signed by the City Mayor to the client.

      10 minutesAdministrative Aide IV / Administrative Aide I

Total time 40 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • The charter notes that endorsement, and the release of signed documents, may take longer depending on when the Mayor approves them.
  • Clients are advised to follow up the next working day for documents still pending because of circumstances beyond the office's control.

Venues and appointments 2 services

3 Booking a city venue for an event 23 minutes at the counter Done in one visit 1 document From P40,000.00

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Reserving the Legazpi Convention Center, the Ibalong Centrum for Recreation, the Ibalong Conference Room, the Gregorian basketball court or Ibalong Park. You get the letter approved first, then pay, then come back to reserve the date.

Internal or external clients

What to bring 1 item

How it works

  1. 1Submit the original letter request addressed to the Mayor.

    The office then

    1. Receives the letter request for scheduling and checks whether the venue is available. If it is, the letter goes to the Mayor for approval; if not, you are told which dates are open. Once approved, you are given a copy of the approved letter to pay against.

      15 minutes per clientAdministrative Aide I

  2. 2Pay the rental at the City Treasurer's Office, or at the City Engineer's Office for Ibalong Park.

    The office then

    1. Receives the payment and issues an official receipt.

      5 minutes per clientP40,000.00 to P60,000.00; P350 a dayCity Treasurer's Office and City Engineer's Office cashiers

  3. 3Go back to the City Mayor's Office with the official receipt to reserve the venue.

    The office then

    1. Acknowledges and keeps a copy of the official receipt and reserves the venue for your event.

      3 minutes per clientAdministrative Aide I

Total time 23 minutes Total fee P40,000.00 to P60,000.00; P350 a day

Good to know

  • Ibalong Park is paid for at the City Engineer's Office; the other venues at the City Treasurer's Office.

Feedback and complaints

Answer the client feedback form and drop it in the box at the Public Assistance and Complaint Desk in the City Hall lobby.

The person in charge opens the drop box and evaluates each form; feedback needing an answer is forwarded to the office concerned. Complaints may be filed in writing or in person to the City Mayor, by email, or on (052) 732 7988. After evaluation and investigation the Local Chief Executive calls both parties in for consultation and appropriate action.
