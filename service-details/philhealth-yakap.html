Universal Healthcare Guarantee for All Legazpeños

Under Republic Act No. 11223 (Universal Health Care Act), all Filipino citizens and registered dependents are automatically covered by PhilHealth YAKAP. Even if your formal contributions are incomplete, you can register and immediately avail of primary care check-ups and maintenance medicines at Legazpi City accredited facilities without out-of-pocket charges.

How to avail, in five steps

From signing up to walking out with your medicines. Most people finish steps 1 and 2 in a single morning.

1

Sign up with one clinic

Choose one accredited YAKAP clinic in Legazpi City and register with it through the eGovPH App, the PhilHealth Member Portal, or via walk-in at your preferred health facility.

No smartphone? Walk in and register at the Legazpi City Health Office or the Homapon Super Health Center. Staff will do it for you.
2

Go for your first check-up

Your clinic records your medical history, checks your blood pressure, weight and other vital signs, and gives you a physical exam. This visit is what opens your benefits.

What to bring: 1 valid government-issued photo ID and your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN).
3

Get your lab tests done

Based on that first visit, your doctor orders whichever covered tests you need: blood tests, urinalysis, X-ray, ECG or a cancer screening.

Zero Copay: Diagnostic tests conducted at accredited facility laboratories are 100% covered with no out-of-pocket fees.
4

Receive your prescription

If you need maintenance medicines, your doctor issues an electronic prescription carrying a security code. That code is what the pharmacy scans, so keep the prescription safe.

Benefit Allocation: The UPSC validates dispensation against your ₱20,000 annual GAMOT medicine cap.
5

Collect your free medicines

Show that prescription at the health centre pharmacy or an accredited partner pharmacy in Legazpi City. You pay nothing.

Dispensation: Chronic maintenance medicines may be dispensed in up to a 3-month supply per valid prescription.

How this list was built

Every entry above was read directly from PhilHealth's two published accreditation lists and matched by its printed entry number, which is shown on each card so you can check it against the source. Nothing is inferred: a clinic is shown as YAKAP Konsulta only where PhilHealth's List of Accredited YAKAP Clinics prints it, and as a GAMOT provider only where the separate List of Accredited GAMOT Package Providers prints it. Both lists are current as of 31 July 2026.

Five City Health Office sites — the Homapon Super Health Center, the Buyuan, Banquerohan and Taysan lying-in clinics, and the CHO Laboratory — hold no separate entry on either list. They serve patients under the City Health Office's own accreditation (no. 2085), the way a bank branch operates under its head office's licence, and are marked as such above rather than claimed as separately accredited.

Accreditation dates are those PhilHealth published, and accreditations lapse. Call a clinic before you travel, and see the full sources and data notes on the health facilities directory.

What YAKAP pays for

PhilHealth YAKAP (formerly Konsulta) provides four comprehensive healthcare guarantees designed for preventive treatment and continuous outpatient care.

Primary Care & Clinical Consultations

Continuous health monitoring, lifestyle profiling, and early disease detection with your designated YAKAP physician.

First Patient Encounter (FPE)

Comprehensive initial health profiling, medical history recording, and biometric screening (blood pressure, BMI, vitals) during your first visit.

Annual Regular Check-ups

Unlimited routine outpatient medical consultations for members and registered dependents to catch chronic illnesses before hospital intervention is needed.

Health Education & Counseling

Personalized dietary, lifestyle, smoking cessation, and physical activity guidance tailored to your age bracket and risk assessment.

Specialized Referrals

Coordinated referral system to higher-level apex hospitals (such as BRHMC in Legazpi) when specialized surgical or inpatient care is diagnosed.

The 13 Covered Laboratory Tests

These are the thirteen tests the YAKAP package pays for. Your accredited YAKAP physician orders whichever ones you need based on your check-up — they are not available on request.

1. Complete Blood Count (CBC)

Evaluates overall health and detects infections, anemia, and immune system abnormalities.

2. Urinalysis

Screens for kidney disease, urinary tract infections (UTI), and diabetes-related kidney damage.

3. Fecalysis

Detects intestinal parasites, digestive infections, and gastrointestinal disorders.

4. Fecal Occult Blood Test (FOBT)

Finds hidden blood in stool — an early warning sign for colorectal cancer and bleeding ulcers.

5. Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)

Plasma glucose after an 8–10 hour fast, used to diagnose diabetes and pre-diabetes.

6. Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT)

Confirmatory diabetes test, also used to screen for gestational diabetes in pregnancy.

7. Glycosylated Hemoglobin (HbA1c)

Shows your average blood sugar over the past three months — the standard check for diabetes control.

8. Lipid Profile

Measures Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, and LDL to assess heart attack and stroke risk.

9. Creatinine

Blood test measuring kidney function, essential for hypertensive and diabetic patients.

10. Chest X-Ray

Radiographic imaging of the lungs and heart for pneumonia, tuberculosis, and cardiac enlargement.

11. Sputum Microscopy

Examines phlegm under a microscope to diagnose pulmonary tuberculosis (PTB).

12. Electrocardiogram (ECG)

12-lead resting heart tracing that detects arrhythmias, ischemia, and heart muscle strain.

13. Pap Smear

Cervical cancer screening for women, detecting pre-cancerous cells at an early, curable stage.

Amoxicillin, Co-amoxiclav, Azithromycin, Cefuroxime, Cefixime, Ciprofloxacin, Doxycycline, Metronidazole, Fluconazole and other outpatient anti-infectives.

Supportive & Other Therapy (18)

Paracetamol, Ibuprofen, Mefenamic Acid, Omeprazole, Cetirizine, Loratadine, Iron and Folic Acid, Oral Rehydration Salts, Zinc and Colchicine.

Anti-asthma & COPD (8)

Salbutamol, Ipratropium + Salbutamol, Fluticasone + Salmeterol, Budesonide + Formoterol, Montelukast, Tiotropium and Prednisone.

Diabetes, Cholesterol & Clotting (9)

Metformin, Gliclazide and Dapagliflozin for diabetes; Atorvastatin, Rosuvastatin, Simvastatin and Fenofibrate for cholesterol; Aspirin and Clopidogrel for clot prevention. Gabapentin covers nerve pain.

21 always kept in stock

Under Advisory 2026-0007, twenty-one of the seventy-five — the original Konsulta medicines — must stay continuously available at every accredited YAKAP clinic. The rest are claimed at GAMOT dispensing facilities.

Your ₱20,000 resets every January 1

The annual benefit limit refreshes on 1 January and unused balance does not carry over. Each dispensing gives you a GAMOT Availment Slip showing your remaining balance.

Electronic Prescription (ePresS / UPSC)

Prescriptions are issued electronically with a Unique Prescription Security Code for seamless dispensing at accredited pharmacies.

Breast Cancer Screening

Annual screening mammography and breast ultrasound for qualified women aged 40 and above or those with family risk history.

Lung Cancer Screening

Low-Dose Chest Computed Tomography (LDCT) for high-risk individuals, long-term smokers, and occupational risk groups.

Fecal Immunochemical Test (FIT) / Fecal Occult Blood Test (FOBT) and diagnostic colonoscopy referrals for early polyps detection.

Liver Cancer Screening

Alpha-fetoprotein (AFP) quantitative serum test and liver ultrasound for patients with chronic Hepatitis B/C or liver cirrhosis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common questions about the PhilHealth YAKAP program in Legazpi City.

All Filipino citizens residing in Legazpi City, regardless of employment status or past contribution history, are eligible under the Universal Health Care Act.

This includes private and government employees, self-earning individuals, OFWs, senior citizens, PWDs, 4Ps beneficiaries, indigents, and all registered legal dependents (spouses, children under 21, and parents).

For the covered services, yes. For consultations and the 13 covered laboratory tests, accredited public clinics (like Legazpi City Health Office and Homapon Super Health Center) are strictly prohibited from charging any out-of-pocket fees or balance billing. In private accredited partner clinics, primary consultations and covered labs are also provided without copayment when properly referred.

Medicines have two limits. You pay nothing until your ₱20,000 annual GAMOT allocation is used up; after that, further medicines are at your own cost. And if you ask for a branded product priced above PhilHealth's fixed fee for that molecule, you pay the difference. Ask for the generic and the fixed fee covers it in full.

Yes. Under PhilHealth Advisory 2025-0065 (YAKAP Clinic Transfer Guidelines), members may change their registered YAKAP clinic once per calendar year, or anytime upon relocation of residence or workplace.

You can perform this transfer directly via the eGovPH App, through the PhilHealth Member Portal, or by visiting PhilHealth LHIO Legazpi at Landco Business Park.

Bring any valid government-issued photo ID (e.g., PhilID / National ID, Driver's License, Senior Citizen ID, Postal ID) and your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) or Member Data Record (MDR).

If registering dependents, bring copies of their Birth Certificate (for children) or Marriage Certificate (for spouse).

Under PhilHealth Circular No. 2025-0013, each registered beneficiary has an annual benefit limit of ₱20,000 worth of covered outpatient medicines drawn from a list of 75 molecules. The limit is per beneficiary, so registered dependents each have their own ₱20,000.

Your accredited doctor issues an electronic prescription (ePresS) with a Unique Prescription Security Code (UPSC). You present this prescription at the clinic's dispensing pharmacy or accredited partner pharmacy to claim your maintenance medications in up to 3-month supply intervals without paying cash.

Two things to know: the limit resets every 1 January and unused balance is not carried over, and once you have used up the ₱20,000 you pay for further medicines yourself. At each pick-up the pharmacy gives you a GAMOT Availment Slip showing how much of your ₱20,000 is left.
