Citizen's Charter, 2026 First Edition. Read the source document

What do you need to do?

  • You need somewhere to live 2 services
    1. Applying for a slot in a relocation site
    2. Buying a city-owned lot through socialized housing
  • You are being evicted or displaced 2 services
    1. Support with financial assistance after eviction
    2. Implementation of the Urban Development and Housing Act
  • You need power or water connected 1 service
    1. Certification for an ALECO or LCWD connection
  • Your neighbourhood wants to organise 2 services
    1. Forming a homeowners' association
    2. Getting a subdivision plan or scheme prepared

Relocation and lots 3 services

1 Applying for a slot in a relocation site 3 days & 35 minutes at the counter 9 days & 35 minutes in total 9 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

A slot in a city relocation or resettlement site, for informal settlers, people displaced by development, and those living in danger zones. You report to the office daily while your application is pending, and the office inspects the sites monthly once you are awarded a lot.

Before you go

Your time
3 days & 35 minutes at the counter, 9 days & 35 minutes before it is finished
Trips to the office
3 separate visits
Where to go
Office of Resettlement, Housing and Community Development
What you pay
Nothing
Office or division
Office of Resettlement, Housing and Community Development
Who may avail
Underprivileged and homeless citizens and informal settlers
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C), Government to Government (G2G), Government to Business (G2B)
Classification
Complex

What to bring 9 items

How it works

  1. 1Write a letter to the City Mayor asking for a slot in the relocation or resettlement site.

    The office then

    1. Helps you write the letter to the City Mayor and submits it to the City Mayor's Office.

      15 to 20 minutesAdministrative Aide I and job order staff

  2. 2Sit the interview and receive your batch number.

    The office then

    1. Interviews you, assigns a batch number, and requires you to follow up daily by reporting to the office in person and signing the attendance sheet.

      15 minutesJob order staff

  3. 3Submit the required documents and receive the award of your lot.

    The office then

    1. Awards the lot to qualified beneficiaries once the required documents are in.

      3 daysActing CGDH I

    2. Conducts monthly monitoring and site inspection of the actual occupants in the relocation and resettlement sites.

      3 daysActing CGDH I

    3. Confiscates the slot from beneficiaries found to have violated the terms and conditions of occupancy.

      3 daysAdministrative Aides I and IV

Total time 9 days & 35 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • You have to report to the office in person every day while your application is pending and sign the attendance sheet.
  • A slot can be taken back if the terms and conditions of occupancy are broken.
2 Buying a city-owned lot through socialized housing 59 days at the counter Done in one visit 9 documents Fee applies

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Acquisition, appraisal and disposition of land the city owns or has acquired. Lots offered through socialized housing are paid on an instalment scheme against a value set by the Appraisal Committee, and you get a certification of full payment at the end.

Before you go

Your time
59 days, and you leave with it
Trips to the office
3 separate visits
Where to go
Office of Resettlement, Housing and Community Development
What you pay
The appraised value set by the Appraisal Committee, on an instalment scheme, plus penalties for past due amounts
Office or division
Office of Resettlement, Housing and Community Development
Who may avail
Underprivileged and homeless citizens and informal settlers
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C), Government to Government (G2G), Government to Business (G2B)
Classification
Complex

What to bring 9 items

How it works

  1. 1File your application for verification and assessment.

    The office then

    1. Processes the documentary requirements of applicants for the city-owned or acquired properties.

      45 daysActing CGDH I / Administrative Aide IV

  2. 2Pay against the schedule of payments.

    The office then

    1. Prepares the schedule of payments of the appraised value set by the Appraisal Committee for lots offered for sale through socialized housing on an instalment scheme, and the corresponding penalties for anything past due. Payment is collected by the City Treasurer's Office.

      7 daysThe appraised value set by the Appraisal Committee, on an instalment scheme, plus penalties for past due amountsAdministrative Aide I / Administrative Aide VI

  3. 3Receive the certification of full payment.

    The office then

    1. Prepares, issues and countersigns the certification of full payment for the city-owned or acquired property, to be signed by the City Treasurer and the City Mayor.

      7 daysActing CGDH I / Administrative Aide I

Total time 59 days Total fee The appraised value set by the Appraisal Committee, on an instalment scheme, plus penalties for past due amounts

Good to know

  • What you pay is the appraised value the Appraisal Committee sets for the lot, not a fixed fee, and it is paid in instalments.
