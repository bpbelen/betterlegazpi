Step-by-Step Process

Follow these steps to register a birth or request a certified true copy in Legazpi City

1

Check Document Type

Determine if your transaction is On-Time Registration (within 30 days), Delayed Registration (beyond 30 days), or Certified True Copy (CTC) issuance.

2

Prepare Documents

Bring valid government-issued ID and accomplished Certificate of Live Birth (COLB). For delayed registration: PSA Negative Result and 2 supporting proofs.

3

Visit City Civil Registrar

Go to the Office of the City Civil Registrar (OCCR) at Ground Floor, Main Building, Legazpi City Hall, Rizal St. Office hours: Mon–Fri, 8AM–5PM.

4

Submit & Verification

Submit documents at the Receiving Window (M. Loria / D. Abejuro / M. Azcune). Staff verifies completeness and issues the Order of Payment.

5

Pay Applicable Fee

Pay at Cashier (I. Mendones / J.K. Nuñez): Timely Marital is FREE; Non-Marital is ₱300 (AUSF/Paternity); Certified Copy is ₱70; Delayed is ₱500.

6

Claim Document

Present Official Receipt (OR) to Registration Officer. Certified copies take ~10 mins; delayed registrations are released after 10-day notice of posting.

Requirements Checklist

Required documents based on transaction type

For Certified True Copy (CTC / Local Copy) — ₱70.00

  • Duly accomplished Verification Form from the CCRO counter
  • Valid Government-Issued ID of document owner (original & photocopy)
  • If representative: Signed Authorization Letter and valid IDs of both owner and representative
  • Official Receipt of ₱70.00 per copy paid at the Cashier

For Timely Registration (Within 30 Days) — FREE (Marital) / ₱300 (Non-Marital)

  • 4 original copies of Certificate of Live Birth (COLB / Form 102) duly signed by Attendant at Birth and Parents
  • Marriage Certificate of Parents (for marital child — 100% FREE registration)
  • For non-marital child: Affidavit of Admission of Paternity (₱100) + Affidavit to Use the Surname of the Father / AUSF (₱200)

For Delayed Registration (Beyond 30 Days) — ₱500.00 / FREE (80+ yo)

  • Duly accomplished COLB with notarized Affidavit for Delayed Registration at the back
  • PSA Negative Certification of Birth (CRS Form No. 1)
  • At least 2 documentary proofs showing name, birth date, place of birth & parents' names (Baptismal Certificate, School Records Form 137, Voter's Registration, PhilHealth/GSIS records)
  • Mandatory 10-day notice of posting period before document issuance

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a birth certificate registered in another municipality or city?
No. The Legazpi City Civil Registrar only holds records of births that occurred and were registered within Legazpi City, Albay. For records in other LGUs, request from that LGU's Local Civil Registrar or directly through the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).
What is the difference between a Local Certified Copy and a PSA Birth Certificate?
A Local Certified True Copy (CTC) is issued directly from the Legazpi City Civil Registry archives (₱70.00, ~10 mins) and is valid for local school enrollments, employment, and city transactions. A PSA copy on Security Paper (SECPA) is authenticated at the national level and is required for passport, DFA, and visa applications.
Is delayed birth registration free for senior citizens?
Yes. Under the Legazpi City Citizen's Charter, delayed registration for senior citizens aged 80 years old and above is 100% FREE (Service 3). For individuals aged 0 to 79 years old, the service fee is ₱500.00.
What if there is a spelling mistake on my birth certificate?
Clerical or typographical errors in first names, dates, or gender can be corrected administratively without a court order under Republic Act 9048 and RA 10172. Please visit our City Civil Registrar Hub for full RA 9048/10172 petition guidelines.
