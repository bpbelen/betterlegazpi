Stall leases 3 services

1 Applying for a new stall lease 18 days & 35 minutes at the counter Done in one visit 4 documents As per approved rates

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Applying for a stall at the Legazpi Public Market if you do not already hold one. The application goes to the Market Committee, and where more than one person applies for the same stall it is decided by raffle. At roughly twenty-one working days this is much the longest service the market publishes.

How it works

  1. 1Submit the application and the requirements.

    The office then

    1. Receives and records the application.

      30 minutesMarket Staff

  2. 2The application is endorsed for review.

    The office then

    1. Conducts an initial review of the documents and forwards them to the Market Committee.

      1-2 working daysMarket Administrator

  3. 3Your eligibility is screened and validated.

    The office then

    1. Evaluates the applications. Where there is more than one applicant, conducts a raffle.

      within the dayMarket Committee

  4. 4The stall is approved and awarded.

    The office then

    1. Issues the notice of award to the raffle winner or the qualified applicant.

      10-15 working daysMarket Administrator / Market Committee

  5. 5Pay the lease fee and goodwill.

    The office then

    1. Issues the official receipt.

      5 minutesAs per approved ratesCashier

  6. 6Sign the contract.

    The office then

    1. Records and releases the contract copy.

      1 dayMarket Administrator

Total time 18 days & 35 minutes Total fee As per approved rates

Good to know

  • Where more than one person applies for the same stall, it is decided by raffle.

6 Making a complaint about the market 8 days & 20 minutes at the counter Done in one visit 2 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

How a vendor, a customer or anyone else raises a complaint about the Legazpi Public Market. It can end in a mediated dialogue between the parties, and you get a written response.

8 days & 20 minutes, and you leave with it

4 separate visits

Nothing

Legazpi City Public Market Office

Vendors, customers or the general public with a valid complaint about the public market
Type of transaction
Government to Client (G2C)
Classification

What to bring 2 items

How it works

  1. 1File the complaint verbally or in writing at the Market Office.

    The office then

    1. Records and acknowledges the complaint, and gives you a complaint form if you need one.

      5 minutesMarket Office Personnel

  2. 2Submit the accomplished complaint form with any evidence.

    The office then

    1. Reviews and evaluates the complaint.

      15 minutesMarket Administrator / Supervisor

  3. 3Attend the dialogue or mediation, if one is required.

    The office then

    1. Facilitates a resolution between the parties.

      Within 3 working daysMarket Officer-in-Charge

  4. 4Receive feedback on how the complaint was resolved.

    The office then

    1. Issues the written response or resolution.

      Within 5 working daysMarket Administrator / Supervisor

Total time 8 days & 20 minutes Total fee Free

The Economic Enterprise Management Division is published under the City Mayor's Office and issues a separate charter for each market it runs. This one covers the Legazpi Public Market. It names only the Market Administrator; its process tables identify everyone else by role.

Feedback and complaints

Submit a written complaint to the Public Market Office, or use the hotline or email.

A complaint is acknowledged within three working days and acted on accordingly.

About this information

This page reproduces the Economic Enterprise Management Division, Legazpi Public Market Citizen's Charter, 2026 First Edition. The published charter is always the authority. If anything here differs from it, follow the charter and please tell us so we can correct the page.

Total processing times are added up from the individual steps. Where a charter states a total that does not match its own steps, the figure shown here is the sum of the steps.
