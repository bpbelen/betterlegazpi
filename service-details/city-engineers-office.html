Building Permit Application Procedure

Issued by the Building Official to allow construction, alteration, renovation, or demolition conforming with the National Building Code

1

Submit Application & Plans

Submit 4 notarized copies of Building Permit Application, ancillary forms, and 4 sets of signed/sealed plans at Window B (Receiving Window).

15 mins (Receiving Clerk)
2

Verification & Zoning Review

Documentary verifier checks completeness and issues Claim Stub. Evaluated for zoning compliance by Zoning Administrator.

1 hr (Verifier) + 4 hrs (Zoning)
3

Technical Review

Joint technical evaluation by Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) and Building Permit Division trade evaluators.

5 Days (Complex) / 15 Days (Highly Tech)
4

Consolidated Order of Payment

Assessment prepared by Assessor, reviewed by Division Chief, and approved by the Building Official. Notice sent via SMS/Email.

3 hrs total review & approval
5

Payment of Fees

Pay required regulatory fees and charges at Window A (Cashier) or through online payment channels.

15 mins (Cashier)
6

Claim Building Permit & Clearances

Permit prepared, recommended by Division Chief, approved by Building Official, and released at Window C with Locational & Fire Clearances.

3 hrs preparation + 15 mins release

Certificate of Occupancy Application

Required before occupying or operating any completed building or structure to certify safety and Code compliance

1

Submit Occupancy Documents

Submit 3 copies of Application Form, 3 notarized Certificates of Completion, As-Built Plans, and Geodetic certification at Window B.

15 mins (Receiving Clerk)
2

Joint Site Inspection

On-site inspection conducted jointly by Zoning Officer, Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), and CEO Trade Inspectors.

1 Day (Joint Inspection Team)
3

Technical Review & Payment Order

Evaluation by Division Chief and Fire Marshall. Consolidated Order of Payment prepared, approved, and notified to applicant.

5 Days (Complex) / 15 Days (Highly Tech)
4

Payment of Occupancy Fees

Pay assessed fees at Window A (Cashier) based on the approved Consolidated Order of Payment.

15 mins (Cashier)
5

Approval of Certificate

Certificate of Occupancy prepared by Clerk, endorsed by Building Permit Division Chief, and signed by Building Official.

2 hrs (Division Chief / Building Official)
6

Release of Certificate

Receive Certificate of Occupancy and Fire Safety Inspection Certificate at Window C (Releasing Window).

15 mins (Releasing Clerk)

Key Documentary Requirements

Official requirements for Building, Occupancy, Electrical, and Mechanical Permit applications

Building Permit Plans & Technical Documents

  • 4 copies of notarized Application Form & Ancillary Permit Forms (from legazpi.gov.ph/services)
  • Certified True Copy of latest OCT/TCT from Registry of Deeds (or notarized Lease / Deed of Sale / Consent)
  • Geodetic Engineer Certification of non-encroachment on adjoining properties
  • 4 sets of Survey Plans, Architectural, Structural, Electrical, Mechanical & Plumbing Plans (signed & sealed)
  • 3 sets of Structural Analysis & Design (for buildings >1 storey or >20 sq.m. floor area)
  • Boring & Soil Load Test (for 3+ storeys or hazard-prone areas per NSCP)
  • 4 sets of notarized Bill of Materials and Cost Estimates
  • 1 Construction Logbook signed & sealed by Engineer/Architect-in-Charge
  • DOLE Approved Construction Safety and Health Program (CSHP)
  • ALECO Certification (Power Line Corridor clearance per RA 11361)

Occupancy, Electrical & Mechanical Requirements

  • Occupancy: 3 copies of Application Form & 3 notarized Certificates of Completion
  • Occupancy: 3 sets of As-Built Plans signed & sealed by registered professionals
  • Occupancy: Photos of completed structure (front, sides, rear) & Fire Safety Compliance (FSCCR)
  • Electrical: 4 copies Application Form, Electrical Design Plans signed by PEE, Location Sketch & Brgy Clearance
  • Mechanical: 5 sets Mechanical Plans, Specifications, BOM, and Equipment Calculations signed by PME
  • Mechanical to Operate: PME Soundness Certification & Test Results (Hydro, Emission, Weight/Trip test)
  • Demolition: 4 application forms, 5 sets Demolition Plans, property proof & GSO clearance for gov't buildings
  • Excavation / Sidewalk: 4 application forms, 5 sets plans, DPWH clearance (if along national road)

Construction, Planning & Barangay Projects Services

Preparation of Programs of Work (POW), structural estimates, construction plans, and project validation

Program of Works (POW) & Estimates Preparation

  • 1 to 2-Storey Building: Site inspection, architectural drafting & cost estimation (13-19 days)
  • 3-Storey Building: Foundation survey, structural drafting & POW preparation (26 days)
  • Roads & Drainages (≤₱1 Million): Profile levelling & topographic survey (24 days)
  • Water Systems & Bridges: Hydro/topographic survey & comprehensive POW (37 days)
  • Barangay Projects: Technical POW for all 70 Legazpi City Barangays (37 days)
  • Final Project Inspection: On-site audit & issuance of Final Inspection Report (3 days 4 hrs 40 mins)

Planning, Design & Infrastructure Maintenance

  • Flagship Architectural Plans: Comprehensive engineering designs for major city structures (2 weeks - 2 months)
  • Streetlight Repair & Maintenance: Rapid inspection and repair of public electrical lighting (7 days)
  • Roads & Drainage Maintenance: Desilting, pothole patching, and culvert rehabilitation (7 days)
  • Heavy Equipment & Motorpool: Preventive servicing and repairs for city service fleets (7 days)
  • Annual Safety Inspections: Structural & electrical assessment of commercial establishments

Important Regulatory Guidelines

  • A Building Permit becomes null and void if work does not commence within 1 year from date of issue.
  • Work is considered suspended or abandoned if construction ceases for a continuous period of 120 days.
  • All design plans must be prepared, signed, and sealed by duly licensed registered architects and engineers.
  • Simple Structures (≤1,500 sq.m., up to 2-3 storeys per JMC 2018-01) qualify for expedited 5-day processing.
  • Standard plan sizes: A3 for residential, 20"x30" or 30"x40" for commercial & other building types.
