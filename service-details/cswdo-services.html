Limited Financial Assistance / Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS)

Face-to-Face & Field Validation

Limited cash assistance extended to clients in crisis due to death, illness, calamity, and other disasters that befell the family. Includes assistance for medical needs, hospitalization, transportation, education, burial, and crisis livelihood.

Tap to view requirements, fees, & 4-step process

Checklist of Requirements

  • Proof of Identity: 1 valid government-issued photo ID with date of birth (National ID, UMID, Driver's License, Voter's, 4Ps, or Barangay ID; original for verification).
  • Barangay Certification: 1 original Barangay Certificate of Indigency or Residency.
  • CSWDO Form: Certificate of Eligibility (CE) form provided by CSWDO upon intake.
  • Medical Aid Medical Documents: Medical Certificate, abstract, doctor's prescription, laboratory order, or hospital billing statement.
  • Educational Aid Enrollment Record: 1 copy of Certificate of Enrolment from the School Registrar.
  • Burial Aid Death Record: 1 registered Death Certificate from Local Civil Registrar (LCR) / PSA.
  • If Representative Authorization: Signed authorization letter and valid ID of the authorized claimant listed in the CE.

Service Summary

Turnaround Time
2 working days, 4 hours & 35 mins
Standard Fees
None (₱0.00)
Mode of Transaction
Walk-in (CSWDO Reception) • Field Assessment • EDAS Releasing
Program Overview: AICS provides direct emergency financial support to families in crisis. If the client is outside the city or unable to appear due to medical appointments, an authorization letter together with valid identification must be provided to the authorized family representative indicated in the Certificate of Eligibility (CE).

Issuance of Certificate of Indigency / Assessment Report / Social Case Study Report / Referral

Face-to-Face & Home Visit

Issuance of official documents by registered social workers to clients exploring resources of other agencies/institutions for education, burial, medical, counseling, psychiatric, legal aid, birth/marriage certificate correction, shelter, and livelihood.

Tap to view requirements, partner institutions, & 3-step process

Checklist of Requirements

  • Proof of Identity: 1 valid government-issued photo ID with date of birth (National ID, UMID, Driver's License, Voter's, 4Ps, or Barangay ID; original for verification).
  • Barangay Certification: 1 original Barangay Certificate of Indigency or Residency.
  • Burial Aid Burial Documents: 1 copy of Death Certificate AND City Mayor's recommendation note.
  • Medical Aid Medical Record: Medical Certificate, clinical abstract, or hospital bill.
  • Educational Aid Enrollment Proof: 1 original Certificate of Enrolment from School Registrar.
  • Legal Aid Legal Issue Evidence: Proof or documentation of dispute/case (for IBP / PAO referral).
  • Clerical Correction Civil Registry: Marriage Contract or Birth Certificate (for PSA / LCR correction).

Philhealth Universal Health Care – Certification of Financial Capability/Incapability

Face-to-Face Assessment

Enrolment of new members and renewal of old members through certification issued by a CSWDO Social Worker after thorough assessment in accordance with Department of Health (DOH) classification of indigence under the Universal Health Care Act.

Tap to view requirements, turnaround time, & 3-step process

Checklist of Requirements

  • Proof of Identity: 1 valid government-issued photo ID with date of birth (National ID, UMID, Driver's License, Voter's, 4Ps, or Barangay ID; original for verification).
  • Barangay Certification: 1 original Barangay Certificate of Indigency.
  • Civil Registry Documents: Marriage Contract (if married) and updated Birth Certificate (PSA / LCR; original to be returned).
  • If Confined Confinement Proof: Proof of confinement or medical care availment from the hospital/facility (if applicable).
  • If Representative Authorization: Signed authorization letter and valid ID of the representative.

None (₱0.00)

Universal Health Care Coverage: For over 13 years, the City Government has prioritized the enrolment of indigent families in PhilHealth. With the enactment of the Universal Health Care Law in 2019, LGU coverage has expanded to all qualified underprivileged residents in Legazpi City, including college students and non-working dependents.

1 Service in this category

Recognition and benefits for parents raising children on their own.

Issuance of Solo Parents ID

Face-to-Face & Home Visitation

Registration, assessment, RA 11861 welfare orientation, and issuance of Solo Parent Identification Cards granting discounts, subsidies, and educational benefits to qualified solo parents in Legazpi City.

Tap to view 13 category requirements, benefits, & 5-step process

Checklist of Requirements

  • Proof of Identity: 1 valid government-issued photo ID with date of birth (National ID, UMID, Driver's License, Voter's, 4Ps, or Barangay ID; original for verification).
  • Birth Certificate: 1 original or certified copy of Child/Children's Birth Certificate (PSA / LCR).
  • Barangay Certificate: 1 original Barangay Certificate of Residency in Legazpi City.
  • ID Photographs: 2 pieces 1x1 recent identical ID pictures.
  • Sworn Affidavit: Notarized sworn affidavit declaring no cohabitation and sole parental custody/care (PAO / Private Attorney).
  • Income Proof: Income Tax Return (ITR) for employed parents, or BIR Certificate of Tax Exemption for non-working parents.
Additional Category-Specific Requirements
Cat 1: Rape
  • Complaint affidavit (PNP / NBI)
  • Medical record on the incident of rape (PNP / NBI)
Cat 2: Death of Spouse
  • Marriage Certificate (PSA / LCR)
  • Death Certificate of spouse (PSA / LCR)
Cat 3: Detention of Spouse
  • Certificate of detention or certificate of sentence for ≥3 months (BJMP)
  • Affidavit of Cohabitation (PAO / Private Attorney)
Cat 4: Incapacity of Spouse
  • Medical abstract/record of incapacity issued within 3 months (Doctor)
  • Affidavit of Cohabitation (PAO / Private Attorney)
Cat 5: Legal Separation
  • Marriage Certificate (PSA / LCR)
  • Judicial decree of legal separation, or Affidavit of 2 disinterested persons (Court/PAO)
Cat 6: Annulment / Divorce
  • Marriage Certificate (PSA / LCR)
  • Judicial decree of nullity/annulment or foreign divorce granting sole care (Court)
Cat 7: Abandonment
  • Marriage Certificate or affidavit of applicant (PSA / LCR)
  • Affidavit of 2 disinterested persons attesting abandonment (PAO/Attorney)
  • Police or barangay record of abandonment (PNP / Barangay)
Cat 8 & 9: OFW Spouse
  • Marriage Certificate (PSA / LCR)
  • Overseas Employment Certificate - OEC (OWWA)
  • Passport stamps showing continuous 12 months overseas work
  • Employment contract (Employer / OWWA)
Cat 10: Unmarried Parent
  • Certificate of No Marriage - CENOMAR (PSA / LCR)
  • Affidavit of a barangay official attesting residency and parental care
Cat 11: Foster / Adoption
  • Proof of legal guardianship, foster care, or adoption decree (DSWD / Court)
  • Affidavit of a barangay official attesting residency and parental care
Cat 12: Loss of Parents
  • Death certificate of parents/guardian, or police/barangay records of disappearance ≥6 months (PSA/LCR/Barangay)
  • Affidavit of a barangay official attesting residency and status
Cat 13: Pregnant Mother
  • Medical record of her pregnancy (OB / Doctor)
  • Affidavit of a barangay official attesting residency and status

Solo Parent Unit (Walk-in & RA 11861 Orientation)
Republic Act No. 11861 (Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act): All applicants undergo mandatory orientation on RA 11861 provisions. Registered solo parents are entitled to 10% discount and VAT exemption on baby essentials, 7 days paid parental leave for employed parents, monthly financial subsidy for minimum wage earners, and scholarship prioritization.

Feedback & Complaints Mechanism

To send feedback: Answer the Customer Feedback Form and drop it at the Suggestion Box near the CSWDO Information Desk. Opened daily at 4:30 PM by the Info Desk Officer; answers forwarded within three (3) days.

To file a complaint: Text/call 0977-6300317, email cswdo.leg@legazpi.gov.ph, or fill out the Customer Feedback Form. Handled promptly by the Administrative Officer IV / Complaints Officer with formal resolution report.
