Financial help in a crisis 1 service

1 Limited Financial Assistance / Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS) 1 day, 4 hrs. & 20 minutes at the counter 2 days, 4 hrs. & 25 minutes in total 7 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Limited cash extended to clients in crisis due to death, illness, calamity or other disasters that befell the family. It also covers assistance for transportation, education and livelihood for those who cannot qualify under the Self-Employment Assistance Program.

How it works

  1. 1Submit your request.

    The office then

    1. Search for the client's General Intake Sheet (GIS) in the database and files, and route the client to the section concerned.

      5 minutesReception Desk

  2. 2Submit for interview and present the required documents.

    The office then

    1. Conduct a detailed interview and assessment of the case. If the client does not qualify, inform them immediately after the assessment.

      15 minutesSection Staff

    2. If the client qualifies, gather collateral information through home visits (if needed), phone calls and texts, then refer the case with complete documents to the Emergency / Disaster Assistance Section (EDAS).

      1 daySection Staff

    3. Prepare the Certificate of Eligibility (CE) for the client to sign, and submit it with complete documents to the Admin Division as the basis for a cash advance.

      5 minutesSection Staff

  3. 3Wait to be told the schedule of release.

    The office then

    1. Submit a batch of Certificates of Eligibility to the Head of Office and the City Mayor's Office for signature, then prepare and process the cash advance and inform EDAS once funds are available.

      1 dayAdmin Division

  4. 4Claim the assistance.

    The office then

    1. EDAS informs all clients to claim their assistance and coordinates with the sections, then releases the financial assistance to the client.

      4 hoursEmergency / Disaster Assistance Section (EDAS)

Total time 2 days, 4 hrs. & 25 minutes Total fee Free

Good to know

  • If you are outside the City, tell the CSWDO when you can return to Legazpi to claim the assistance. If you have an appointment elsewhere, leave an authorisation letter and your ID with a family member who will claim the cash.
  • The charter marks several steps as paused-clock, meaning the processing time is suspended while the office waits on something outside its control.

Certificates and referrals 2 services

2 Certificate of Indigency, assessment report, social case study or referral 25 minutes at the counter 1 day & 35 minutes in total 7 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Documents issued by registered social workers and authorised social welfare staff, which clients use to seek help from other agencies for education, burial, medical, counselling, psychiatric, legal, shelter and livelihood needs, and for correcting errors in birth certificates and marriage contracts. Receiving agencies include Simon of Cyrene, the Provincial Social Welfare and Sweepstake Office, the IBP, the Public Attorney's Office and other hospitals, agencies, foundations and NGOs. The extent and amount of assistance depends on the receiving party, which may also decline the request.

3 PhilHealth Universal Health Care certification of financial capability 1 hr. & 15 minutes at the counter Done in one visit 4 documents Free

Draft. Not yet checked against the published charter.

Enrolment of new members and renewal of existing members through a certification issued by a CSWDO social worker after a thorough assessment in accordance with the DOH classification of indigence. With the Universal Health Care Act of 2019, the City's coverage has expanded to most qualified persons in the City, including college students.

Feedback and complaints

Answer the Customer Feedback Form and drop it in the Suggestion Box at the CSWDO.

Every 4:30pm the Info Desk Officer opens the drop box, compiles all forms and submits them to the Administrative Officer IV. Feedback requiring an answer is forwarded to the relevant division or section, which must answer within three (3) working days of receipt. The office's answer is then relayed to the client.
