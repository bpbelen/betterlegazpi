**CLASSIFICATION:** SIMPLE
**TYPE OF TRANSACTION:** GOVERNMENT TO CLIENT
**WHO MAY AVAIL:** APPLICANT FOR RENEWAL OF BUSINESS PERMIT

---

---

### 1. SUBMIT

**1. SUBMIT THE REQUIRED DOCUMENTS TO ANY OF THE DESIGNATED LANES FROM 1, 3 TO 7**

**REQUIREMENTS:**

1. 1 original and 1 photocopy of Proof of Income (latest income statement from the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) or Audited Financial Statement)
2. 1 original copy of Market Clearance for Market Stall Holders. The market clearance shall be returned to the applicant upon release of the business permit.
3. 1 original and 1 photocopy of Cert. of Authority or Valid Provisional Cert. of Authority for Head Office of Pawnshop and Money Service Business (JMC No. 1 Series of 2019).

## Online Application for New Business Permit | legazpi.gov.ph

**CLASSIFICATION:** SIMPLE
**TYPE OF TRANSACTION:** GOVERNMENT TO CLIENT
**WHO MAY AVAIL:** ONLINE APPLICANT FOR NEW BUSINESS PERMIT

---

### 1. SUBMIT / PAY / CLAIM

**REQUIREMENTS:**
**Note:** The requirements for face-to-face application for new business permit are the same as those for online new.

**1.1.** Client logs in to **legazpi.gov.ph > Online Services > Services**

**1.1.1.** Click the "new business application."
**1.1.2.** Specify the required information.
**1.1.3.** Fill out the necessary details in the Unified Application Form (UAF).
**1.1.4.** Attach the required documents in pdf or image file.
**1.1.5** Review and verify the information before submitting the application.
**1.1.6** Upon approval of the application, a notification regarding the tax order of payment (Assessment Record) will be sent to the client via email or text message.

**1.2.** If the client opts to pay online, he or she should type in the Application Number icon in the required space, which can be found in the Tax Order of Payment (Assessment Record) sent to him/her.

**1.2.1.** Client selects payment options by filling-in the needed information then proceed to click the "Pay Now" icon to select the type of payment he or she chooses.

**Note:** If the client opts to pay at the City Treasurer's Office, he/she brings the Tax Order of Payment and pay the taxes and fees.

**1.3.** Client sends the e-copy of the Feedback Form to legpermit2@gmail.com.

**1.4.** Submit the duly accomplished feedback form to Lane 9 Releasing Officer. *(if the client prefers to claim the business permit at the BPLO)*

**Note:** If a representative will claim the Business Permit, Action Officer advises the client to let his/her representative bring an Authorization Letter.

**AGENCY ACTION:**

**1.1.** The Action Officer, upon receiving the complete requirements, processes the application and sends the Tax Order of Payment (TOP)/Assessment Record to the client thru email or text message.

**Note:** If the application is deemed ineligible, an applicant shall be given a notice of deficiency for compliance.

**1.1.1** Action Officer sends an e-copy of a Feedback Form to the client to be accomplished and submitted upon claiming the permit, and advises the client to pay thru Land Bank of the Philippines (LBP) or DBP; GCash or Paymaya by following the instructions provided on the city's website or pay at the City Treasurer's Office Payment area.

**PERSON RESPONSIBLE:**

| **MARK L. RANOLA** | **ROLANDO ABION, JR.** |
|---|---|
| Licensing Inspector I | Admin. Officer I |

**1.2.** If the client paid online, Land Bank, DBP Staffs, GCash and Paymaya processes the payment of the client.

**Note:** If the client paid at the City Treasurer's Office, the Revenue Collection Officer processes the payment & issue the official receipt.

**PERSON RESPONSIBLE:**
Land Bank or DBP Staffs, GCash or Paymaya
Revenue Collection Officers - CTO

**1.3** Upon approval of the City Mayor's Office, the Action Officer sends an electronic copy of the Business Permit to the client thru email after receiving the e-copy of the duly accomplished Feedback Form, or advises him/her to proceed to BPLO Lane 9 to claim the permit.

**PERSON RESPONSIBLE:**

| **MARK L. RAÑOLA** | **ROLANDO A. ABION, JR.** |

## FEEDBACK AND COMPLAINTS MECHANISM

### HOW TO SEND FEEDBACK?

1. Clients are given the Feedback Form based on the number of services they availed.
2. The assigned officer collect these forms at the end of the transaction day and submitted to the Admin. Officer for recording.
3. These are then collated per quarter and averaged as to the rating each client gave per service availed.
4. The collated rating per quarter are then presented under the Client Satisfaction Measurement (CSM) Report to get the office' rating.
5. This report is then submitted to the City Human Resource Management Office (CHRMO) for the collation of the agency's rating to be presented under the Harmonized CSM Report which will be submitted to the Anti-Red Tape Authority.

- Accomplish the Feedback Form

### HOW TO FILE A COMPLAINT?

1. In the Client Satisfaction Measurement (CSM) Survey Form, the client (online/walk-in) can air their complaints/suggestions through online or face to face. These complaint/s are assessed as to what action/s shall be taken.
2. The Head of Office calls the attention of the concerned officer to verify the veracity of the complaint.
3. When the complaint is checked as to its veracity, the Head of Office will then act on the complaint. This can fall under the light offense or grave offense depending on the commission of the act. The City Human Resource Management Office, if called to assist, can apply their corresponding sanction/s.
4. If the client asks for an appropriate action in the form of a letter, he/she shall be given appropriate feedback as to the action/s applied to rectify the act which need to be addressed.
