Employment & Human Resources

Employment & Public Personnel Services

Career opportunities, plantilla job applications, employee certifications, student internships, and HR administration in Legazpi City.

Getting in

Applying for permanent civil service plantilla positions, student internships, or contract appointments.

Apply for a position in the city government

Merit-based hiring for plantilla vacant positions adhering to CSC Qualification Standards and Equal Opportunity principles.

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 29 days, 3 hrs

Complete a work immersion/OJT placement

Institutional MOA facilitation, orientation, department deployment, and Certificate of Completion for student interns.

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 16 days, 2 hrs

Receive a COS/JO appointment

Preparation, legal review, notarization, and endorsement of appointments for contract of service and job order personnel.

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 5 days, 3 hrs

Being an employee

Official employee certifications, certified government service histories, and learning & development travel orders.

Request a Certificate of Employment

Issuance of official Certificate of Employment (COE) with tenure and compensation details for active and former employees.

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 17 minutes

Request a Service Record

Official certified transcript of government employment history for promotions, loan applications, GSIS, and step increments.

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 27 minutes

Get sent on official travel

Preparation and certification of official travel orders for personnel participating in seminars, conventions, and trainings.

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 2 days, 1 hr

Leaving/winding down

Processing statutory employee leave applications, credit monetization, and terminal leave/retirement clearance.

Apply for leaves

Computation, auditing, and certification of statutory leaves (Vacation, Sick, Maternity/Paternity, Monetization, Forced).

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 5 days, 2 hrs

Process optional or mandatory retirement

Facilitates documentation, complete terminal leave credit computation, and clearance routing for retiring personnel.

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 10 days, 10 mins
