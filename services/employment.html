Employment & Livelihood

Employment & Livelihood

Finding work, and working for the city

Republic Act No. 11261

Applying for your first job? Most of the paperwork is free

The First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act waives the government fee on your NBI and police clearances, birth certificate, TIN, UMID and more. One certification from your barangay unlocks it.

How to claim it PESO Legazpi
You pay at the counter
₱0
Document fees waived

Valid from your barangay certification
