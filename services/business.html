New Applicants Lane & Electronic Routing

Electronic Inter-Agency Routing

Requirements submitted at the BPLO receiving desk are forwarded electronically by the BPLO to regulatory offices for endorsement and approval. Once approved, the application proceeds to Step 2.

Step 1

Filing, Verification, Evaluation & Assessment

BPLO Receiving Officer & Regulatory Offices

Applicant submits 1 copy of the duly accomplished and signed Unified Application Form (UAF) along with mandatory requirements to the receiving officer.

Standard Unified Requirements (UAF):

  • Duly Filled-out Application Form (1 Original): Accomplished and signed Unified Application Form (UAF) for accountability.
  • Sketch of Business Location (2 Copies): Detailed sketch/map showing exact business premises location.
  • Certificate of Occupancy (1 Original + 1 Photocopy): Certificate of Occupancy of space/building where business is located. (Original returned upon verification)
  • DTI / SEC / CDA Registration (1 Photocopy): Certificate of Business Name Registration (DTI for sole proprietorship, SEC for corporation/partnership, CDA for cooperative).

Other Possible Requirements (As Applicable):

  • For Market Stall Holders: 1 Original copy of Market Clearance and 1 Photocopy of Contract of Lease.
  • If Business Location is Rented/Not Owned: 1 Photocopy of Valid Lease Contract / Authority / Certification from Property Owner, or Notice of Award. (Building/space owner must have a valid business permit as lessor or real estate rental)
  • Evaluation Requirements (Determined upon application review): • 1 Original copy of Affidavit of No Improvement / Electrical Plan (for change of space use)
    • 1 Original copy of Affidavit of Non-Objection / Manifestation

Step 2

Payment of Taxes & Regulatory Charges

City Treasurer's Office & Payment Partners

Applicant pays computed taxes and fees based on the issued Order of Payment (provided in hard copy, text, or email) at the City Treasurer's Office or online payment partners.

Available Payment Channels:

City Treasurer's Office
Land Bank of the Philippines
Development Bank of the Philippines
Maya (PayMaya)
GCash
Step 3

Claim the Business Permit & Other Clearances

BPLO Releasing (Lane 9)

Applicant claims the official business permit, business plate, sticker, and regulatory clearances at BPLO Lane 9 upon presenting their completed Client Feedback Form.

Documents Released:

Business Permit Business Plate Official Sticker Regulatory Clearances
Pickup Location: BPLO Lane 9 (Releasing) • Must present completed Client Feedback Form.

Priority Lane

Gender and Development (GAD) Priority

For Senior Citizens, Pregnant Women, PWDs, and Parents with young children (10 years old & below).

Express Lane

1 – 2 Applications

Fast-track processing for applicants filing 1 to 2 business permit applications.

Bulk Lane

3+ Applications

Dedicated processing lane for applicants filing 3 or more business permit applications.

Step 1

Filing, Verification & Assessment

BPLO Receiving Officers (Lanes 1, 3–7)

Applicant submits all mandatory requirements for verification and assessment to the BPLO Receiving Officer in any of the assigned processing lanes (Priority, Express, or Bulk).

Checklist of Requirements (Renewal):

  • Proof of Income (Original + 1 Photocopy): Latest Income Tax Return (ITR) from BIR or Audited Financial Statement. (Original copy returned immediately upon verification)
  • Market Clearance (1 Original): For Market Stall Holders only. (Returned upon release of Mayor's Permit)
  • Certificate of Authority (1 Photocopy): Valid Certificate or Provisional Certificate of Authority for Head Office of Pawnshops and Money Service Business (Joint Memorandum Circular / JMC No. 1 S. 2019).
  • BSP Letter of Code Issuance (1 Photocopy): For Pawnshops and Money Service Business branches other than the Head Office.
