Social Services

Social Services

Support programs for vulnerable sectors, families in crisis, and communities in Legazpi City

Facing a Crisis or Emergency

Financial help when death, illness, calamity, or accidents hit your family.

Limited Financial Assistance (AICS)

Emergency financial aid for medical, burial, educational, transportation, and crisis situations.

Fee: Free Time: 2 days

Certificate of Indigency / Social Case Study Report / Referral

Social case study reports, certificates of indigency, and partner agency referrals.

Fee: Free Time: 2 days

Getting Access to Healthcare

Qualifying for subsidized or free health coverage.

PhilHealth UHC – Certification of Financial Capability/Incapability

Financial capability & indigency assessment for PhilHealth Universal Health Care.

Fee: Free Time: 1 hour

Raising a Child on Your Own

Recognition, benefits, and support for solo parents.

Issuance of Solo Parent ID

Solo Parent ID issuance and benefits under RA 11861 across 13 recognized categories.

Fee: Free Time: 2 days

Responsible Offices

CSWDO Services

Access consolidated social welfare programs, financial aid, PhilHealth UHC, and Solo Parent IDs.
