Reading your ALECO bill

10-digit account number

Upper left of your monthly statement. This is the number every e-wallet and bank portal asks for.

Meter serial number

Under the consumer details box, and etched on the glass of your physical meter. Use it to check the bill is for your unit.

Due date

Bills carry a 9-day grace period from the delivery date. Settle on or before the due date to avoid disconnection fees.

Apply for a new ALECO connection

The standard route for residential and commercial connections in Legazpi City.

  1. Attend the orientation

    Sit the Pre-Membership Education Seminar at the ALECO compound or an accredited online session.

    Saturdays, 9:00 AM, ALECO main office

  2. Secure permits and inspection

    Get the Certificate of Final Electrical Inspection and the wiring permit from the City Engineering Office.

    City Engineering Office, City Hall

  3. Submit and pay the deposit

    Hand in the membership application with your documents, then pay the bill deposit and connection fees.

    ALECO Member Services desk

  4. Meter installation

    The ALECO crew inspects the service drop line, installs your kilowatt-hour meter, and energizes the property.

    3 to 5 working days

Reading your LCWD bill

8-digit account number

Upper left of your water bill. This is the number every e-wallet and bank portal asks for.

Current and previous reading

Cubic meters consumed this billing cycle. Subtract the previous reading from the current one to check the volume yourself.

Due date

Water bills are due within 15 days of the reading. Overdue accounts past the disconnection date incur a penalty and a reconnection fee.

Apply for a new LCWD connection

For residential, commercial, and institutional water tapping in Legazpi City.

  1. Orientation and application form

    Attend the applicant briefing at the LCWD Commercial Division in Bitano and collect the new service application form.

    Commercial Division desk

  2. Site inspection and pressure test

    An LCWD inspector surveys your site for the nearest distribution mainline, the tapping distance, and water pressure.

    Within 24 to 48 hours

  3. Plumbing and fees

    Finish the private plumbing up to the meter stand, then settle the connection fee, meter deposit, and inspection charges.

    LCWD cashier, Bitano

  4. Mainline tapping and meter

    The installation team fits the calibrated meter, connects your service pipe to the mainline, and starts the flow.

    2 to 4 working days
