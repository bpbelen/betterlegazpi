How to Apply for a New ALECO Electric Connection

Standard procedure and requirements for residential and commercial electric service connections in Legazpi City.

1

Pre-Membership Orientation

Attend the Pre-Membership Orientation Seminar (PMOS) conducted at the ALECO compound or accredited online session.

Schedule: Tuesdays & Thursdays
2

Secure Permits & Inspection

Secure Certificate of Final Electrical Inspection (CFEI) and Wiring Permit from the Legazpi City Engineering Office (CEO).

City Engineering Office (City Hall)
3

Submit Application & Deposit

Submit completed Membership Application, valid IDs, proof of ownership or tenancy, and pay bill deposit and connection fees.

ALECO Member Services Desk
4

Meter Installation & Energization

ALECO technical crew inspects service drop line, installs electric kilowatt-hour meter, and energizes your property.

Estimated: 3 - 5 Working Days
ALECO New Connection Web Portal (web.alecoinc.com.ph) ALECO Online Application System (webapp/apply.php) Official ALECO Advisory Post

Specific Category Guidelines (Per ALECO Citizen's Charter):
  • Renters / Lessees: Submit Notarized Lease Contract + Certification/Consent from the lot owner + Photocopy of Lot Owner's ID.
  • Informal Settlers / Public Land: Submit Certificate of Residency / Right to Occupy from LGU Legazpi City or BAPA Clearance.
  • Private Lots with Permission: Submit Notarized Certification / Waiver from the registered landowner.
  • Commercial / Juridical Entities: Submit SEC/DTI Registration, Mayor's Business Permit, and Board Resolution/Secretary's Certificate designating the authorized signatory.

How to Read Your ALECO Electric Bill & Account Number

10-Digit Account Number

Located on the upper left portion of your Monthly Electricity Statement. Always enter this 10-digit number when paying through GCash, Maya, or Bank portals.

Meter Serial Number

Printed under the consumer details box and etched on your physical electric meter glass to verify that billing corresponds to your unit.

Due Date & Disconnection Notice

Bills have a 9-day payment grace period from delivery date. Settle on or before the indicated Due Date to prevent automated disconnection fees.

How to Apply for a New LCWD Water Service Connection

Step-by-step guide for residential, commercial, and institutional water tapping in Legazpi City.

1

Orientation & Application Form

Attend the applicant briefing at the LCWD Commercial Division (Bitano) and secure the New Water Service Application Form.

Commercial Division Desk
2

Site Inspection & Pressure Test

An LCWD technical inspector surveys your site to determine the nearest distribution mainline, tapping distance, and water pressure.

Inspection: Within 24-48 Hours
3

Plumbing & Fee Settlement

Complete standard private house plumbing up to the meter stand and settle the connection fee, meter deposit, and inspection charges.

LCWD Cashier (Bitano)
4

Mainline Tapping & Meter Install

The LCWD installation team installs the calibrated water meter, connects service pipes to the mainline, and commences water flow.

Installation: 2 - 4 Working Days

How to Read Your LCWD Water Bill

Account Number (8 Digits)

Displayed at the upper left section of your water bill. Use this exact 8-digit number for online and e-wallet payments.

Current & Previous Reading

Indicates cubic meters (cu.m) consumed during the billing cycle. Subtract previous reading from current reading to verify your volume.

Due Date & Disconnection Notice

Water bills must be paid within 15 days of reading. Overdue accounts after the disconnection date incur a penalty and reconnection fee.
