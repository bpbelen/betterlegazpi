Taxation

Tax & Payments

Property taxes, business taxes, transfer taxes, certifications, assessments, and payment services in Legazpi City.

Online Services via Filipizen

Online Tax Transactions

Pay your taxes and fees online through the official Filipizen Legazpi portal

Realty Tax Online Billing and Payment

Inquire, view statements of account, and pay real property taxes (amilyar) online

City Treasurer's Office

Online Payment Order

Inquire and settle Payment Orders for regulatory fees, permits, and city assessments

City Treasurer's Office

Property & Real Estate Services

Pay real property taxes, request tax declarations, transfer property ownership, and obtain clearances.

Pay Real Property Tax (Amilyar)

Annual basic real property tax and Special Education Fund (SEF) payment for land, buildings, and improvements.

Fee: Assessed Value Time: 5-9 mins

Transfer of Property Ownership

Issuance of new Tax Declaration upon transfer of real property via sale, donation, or estate partition.

Fee: ₱100.00 / TD Time: 1-3 days

True Copy of Tax Declaration

Certified true copy of current or historical Tax Declarations for bank loans, BIR, and legal transactions.

Fee: ₱100.00 Time: 15-20 mins

Real Property Tax Clearance

Official clearance certifying that real property taxes are fully paid, required for deed transfers and BIR.

Fee: ₱100.00 Time: 10 mins

Assessment of Building & Structures

Declaration, ocular inspection, and initial appraisal of newly erected residential, commercial, or machinery assets.

Fee: ₱100.00 / TD Time: 3-5 days

Tax Mapping & Lot Location

Cadastral boundary verification, Property Identification Number (PIN) validation, and certified map printouts.

Fee: Free / ₱100.00 Time: 20 mins

Business & Commercial Taxes

Quarterly business tax billing, permit payments, and closure certifications.

Business Tax Quarterly Billing

Computation and issuance of quarterly billing statements for business taxes, regulatory fees, and garbage charges.

Fee: Gross Sales Based Time: 7 mins

Payment of Business Tax / Permit

Official receipt and tax payment clearance for annual renewal or newly registered commercial establishments.

Fee: Per Assessment Time: 5-10 mins

Certification of Business Retirement

Official verification and certification confirming legitimate closure or retirement of business operations.

Fee: ₱100.00 Time: 15-20 mins

Personal Taxes, PTR & Certifications

Community tax certificates, professional tax receipts, and official property certifications.

Community Tax (Cedula / CTC)

Individual and corporate community tax certificate issuance for employment, licensing, and legal notarization.

Fee: Basic + Computed Time: 7 mins

Professional (PTR) & Occupation Tax

Annual tax payment receipt for PRC-licensed professionals, medical practitioners, and regulated occupations.

Fee: ₱300 / ₱150 Time: 7 mins

Assessor Certifications

Official certificates of Landholding, No Improvement, or Non-Ownership for scholarship, legal, or PAO requirements.

Fee: ₱100.00 Time: 15 mins

Responsible Offices

CTO Services

Tax collection, land tax, business tax, cedula, clearances, PTR, and financial disbursements

CAssO Services

Real property assessment, tax declarations, land classification, mapping, and property records
