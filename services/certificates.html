Certificates

Certificates & Vital Records

Official documents for birth, death, marriage, legal instruments, and other vital records in Legazpi City.

Starting a family

Register a new birth, legitimize children born prior to marriage, or fix errors on a child's birth record.

Register a New Baby

On-time or delayed birth registration, including the Birth Registration Assistance Project (BRAP) for indigent and IP families.

Fee: Free (On-time/BRAP) / ₱150 Time: 25 mins

Legitimize a Child Born Before Marriage

Legitimation by subsequent marriage of parents and affidavits to establish paternal rights and surname (RA 9255).

Fee: ₱300.00-₱500.00 Time: 40 mins

Fix Wrong Entry on Child's Birth Record

Administrative petition to correct misspelled names, gender marks, or birth dates under RA 9048 / RA 10172 without court litigation.

Fee: ₱1,000.00-₱3,000.00 Time: 10-15 days

Getting married

Marriage license application, legal requirements, and registration of solemnized marriage ceremonies.

Apply for a Marriage License

Filing of legal marriage license application for couples intending to marry, including the mandatory 10-day publication notice.

Fee: ₱300.00-₱350.00 Time: 10 days posting + 30 mins

Register Marriage Ceremony

Registration of marriage certificate after solemnization by priest, minister, or judge (on-time or delayed/reconstruction).

Fee: Free (On-time) / ₱150 (Delayed) Time: 25 mins

A relationship or life status changes

Record court judgments, legal separations, judicial adoptions, and subsequent registry corrections or annotations.

Register a Court Decree

Official registration and annotation of court judgments, annulment, declaration of nullity, legal separation, and adoption.

Fee: ₱500.00-₱1,000.00 Time: 1 hour (2 days)

Correct or Supplement Civil Records

Registration of supplemental reports, affidavits, paternity acknowledgments, and legal instruments after the fact.

Fee: ₱200.00-₱300.00 Time: 30 mins

A death in the family

Death certificate registration, burial clearances, and certified copies required for bereavement claims.

Register a Death

Registration of death certificate within 48 hours (on-time) or delayed reporting, including burial and transfer permits.

Fee: Free (Burial: ₱50) / ₱150 (Delayed) Time: 25 mins - 1 hr

Certified Copies for Estate & Benefits

Fast-track issuance of certified true copies and death certifications for estate settlement, insurance, and GSIS/SSS claims.

Fee: ₱100.00 / copy Time: 20 mins

Proving who you are, later in life

Request certified copies, verify records online, and advance endorse documents to the PSA central database.

Request Certified True Copies (CTC)

Official certified copies or local transcripts (Form 1A, 2A, 3A) for passport, employment, retirement, and legal identification.

Fee: ₱100.00 / copy Time: 20 mins

Verify & Inquire Online (Email-Assisted)

Check archive availability, verify records, and confirm document requirements via email before traveling to the office.

Fee: Free (₱0.00) Time: 1-2 working days

PSA Endorsement of Records

Advance electronic endorsement and dispatch of local civil registry records to PSA so your record shows up nationally.

Fee: ₱150.00 Time: 30 mins

Responsible Offices
