    Disclaimer & data notes

    This directory consolidates several DOH registries that do not always agree with one another, and whose publication dates differ by facility type. Where two sources conflict, the DOH National Health Facility Registry takes precedence and the superseded value is recorded below rather than discarded. Licence and accreditation dates are those the sources published; confirm with a facility before relying on them.

    This directory records what each facility is licensed to do, which is not the same as a list of everything it offers. Phone numbers and email addresses are reproduced exactly as the sources print them, including a few that are missing a digit; they have deliberately not been tidied into valid-looking numbers. Always ring ahead before travelling to a facility, and dial 911 in an emergency.

      One entry departs from that rule deliberately: Bicol Regional Hospital and Medical Center is registered by DOH under Daraga, Albay, but is listed here because it operates on Rizal Street and serves Legazpi City.

      Disclaimer & data notes

      How this list was built. Every entry was read directly from PhilHealth's published GAMOT list and matched by its printed entry number, which is shown on each card so you can check it against the source. Nothing here is inferred: a provider appears only where PhilHealth prints it, and the two PhilHealth lists are never treated as interchangeable.

      Two accreditations, not one. A place on the YAKAP Konsulta list provides free consultations and laboratory tests. A place on the GAMOT list dispenses the free maintenance medicines. Many hold both, but not all — check the badges on a facility before travelling for a specific service.

      Pharmacies are not health facilities. Drugstores here are FDA-licensed drug outlets, not DOH-licensed health facilities, which is why they are listed separately and are not counted in the facility directory above.

      Bicol Regional Hospital and Medical Center appears on the GAMOT list (no. 1594) but not on the YAKAP Konsulta list, so it dispenses YAKAP medicines without providing YAKAP consultations. It is registered by DOH under Daraga, Albay, and is listed here because it serves Legazpi City. Accreditation dates are those PhilHealth published; confirm with a provider before relying on them, and bring a valid prescription.
