Health Services & Facilities Directory

Access consultations, PhilHealth YAKAP primary care, immunizations, maternal health, and explore Legazpi City’s complete network of licensed hospitals, clinics, and 70 Barangay Health Stations.

Core Public Health Programs

Accessible healthcare programs offered by the City Government of Legazpi and accredited PhilHealth partners.

Medical Consultation

Free general medical consultation, triage, and physical exams at the City Health Office (CHO), RHUs, and Homapon Super Health Center.

Fee: Free Schedule: Mon-Fri 8AM-5PM (Walk-in)

PhilHealth YAKAP & Konsulta

Accredited primary care package covering free consultations, targeted diagnostic tests, and maintenance medicines (GAMOT program).

Fee: 100% PhilHealth Covered Locations: 13 Accredited Clinics

Immunization & Vaccination

Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI) for infants, children, pregnant women, and senior citizen flu and pneumonia vaccines.

Fee: Free Access: 70 Barangay Health Stations

Maternal & Lying-In Delivery

24/7 Normal Spontaneous Delivery, prenatal, and postnatal care at Northern District (Buyuan) and Southern District (Banquerohan & Taysan) Birthing Clinics.

Fee: PhilHealth / Subsidized Availability: 24/7 Active

TB-DOTS & Respiratory Care

Free GeneXpert molecular testing, sputum diagnosis, and complete anti-TB medication treatment under the National TB Control Program.

Fee: Free (DOH Subsidized) Venue: CHO TB-DOTS Center

Diagnostic Lab & Blood Banking

Clinical lab tests (CBC, Urinalysis, Blood Chemistry) at CHO Lab and 24/7 emergency blood services via Philippine Red Cross Albay Chapter.

Fee: Free / Standard Rates Turnaround: Same day results
