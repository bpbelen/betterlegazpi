Environment Services

Environmental Services

Solid waste management, coastal protection, and environmental conservation programs in Legazpi City

Managing waste & business compliance

Secure permits for waste disposal, attend mandatory ESWM seminars, and schedule septic tank siphoning.

Get a Permit to Dump

Secure permits for direct garbage disposal at Banquerohan Sanitary Landfill or composting facilities.

Fee: Free Time: 1 hr 35 mins

Attend the ESWM Seminar

Complete online or face-to-face ESWM seminar to obtain the mandatory Environmental Certificate.

Fee: ₱50.00 Time: ~2 hrs 55 mins

Schedule Septic Tank Desludging

Apply for septic tank siphoning and desludging services under the City Septage Management Code.

Fee: Septage Fee Time: 2 hrs 12 mins

Cutting trees & registering equipment

Obtain LGU certifications and clearances required for DENR tree cutting and chainsaw registration.

Get a Certificate of No Objection to Cut Trees

Obtain LGU Certificate of No Objection prerequisite for DENR tree cutting permit applications.

Fee: Free Time: 6 days 40 mins

Get Chainsaw Registration Certification

Secure LGU registration support and serial number validation before registering chainsaws with DENR.

Fee: Free Time: 1 hr 10 mins

Community & environmental requests

Request facility entry passes, free tree seedlings for planting activities, and educational campaigns.

Visit the Sanitary Landfill Facility

Request visitor entry pass and project orientation for the Banquerohan Sanitary Landfill Facility.

Fee: Free Time: 1 hr 18 mins

Request Free Tree Seedlings

Request free tree seedlings from the OCENR Nursery for community planting and urban greening.

Fee: Free Time: 4 hrs 35 mins

Request an IEC Campaign

Book OCENR speakers for ecological solid waste and environmental awareness lectures.

Fee: Free Time: 20 mins
