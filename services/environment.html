Environment

Environment

Office of the City Environment and Natural Resources

Your official guide for environmental permits, waste management, and environmental sustainability

Frontline Services

Sanitary Landfill Facility (SLF)

Disposal clearance and tipping access for residual and institutional solid waste at the city's sanitary landfill in Banquerohan.

Fee: ₱675.00 – ₱875.00 Time: 30 mins

ESWM Seminar & Environmental Certificate

Mandatory Ecological Solid Waste Management seminar and compliance certificate for business permits and community groups.

Fee: Free Time: 2 hours

Tree Cutting & Pruning Clearance

Ocular inspection, hazard evaluation, and LGU clearance for tree cutting, trimming, or pruning on private and public properties.

Fee: ₱50.00 – ₱150.00 Time: 3–5 working days

Chainsaw Registration & Permitting

Unit verification, serial number stamping, and registration certificate issuance under the Chainsaw Act of 2002 (RA 9175).

Fee: ₱150.00 Time: 1–3 working days

Request for Free Tree Seedlings

Free native forest and fruit-bearing tree seedlings from the OCENR Nursery for civic groups, schools, and greening initiatives.

Fee: Free Time: Same day

Septic Tank Desludging & PACD

Vacuum truck septic tank desludging services under the City Septage Code, plus Public Assistance and Complaints Desk.

Fee: ₱1,500.00 (Standard) Time: 1–2 working days

Key Environmental Policies

01

No Segregation, No Collection

Strict enforcement of segregated waste collection daily from established Materials Recovery Facilities / Drop-Off-Centers (MRDOC) across all barangays.

02

Sanitary Landfill Facility (SLF)

Engineered final disposal facility operating under RA 9003 standards with strict Yellow (Safe) and Red (Critical) zoning protocols for educational visitors.

03

Septage Management Code

City Ordinance No. 0025-2015 mandates scheduled septic tank desludging for all residential and commercial buildings to protect municipal groundwater.

04

Business ESWM Certification

City Ordinance No. 0010-2008 requires all business establishments to undergo ESWM training (3-year validity) prior to business permit issuance/renewal.

05

Urban Greening & Tree Care

Production and free distribution of native tree seedlings from the OCENR Nursery, combined with strict LGU tree cutting clearances and replacement undertakings.

Responsible Office

Office of the City Environment and Natural Resources (OCENR)

Solid waste management, Sanitary Landfill Facility (SLF) operations, ESWM business seminars, tree cutting certifications, chainsaw registration, nursery seedling distribution, and septage desludging.

Empowering the people of Legazpi City with transparent access to the services, programs, and public funds of LGU Legazpi.
