Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about municipal services

General Questions

Is this the official website of the City Government of Legazpi?

No. BetterLegazpi.org is an independent volunteer project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the City Government of Legazpi. The official city website is legazpi.gov.ph.

What we do is reorganise information the city already publishes, mainly its Citizen's Charter, so it is easier to search and read. If anything here disagrees with an official city source, the official source is correct. For anything that affects a decision, confirm it with the office concerned.

How current and reliable is the information here?

Service details, fees and processing times come from the city's published Citizen's Charter. We are still checking each entry line by line against the published document, so services that have not been checked yet are labelled Unverified in search results and on the office pages. That label means we transcribed it but have not confirmed it, not that we think it is wrong.

Fees and requirements do change, and a charter can be updated before we notice. Treat what you read here as a guide to what to expect and who to ask, not as a final quotation.

What are the office hours of the City Hall?

The City Hall is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with a lunch break from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM. It is closed on weekends and on national and local holidays.

How can I contact a specific city office?

Visit our Government Directory page to find contact information for all city offices and department heads.

Can I request services online?

Some can. Business permit applications and renewals, business billing, and real property tax billing and payment can be started online through the city's Filipizen portal. Building permit and certificate of occupancy applications have published step-by-step guides, but you still complete them in person. Most other services are still in person only. Each service category page lists what is available online for that category, and says plainly whether a link is a working portal, a guide, or a payment channel.

Certificates & Documents

How long does it take to get a birth certificate?

For birth certificates registered in Legazpi City, it typically takes 15-30 minutes while you wait, provided the record is readily available.

Can someone else request my certificate for me?

Yes, but they must bring:

  • An authorization letter signed by you
  • Valid ID of both you and the representative
  • Photocopy of your valid ID
What is the difference between PSA and local civil registrar certificates?

Both are certified true copies. PSA certificates are the nationally-recognized version required for passport and visa applications. Local civil registrar certificates are accepted for most local transactions and are often processed faster.

Business & Permits

When should I renew my business permit?

Business permits must be renewed annually, preferably in January. The deadline for penalty-free renewal is typically January 20th of each year.

What do I need to start a new business in Legazpi City?

To start a new business, you'll need:

  • DTI Registration (for sole proprietorship) or SEC Registration (for corporation)
  • Barangay Clearance
  • Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
  • Location Sketch/Map
  • Contract of Lease (if renting)

Visit our Business Permit page for complete details.

Payments & Fees

What payment methods are accepted?

The City Treasurer's Office accepts cash payments in person. Real property tax and business billing can also be paid online through the city's Filipizen portal. Fees for individual services are listed on that service's page, taken from the city's published Citizen's Charter.

How can I pay my real property tax?

Visit the City Treasurer's Office at the City Hall with your Tax Declaration or latest Official Receipt. Payment is in cash. Property taxes are due quarterly, but you may pay annually to avail of discounts.

Social Services

How do I apply for a Senior Citizen ID?

Go to the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) with:

  • Birth Certificate or any valid ID showing your age (60 and above)
  • 1x1 ID photo
  • Barangay Residence Certificate

The ID is issued for free.

What benefits do senior citizens receive?

Senior citizens enjoy 20% discount and VAT exemption on purchases (with a minimum purchase amount per establishment), priority lanes, and access to special programs and medical assistance from the municipality.

About the Developer

Who developed BetterLegazpi?

Ramon Logan Jr. is the developer behind Abakada.org and BetterLegazpi.org, a community-driven initiative building a digital bridge between the residents of Legazpi City and the local government of Legazpi City. Based in the United Arab Emirates, he works in IT and practices full-stack development, helping build practical digital solutions in web development, design, cloud services, and cybersecurity.

He also started HelloPinas.com, a small cloud-based solutions initiative. Ramon contributes to BetterGov.ph, a volunteer-driven civic-tech effort focused on improving access to local government information and services in the Philippines.

He is also an individual participant of the OpenJS Foundation, a nonprofit supporting open-source JavaScript communities worldwide.

Ramon has made the Legazpi City Project open source under MIT | CC BY 4.0 to empower community-driven development, and contributions are warmly welcomed from everyone; whether you are a developer, data researcher, designer, content writer, translator, or a concerned citizen of Legazpi City, your participation helps shape the project for all.
